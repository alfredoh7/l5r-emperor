# Expanded navy playing mat · 0.3.8

The inner wooden frame and broad exposed wood margins are replaced with navy fabric. The new plate keeps the weave, gold embroidery, warm lanterns and dark perimeter; both lanterns sit directly on the fabric. The existing card sizes, positions, camera controls and hand remain unchanged.

The renderer uses a conservative fabric region inset 4% from the source image's edges. The environment grows with the measured board bounds and provides 90 logical units of additional fabric on all sides. Its aspect ratio is preserved. Thus a wider economy or taller army layout does not push cards onto the frame. This increases the playing fabric, rather than shrinking cards to fit an undersized mat.

The native capture checks actual projected card corners and pile top faces, maps them back to the table plane, and requires at least 88 logical units of navy clearance (90-unit target with a two-unit geometry tolerance). The fixed hand and detached inspection trays are excluded from the board-clearance calculation.
