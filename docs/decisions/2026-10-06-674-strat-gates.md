## Readings built in (for Mendon to confirm or correct)

1. **Allied Efforts** reuses Spitting Llama's action (`spitting-llama-force`, enemy Personality or Follower, -3F) unchanged.
2. **Iaijutsu Dojo**: "Your target unbowed Personality challenges a target enemy Personality" is built as a performing unbowed Personality at the battlefield (Reckless Duel / First Blood shape) rather than a second targeted card, so it is not "targeted" for reactions that look at targeting. The scan prints no "He may refuse", so the challenge is **not refusable**. The loser (not only a destroyed one) is dishonored then bowed; a tie has no loser. Iaijutsu is an action keyword of the ability.
3. **Boiling Point**'s Focus Effect gives +1F to **each** of the Focus Effect owner's Yojimbo that stands at the same location as that owner's Personality in the duel (no choice is asked, and it does not depend on winning). "At any location" for the bow target means at home or at any battlefield still to be fought (the existing "at any location" definition). "Another target Personality" (to straighten) is any Personality other than the bowed one, either player's. Bow is skipped for a Personality whose bow is negated (Stalwart); straighten is the ordinary straighten (respects straighten locks).
4. **Chaos of Battle**: "opposed" means the other side has a Personality at the battlefield; the count is the number of different Clan alignments among **all** Personalities (either side) at the current battlefield, read when the action resolves (Unaligned adds none, a Personality with several alignments adds each).
5. **Nubane's Defense**: the Rings counted are the Rings in play that the acting player controls, read at resolution; with none, the action still resolves for +0F.
6. **Defensive Formation**: "defending" is the non-active player's Personality at the current battlefield, so an attacking player has no legal target.
7. **Honor Replaces Rage**: "-2F while attacking this turn" is a per-turn marker (Personality, turn number) read by the PersonalityForce hook while he is attacking (at a battlefield on his controller's turn). Two plays stack. The marker simply stops counting when the turn number moves on.
8. **Exotic Farmlands** and **Ivinda Village** use the existing variable Gold cost (`GoldCostRule.Variable`). Farmlands: any amount 0..12 may be paid; one Follower is created per full 3 Gold; with fewer than 3 nothing is created and the action is still taken (the Gold is spent). The Follower is the "user-scan-colonists" 1F Ashigaru Follower Colonial Farm already creates. Ivinda: at least 1 Gold (0..cap would target nothing), at most 12, and the number of targets must **equal** the Gold paid; "target cards" is any card in play (either player's), Cavalry lasts until the end of the turn (R-001).
9. **Claiming the Ruins** is a search of the acting player's own Fate discard pile for a non-Unique Item (the existing discard-search stage), not a targeted action; if there is none the action is still taken.
10. **Lost Caravan**: "your Stronghold or Holding" is the acting player's own, in any bow state.
11. **Mukudori's Wisdom** is two abilities sharing one use (straighten, bow), as the other "or" cards. **Mukudori's Wisdom** and **The Tomb of Sun Tao** target a Ring **in play** (either player's), not one in a discard pile. A Ring returned to hand by the Tomb goes to its **owner's** hand, unbowed.
12. **Recovering What Was Lost**: "If he would be opposed" is an entry condition (the other side has Personalities at the battlefield; otherwise the action is not offered), the action is an Engage Reaction (the "after engaging" window, the shape Steal an Advantage uses) and "Absent" needs no presence. The +2F for a Tactician applies whether or not the movement was negated.
13. **The False Route**: modelled exactly on Surprise Attack's created battlefield (not at any province, joins the battles still to be fought), with both chosen Personalities (mine and an enemy one at the current battlefield) moved there by their own movement effects. "If your Personality is a Scout, straighten his unit" is read at resolution and does not depend on the movement succeeding.
14. **The Final Lesson**: the keyword is given permanently through the existing permanent keyword grant; the engine's own Expendable rule draws the card.
15. **Tactical Setback** is the Absent Battle flag: playable with no unit at the battlefield (its target must still be at the battlefield).
16. The Kharmic reminder text on Ivinda Village, Mukudori's Wisdom and The Tomb of Sun Tao is the universal Kharmic action, not an ability of the card.
