## The primitive (Mendon's ruling on #667)

Mendon: apply a block with an arbitrarily large number of turns, or, if there is no such thing, make the removal last while the card stays in play and reset when it leaves play and returns, "like other abilities". There was no such block (`DisabledAbilities` is per turn; Unsettling Doubts removes whole-card abilities for a Ring only), so this is the second alternative, generalised so it covers one chosen ability, all traits, or everything:

- A record per (card, key). The key is the `UsageKey` of one ability (a printed ability split across catalogue entries is removed whole), `trait` (the card's Effect) or `*` (all abilities and traits).
- It takes effect by rewriting the card's per-instance printed definition (`CardInstance.Printed`, which `Definition(card)` reads everywhere) without the removed abilities / with Effect `none`, and `AbilityIdsOf` also filters granted and static-granted abilities by the same keys.
- The sweep that runs after every command (`ReturnCardsOutOfPlayToTheirOwners`) drops the records of a card that is no longer in play and gives its baseline Effect and Abilities back, so a card that returns has all of its printed text.
- The choice ("one of his abilities", "one of its non-Gold-producing traits or abilities") is the engine-listed `effectChoice` decision Doji Hakuseki's lock already uses, so the client and the bots answer it with no new UI; one candidate is applied without asking, none does nothing. Sea & Sky's "Either destroy it or permanently remove its traits and abilities" is the same decision with two fixed options (`destroy`, `remove`).
- Shared edits (one line each): `AbilityIdsOf` (CardCopy.cs), the after-command sweep (AttachAction.cs), `ApplyBranchEffect` (EffectChoice.cs).

## Readings built in (not confirmed rulings)

- **"His abilities" (Jade Embrace)** are the Personality's own abilities of every timing (actions and Reactions), printed or granted, never his keywords or the abilities of his attachments. A Personality with none is still the legal target (the Spell is bowed and nothing is removed).
- **"If this Shugenja is Earth"** is the Earth keyword on the Spell's wielder; the target's Force is reduced to 0 with the same modifier the other "Reduce his Force to 0" actions use (until the end of the turn).
- **Oni**: the target is destroyed first and the Spell with it; a destroyed Personality needs no ability removed, so nothing is asked. The destruction of the Spell does not depend on the Oni's destruction being negated.
- **Traits** (Sea & Sky, Through the Breach) are the card's Effect: its static and triggered traits are data keyed on the Effect, so removing them means the Effect no longer applies. Keywords are not traits and stay (core rules, Reading the Cards). A Holding's printed Gold production and its Gold-source abilities are Gold-producing and are never offered by Through the Breach.
- **A removal while the card stays in play is permanent**, including across turns, and it is not negated by anything (nothing negates a card effect on a card that does not have the ability). Restoring the baseline when the card leaves play overwrites a later permanent copy the card made while its abilities were removed.
- **Interrogation's "destroys its target" list**: Sea & Sky is not in the "destroys its target" list that Interrogation reads, because the destruction is only one of its two branches and the branch is chosen after the target is named; a destruction that is actually carried out still checks the existing negation (`DestroyRegion`, `DiscardTerrain`).
- **Colonial Farm**: the printed text on the scan is "Limited: Bow this card and target your Personality" (the catalogue says "Limited, Bow"); the Farm bows and is not destroyed. Its entry discount is the general Clan discount (Effect `lion-clan-discount`).
