# Cluster strat-torn (issue #674): nineteen Torn Asunder Strategies, strategy-wave-118

Companion to [the primitive plan](2026-10-05-remaining-unwired-primitives.md) and its [card appendix](2026-10-05-remaining-unwired-primitives-cards.md). Every card is the Torn Asunder printing, wired from its own scan (read by eye; where the catalogue text differs the scan wins). Nineteen cards wired, none blocked. The code is `src/L5R.Core/WlStratTorn.cs` and `WlStratTornRun.cs` (hook priority 14700, `MatchState.WlStratTorn` at `JsonPropertyOrder` 20700); the packager is `tools/content/package_strategy_wave_118.py`; the tests are `tests/L5R.Core.Tests/WlStratTornTests*.cs` (23 scenarios).

## Scan versus catalogue

* **A Threat Enacted** prints only the **Political** keyword (the catalogue also lists Favor) and a **Gold Cost of 4**; the published card follows the scan (`Political`, cost 4, Focus 2). Its text is "Choose your performing unbowed non-Duelist Courtier Personality, target a Personality ..., and discard the Imperial Favor" (the catalogue's "Target your performing ..." is a paraphrase).
* **Scandalous Gossip** prints a Gold Cost of 1 (see the readings below); **Spoils of Exploration** prints its draw as an unlabelled trigger.
* All other texts, costs and Focus values match the catalogue.

## Readings for Mendon to confirm or correct

None is a confirmed ruling.

1. **A Threat Enacted.** "Destroy a non-Unique card in his unit without attachments" is not targeted, so the acting player picks it as the action resolves (the engine lists the choices; one choice is made without asking; none leaves only the dishonoring). The card may be the Personality himself when he has no attachments and is not Unique; "Dishonor him (if he is still in play)" then does nothing. The performer is a condition ("choose your performing unbowed ..."), not bowed. "A Personality with equal or lower Personal Honor" reads the current Personal Honor of target and performer; the performer may name himself.
2. **Courteous Gift.** The game has two players, so "choose another player" is the opponent and is not asked; he is asked "may draw" (an Either/Or). The card is a face-up card in either player's province. The Gold Cost reduction is a turn modifier on that card (2, or 4 if he drew), never below 0.
3. **Eye of the Sword.** Not refusable ("Your Personality duels the target"); the winner's two tokens come before the loser's destruction, as printed; a tie (no winner) gives no tokens and destroys both losers (Steel on Steel's shape).
4. **Forest Cover.** "You may attach" is the second of two actions sharing one use; the extra End Phase draw is queued only if the Follower actually attached. Reconnaissance is granted whether or not the Follower attaches.
5. **Forgotten Teachings.** "All traits and abilities" is the #667 removal primitive's all-key (keywords stay: they are not traits); they are restored as the turn ends, leaving any other removal in force.
6. **Improper Papers.** The window is offered when the action would move at least one Personality and the movement may be negated; the target is any Personality (as printed, like Request Authorization), and naming one the action does not move has no effect. Either player's action qualifies (no "another player's" is printed).
7. **Imprudent Misstep.** "Family Honor" is the player's Honor; "another player" excludes the acting player.
8. **In the Right.** "Attacking cards" are the Personalities of the attacking army and the Followers, Items and Spells attached to them (either side's, whoever takes the action); the limit is half the Courtier's current Chi rounded down (Chi 1 allows none, so the action cannot be played).
9. **Martyr's Call.** The +3F and Stalwart last until the end of the turn (R-001's default); the performing Paragon cannot also be a target.
10. **Overpower.** "No Attack Phase may be declared" is the active player's `MayNotAttackThisTurn` lock (no attack can be declared; the Attack Phase's declare-or-decline decision remains and can only be declined); the first or second turn is the active player's own `TurnsTaken` (1 or 2), whoever plays the Open. The Limited needs a province that holds a card (`ChoosesOwnProvince`), narrower than "discarding any card currently there"; "(not dead)" excludes a Personality in the dead pile. The Passing of Tradition's own Limited, which prints no such exclusion, accepts a dead Personality (Scavenging Party's reading).
11. **Scandalous Gossip.** Playable only from the discard pile (never from hand); its printed Gold Cost of 1 is paid as for any Strategy, and it is removed from the game (the engine's play-from-discard rule). The chosen player (it may be the acting player) chooses an unbowed Personality of his own; with none, nothing is bowed and the Honor is still gained; with exactly one it is chosen for him.
12. **Spoils of Exploration.** The unlabelled trigger is a trait (like Shiba's Guidance): while the Kata is in play its controller draws a card after any resolution in which he destroyed an enemy army or a province.
13. **Tactical Sacrifice.** "Your Follower's Gold Cost" is the destroyed Follower's current Gold Cost; the target is any Follower, or Personality without attachments, in play (either player's, not the destroyed Follower).
14. **The Last Move.** The second target ("You may target and destroy a card without attachments in his unit") is an optional slot; the Courtier is destroyed as the cost.
15. **The Passing of Tradition.** The copyable keywords are the target's current keywords other than Courtier, Samurai and Shugenja, and (a reading) Unique, that the performer lacks; the player chooses one (no choice when there is exactly one, nothing when there is none). The copy is permanent (`GiveKeywordPermanently`).
16. **Touch of the Night.** "Opposing him" is the other player's Personalities at the current battlefield and the Followers attached to them, -2F until the end of the turn.
