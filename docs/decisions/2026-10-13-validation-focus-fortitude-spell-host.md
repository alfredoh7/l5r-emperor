# Validation report fixes: Reserve Weapon focus cap, Fortitude total Force, Spell actions need a Shugenja host (issue #915)

Status: implemented per the accepted reading in `2026-10-13-validation-report-reading.md` (findings 1-3, "fix as drafted"). Lane: rules.

- **Reserve Weapon consumes a focus opportunity.** The core rules say that traits and actions that substitute "instead of focusing" count against the cap of four. Kakita Gonji already did; Reserve Weapon now shares the same counter (`P2.FocusSubstitutions`). The engine stops listing and accepting substitutions at the cap and offers only a Strike. This supersedes the 2026-09-25 reading (group G, item 33) that Reserve Weapon "does not count toward the four-focus limit" and its "no use limit" remains true only up to the cap. R-027 is unchanged: a substitution is still not a focus for Regret or a required focus count. The client lists the engine's options and counts nothing.
- **Fortitude compares the unit's total Force**, every card of the unit counted whether bowed or not (`UnitTotalForce`, the same total Kaiu House Guard, the Stronghold Batch 3 cards and `rd-enemy-personality-unit-force-lt-performer` use), instead of its current battle contribution. This supersedes reading 3 of the 2026-09-25 remaining-cards pass ("the current unit total"). Not changed here: Great Sacrifice still compares `BattleForce` (the 2026-09-21 second-city decision defines its "total Force" as the battle contribution); it has the same hole and is reported for a ruling.
- **A Spell's actions need a Shugenja host.** `AbilityIdsOf` returns nothing for a Spell attached to a Personality who is not currently a Shugenja (keyword removed, never had it), so listing, validation, Reaction scans and execution agree. The Spell stays attached and its static effects are untouched; its actions return when the host is a Shugenja again, including by a granted keyword. A regression audits every published Spell.

`ContentVersion`: a bump is advisable (command legality for existing journals changes: a fifth Reserve Weapon substitution, a Fortitude target and an action of a Spell on a non-Shugenja are now refused); branches do not bump it themselves.
