# The remaining cards of eight sets (issue #503)

With the #501 mechanics merged, every unwired card of Before the Dawn, Seeds of Decay, Embers of War, Second City, Forgotten Legacy, The Shadow's Embrace, Emperor Edition and Honor and Treachery was evaluated by six parallel passes (groups P1-P6, by blocker category) and wired where the core rules allow. **126 of the 143 cards are now wired; 17 stay blocked.** `docs/CARD_INVENTORY.xlsx`: 2183 rows, 1419 wired. Art pack `catalogue-art-pr503-20260926-r1` (3866 images). This PR is stacked on #504 (#501); it retargets to `main` once that merges.

## Still blocked (17), see `docs/CARD_INVENTORY.xlsx`

- **Created card has no Proxy scan (12)**: Kakita Saki, Kitsune Hisano - exp, Kitsune Kichi, Vanquisher's Blades, Our Ancestor's Call, Yari of Air - exp, Fruitless Search, Grateful Reward, Sword for Hire, Humble Farm, Natural Aviary, Crippled Sensei. The cards' own scans are local; the Proxies they create have receipts only (nothing was downloaded). Sword for Hire also needs a duel-scoped removal of its created card, Crippled Sensei an "after one of your Personalities is destroyed" window that covers destruction outside an action.
- **Plains of Otosan Uchi**: the second-slot province view (issue #499, Alfredo).
- **Wanted for Questioning**: "custody" is undefined in the core rules; ruling it flavour would allow the card at once.
- **Regret**: several attachments entering play in one resolution need an entering-play queue and a combined legality check.
- **Bayushi Nitoshi - inexperienced**: playing a card from the top of the Fate deck (source-zone rule, listing, payment and the client all assume hand or discard).
- **Inexorable**: "its effects will not negate other effects" needs one engine-wide record of every negation an action makes.

## Rules enforced that were not before

The core rules' "each individual Tactician may perform at most one Tactical action per turn" is now enforced (Moto Taigo three); the old `core/TacticalAdvantage` test that let one Tactician act twice was updated. `Equip (Limited)`-from-discard no longer removes a self-attaching card from the game. A duel-issuing ability now pays its declared costs.

## Readings for Mendon to confirm or correct

**P1.** Koiso's "Shugenja with the base Fire keyword" is another Shugenja with printed Fire. Damning Ceremony gives no bonus when Force is already at or above the base Gold Cost. Mara's Touch: "changes shape" is flavour; -2F cost cannot be paid while Tireless Efforts negates his Force penalties. Extending Influence's copy lasts until end of turn. Hotei's Contentment's lock lasts until end of turn (the old note read it as permanent). Bakishi's negation uses the "all" scope (no keyword grants). Osote's targets are judged once, at announcement. Tamisu: a Lion Clan Personality of either player. Shichiro: declining does not use the once per battle. Ochiai's Bravery: on a tie each loser's unit loses a card. Sanetomo's exemption covers Spells only.

**P2.** Kazutoshi: +10 Honor Requirement per Kazutoshi another player controls; "-" stays none. Gonji's replaced focus counts against the four-focus cap. Turning Away ends the duel with no loser and no further Focus Effects; the Personality is discarded, not destroyed. A Champion in Court: any of the player's Yojimbo, any location. Spirit of the Shadows' switch is not Absent. Shrine to Nimuro can target any province; "lower to 0" is a penalty repeated until it reads 0. Brothers in Battle lasts until end of turn and stacks. Gaining Advantage: declining the search means no discard. Pillowbook blocks trait and event effects as well as actions; the Wall blocks actions only, its cap is for all players. Mina can choose tokens on any card at the battlefield. Keirei: "different battlefield" includes battlefields already fought this Attack Phase; each Poison stat negated on its own.

**P3.** A Follower that leaves play with its destroyed host is not protected by Fujita or Vigilant Riders. Hida Defenders' "has opposed this card": on the other side now, or destroyed/moved from there by the action. Iron Will: in an Attack's window costs are already paid so only effects are negated; only Personality performers count. Aroru: "another of your cards" is any card; the move is still owed if the target is illegal; his lockout starts at resolution while Thunder Dragon's starts when announced. Reaction lockouts end when the action ends. Easy Persuasion: an entry with no payment counts as paid 0; the window opens after the entering player's own windows; an Experienced overlay is not an entry. Destructive Priorities covers every gain during its player's turn. Tairu: "opposing" means now at Tairu's battlefield on the other side. Kenta's target must be a dishonorable enemy at the battle. Announce windows now also hold Terrain plays, Reserve recruits and Battle equips.

**P4.** Tenzan's "may target a Shadowlands Personality" is a reminder (any Personality is legal); his Battle use is the Combat Segment and dishonors. Gozaru is destroyed by any means into the Dynasty discard. Games of Will and Show of Restraint read "Force penalties from actions" as all negative Force modifiers (Tireless Efforts' behaviour). Targets after the colon are chosen at resolution and open no "targeted by an action" window. Yojimbo Assemble's "enemy card" is a Personality or Follower. Ride the Breeze locks until end of turn. Drawing on the Mountain lasts the game and is spent only by a real straightening. Scars of War notices a bow at the end of each command (a bow and straighten in one command is missed), copies stack, Gold production counts as a bow. Too Close to Home negates battlefield-to-battlefield moves too; assignment and the return home are not movement. Hunger's obligation applies only when its controller may declare an attack. Hayan has no designator, so it is a trait, optional, once per turn, usable while bowed. Power of Strength: the acting player chooses which target bows.

**P5.** The scan wins over the catalogue: Raido has no "only enter from a province" line; Economic Repercussions and Nara have no Favor designator; Starting on the Path has no Enlightenment clause (the Ring still does not count, as the core rules require); Never Enough Soldiers is "until your Events Phase three turns from now"; Perplexing Guests' Battle half has no enemy-units condition; Searching for Answers puts the Personality into play at the battlefield (not a Recruit; Honor Requirement, Unique and Loyal apply, Gold ignored). The Price of Honor bars Order Seppuku for the Personality until end of turn. Scouting the Pass: the two cards are chosen blind (random). Two-Fold Virtue: created at home, Force equals the target's current Personal Honor, printed keywords, titled "(Two-Fold Virtue)". Stolen Merchandise lasts until end of turn. Blessings' shield lasts until used or end of turn and covers any card's effect. Plain of Glass: two Conquests work; the once-per-turn Conquest limit is still not enforced by the engine. Step Through the Void returns the unit at the top of EndTurn.

**P6.** "Refill a province face-up" (Susaiken, Distant Expansion) is read as the manner of the normal immediate face-down refill (the old conflict note is withdrawn); declining does not spend Susaiken's use. Distant Expansion lasts until its owner's fourth turn from now begins. Ietsuno's trait is mandatory; switched cards keep their own facing. Renyu's Honor shield applies to every Honor loss from an action that targeted him, including unnegatable ones (a reduction, not a negation); "create a province" is treated as "gain a province". Suikihime's leaves-play check runs after each command. Perfect Sacrifice reads each card's current Force before any bonus and lasts until end of turn. Incredible Tragedy's scan prints "Personalities may not be assigned during the Cavalry Maneuvers Segment" so the "Cavalry player ability" blocker is withdrawn.
