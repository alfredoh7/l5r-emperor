# Seeds of Decay pass: every unwired card re-checked against the engine (issue #453)

Requested by Mendon: evaluate every Seeds of Decay card against `docs/rules/EMPEROR_EDITION_CORE_RULES.md` and the engine as it now stands, wire everything that can be wired -- adding small primitives or extending existing mechanisms where that does not conflict with the core rules -- and record a specific blocker for the rest. Lane: rules. Issue: [#453](https://github.com/alfredoh7/l5r-emperor/issues/453). Same shape as the Second City pass (#264, `docs/decisions/2026-09-21-second-city-pass.md`).

At the start of the pass **126 of the 151 Seeds of Decay reference cards were unwired** (48 Personalities, 42 Strategies, 12 Items, 8 Spells, 5 Holdings, 4 Events, 3 Followers, 2 Strongholds, 1 Celestial, 1 Region). The work was split into five independent batches by card family, each evaluated and wired in its own worktree/branch off the same base commit, then union-merged. **133 of the 151 cards are now wired** (108 newly wired this pass); **18 stay unwired**, each with an updated, specific blocker note in `tools/content/build_card_inventory.py`.

## New primitives, by batch (each with its own regression test)

- **Batch 1** (`StandingRedirects.cs`, `SeedsOfDecayBatch1.cs`): a **standing redirect** -- a watch that rewrites another player's next matching action as it is announced ("the next time (this turn/this battle) an action would target X, it targets Y instead, if legal") -- for Ceaseless Vigil, Ruthless Onslaught and Metsubushi's "must target him"; a **destruction shield that outlives one action** (`CardInstance.DestructionShieldTurn`, Megumi's Guidance); a **per-battle Force non-contribution stamp** (Defensive Blockade); **assignment while bowed** (Embracing Battle, Military Promotion); a **keyword-keyed Stronghold Honor-loss immunity** plus a once-per-battle targeted redirect Reaction (The Castle of Order); Wide Valley's "negate a destruction inside a unit" trait, folded into `DestroyUnits`/`DestroyAttachment`; Grappling the Snake's granted two-way choice (negate now, or bow the performer's unit after resolution); Advanced Intelligence's per-battle Recon Strategy action count; a mandatory (non-"may") straighten Focus Effect choice.
- **Batch 2** (`SeedsOfDecayBatch2.cs`): a **timed province-strength effect** (`ProvinceStrengthEffects`, distinct from the existing permanent `ProvinceState.Strength` path) for Incriminating Testimony, Bayushi Shibata - exp and Imperial Protection's standing rule; **assignment/destruction watches** ("after the next time this turn he is assigned to attack [or is destroyed]", Imperial Protection, Fair Warning); an **action allowance** counter (Urgency's "each player may only take two more non-Reaction actions this phase"); a **before-Proclaim negation window** and **Proclaim locks** (Turning the Story, Yogo Ugimori); **permanent keyword removal** (Manipulate Energies' "remove" half, alongside the existing "give"); the **Honor-loss record on a resolved action** (`CurrentActionHonorLosers`/`LastResolvedAction.HonorLosers`, An Insult Answered's "which caused you an Honor loss").
- **Batch 3** (`SeedsOfDecayBatch3.cs`, `MoshiInoStorm.cs`, `TargetOfLastResort.cs`): a true **Exchange of Force and Chi** (Asako Jukko, Tanemono -- the Glossary's Exchange, not a Set); a **per-card once-a-turn automatic negation of another player's movement** (Hida Kaiji's "before the first time each turn ... negate his movement"); a **per-card attacking-victory stamp** (Yoritomo Iwashi's "has been in an attacking army when it won a battle since your last turn began"); a **target-of-last-resort restriction** inside target legality (Goju Yurishi - exp: "may not target Yurishi with actions if any other legal targets are available"); **Moshi Ino's storm** as a standing watch answered through the existing Either/Or primitive (the acting player chooses discard-a-card or bow-the-performer after the next non-Thunder action); a **Loyal-like Clan control refusal** (Tanemono: "will not be controlled by a Phoenix Clan player"); `CreatedCardSpec.HostIsSource` and first-slot hosts for a created attachment; the **battle-action-performer mover** (Shosuro Hawado - exp: "target a Personality that performed" the just-resolved Battle action).
- **Batch 4** (`StaticKeywordRules.cs`, `AttachmentTransfers.cs`): **static keyword rules** -- a card in play granting or suppressing a keyword (or a Clan alignment) over a category of its controller's cards, hooked into `Keyword()`/`ClanAlignments` -- for Ikoma Aimi, Seppun Nagi, Tsuruchi Nobukatsu - exp, Shinjo Katsuo and Zansho's Force term; a general **attachment transfer** primitive (`CanTransferTo`/`TransferAttachment`: same controller, attaching restrictions still apply, no cost re-paid) for Daigotsu Yamato, Hiruma Sawai and Cavalry Detachment's created Personality; **additional actions extended to the Action Phase** (`ContinueUseAbility`/`Pass` now consume or forfeit Limited/Open/BattleOrOpen grants, not Battle only); an **any-of keyword search criterion** (`SearchCriteria.AnyKeywords`, Naka Mahatsu - exp); two new **Events Phase decisions** (`offeringReverence`, `testedLandProvince`), dispatched the way Harsh Choices already is; a **created Personality at the target's location**, transferring attachments before its entry window opens (Cavalry Detachment).
- **Batch 5** (`SeedsOfDecayBatch5.cs`): **Moksha Patam's after-action-Honor-gained trait** (`CurrentActionHonorGained`, read once by `ApplyMokshaPatamTokens` when the action in progress is truly done); **Sandalwood Incense's effect-key Focus Effect choice** (a choice between two EFFECTS rather than two cards, generalizing `FocusEffectChoice.cs`'s candidate shape); a **"negate while at this battlefield" movement lock** variant (Moksha Patam's "negate his movement to this battlefield while this card is here"); a general **"negate all current effects on it from other players' cards" plus Force-penalty negation** (Peace of the Kami, reusing/extending `ForcePenalties.cs`); a **conditional destroy after a bow-and-optional-discard sequence** (Celestial Sword of the Scorpion, via `BeginConditionalChoice`); the **Extend the Soul's Boundaries Either/Or** (the target's controller chooses to shuffle the unit into the owners' decks, or the acting player takes two additional Battle actions).

## Readings and assumptions (Mendon to confirm or correct; each is one line to change)

Batch reports (kept as scratch, not committed) name a numbered reading per card where the printed text or the scan needed interpretation. The recurring shapes, for a quick scan:

1. **"At any location"** (Tsuruchi Nobukatsu - exp, Daigotsu Yamato's Follower, Hiruma Sawai's two Personalities) reads as the existing `AtAnyLocation` exemption (home, current battlefield, or any unresolved one), for either player's card, exempting only the ordinary Battle-timing presence check -- not a new location concept.
2. **A timed (until-turn-end) province-strength bonus** (batch 2's `ProvinceStrengthEffects`) composes with the existing permanent `Strength`, Times of Strife's ceiling and Ancient Feud's cap by reading the sum at `EffectiveProvinceStrength`, the same point those already apply.
3. **"Blockade it"** (Hachi's Revenge) and **"Dainagon may Lobby as an Open action"**-style flavor clauses with no printed mechanical verb are read as flavor text with no rules effect, per the core rules' own Reading the Cards section (only phrasing referencing defined terms is rules-relevant).
4. **Scan-vs-catalogue wording differences**: several cards' bundled scans phrase a clause slightly differently from the catalogue text pulled into `CardDefinition.Text` (e.g. Ceaseless Vigil's scan says "Choose your performing Yojimbo" where the catalogue says "Target your performing Yojimbo" -- wired as a performer, matching the scan's own mechanical shape); each batch's own scratch report lists its specific instances. The published `Text` field keeps the catalogue's wording per existing policy.

## Not wired (18 cards) -- exact blocker notes in `tools/content/build_card_inventory.py`

- **Control transfer of an in-play Personality** (deliberately unbuilt, `docs/decisions/2026-09-12-unique-singular-control-transfer.md`): Goju Asuka, Black Marketeering, Only a Shadow.
- **A fifth/extra province** (a player's four provinces are a fixed, position-indexed list read everywhere): Mining Apparatus, Otomo Suikihime.
- **A Reaction lockout closing the other player's side of every reaction window**: Thunder Dragon - exp2.
- **An Additional Action usable in the Action Phase, not just Battle** (beyond what batch 4's extension already covers -- Isawa Momoko's own shape needs the grant offered mid-resolution of the SAME ability, not a separate later action): Isawa Momoko.
- **A per-Tactician one-Tactical-action-per-turn cap** (the Glossary states it; the engine does not enforce it, so nothing needs raising for Moto Taigo): Moto Taigo.
- **A Battle action taken by a dead card from the dead pile, at full recruit price, without the ordinary province-refill entry**: Daigotsu Yutaka.
- **Delayed revival** ("remove from the game; before this turn ends, put the unit back into play ignoring costs/entering-play effects/restrictions"): Step Through the Void.
- **A dual-source (discard-pile-then-deck) search for one card**: Celestial Sword of the Dragon - exp.
- **A permanent, per-province, game-long mark with sub-triggers of its own**: Dark Experiments.
- **An undefined rulebook term** ("the rulebook Cavalry player ability" is not defined anywhere in the compiled core rules): Incredible Tragedy.
- **A player-level granted ability bounded to a future turn count**: Starting on the Path.
- **Two gaps at once**: Learning (an opponent's discard-pile Strategy played by its non-owner, plus a per-instance use-limit raise); Turning Away (a pre-resolution Focus Effect stage that can end a duel without resolving); Unsettling Doubts (a Ring's own entry provenance, plus permanent removal of a card's abilities); Aramasu's Legacy (a second Attack Phase with its own resolution exemptions).

## Merge notes

Five batches were built in parallel worktrees off the same base commit and union-merged one at a time. Most conflicts were independent appends (new `AbilityCatalog.All` entries, new switch-expression/switch-statement arms, new lines in a wave-registration or embedded-resource list) and resolved mechanically. A handful needed a hand merge because two batches modified the very same statement -- an ability-agnostic Focus Effect helper method both batches added a branch to, two `abilityId is "a" or "b";` boolean chains, an `if` with two independent exemption lists, and one switch-expression arm both batches had separately extended from the same base line (the duplicate was dropped, not kept twice, since a repeated case pattern is a compile error). Two mechanical resolutions silently dropped a `break;` that happened to sit exactly on a conflict boundary; both were caught immediately by the C# compiler (CS0163, case fallthrough) rather than by inspection, and one dropped comma in `tools/content/build_card_inventory.py`'s dict literal was caught by `ast.parse`. Every merge commit was rebuilt and re-run through the full suite before moving to the next.
