# Gold payment rulings: the second player's payment, current-cost totals, A Stain Cleansed, At Any Cost, Disgraceful Conduct

Status: accepted (Mendon, standing authority; rulings verbatim below; the four readings this work first left open were confirmed by Mendon on PR #378 and are recorded in "Confirmed readings"). Lane: shared (rules engine, MatchView, an engine-owned decision the client must present). Issue #373. Extends [2026-09-18-generalized-gold-payment.md](2026-09-18-generalized-gold-payment.md), whose four open questions these answer.

## Rulings (Mendon)

1. **Virtuous Charity** (`oracle-l5r-10181`, "Open: If it is not your turn, choose another player: You come across some starving peasants in need. You, then the player, each pay zero or more Gold. Whoever paid more Gold gains Honor equal to the difference between the two amounts paid, or 4, whichever is less."): "the second player should see how much the first player paid and should be prompted to pay using holdings (they may pay any value, including 0)."
2. **Total Gold Cost of a unit or Personality**: "should be the total of the CURRENT cost of every card in the unit. Some cards have Gold cost reduction effects (including tokens and actions). These should be taken into account when summing the cost of a unit or a Personality for action effects."
3. **A Stain Cleansed** (`oracle-l5r-82`): first ruled +2 (relayed as "A 9-cost unit would be 11"); then, after re-reading the scan, the final ruling: "pay Gold equal to the card or unit's total Gold Cost minus 2. That really is the total cost of the unit minus 2, not the card destroyed being equivalent in cost to what was paid minus 2." So the price is the target's current total Gold cost MINUS 2 (floor 0): Daidoji Ebizo alone (9) costs 7. "No choice needs to be given": the target selection decides the payment path: a Personality WITH attachments is priced by his unit's total current Gold cost minus 2; a card without attachments by its own current Gold cost minus 2 (a Personality with no attachments is the same number either way). The scan governs.
4. **At Any Cost** (`oracle-l5r-639`): "has the same entry requirements as any recruit action (This Personality may not be proclaimed), so the restrictions hold and Singular, Unique, Sensei, Honor Requirement, and Loyal still impact recruitment." And, on the destination: "Recruit into play at the battlefield at which At Any Cost is played."
5. **Disgraceful Conduct** (`oracle-l5r-2020`): uses the same current-cost total (ruling 2) and the payment path. "The player of Disgraceful Conduct chooses: either the Personality or the unit and then pays the cost accordingly (the player is either choosing to target the Personality or the unit)."
6. **Virtuous Charity's negation**: "If the honor gain is negated, the payment is still made. If the action is negated, then no payment is made."

## What was built

### The second player's variable payment (Virtuous Charity; `OpponentPayment.cs`)

A general primitive, the numeric sibling of the Either/Or choice (`EitherOr.cs`): an action whose text has the OTHER player pay "zero or more Gold".

- The acting player's amount is the ability's variable Gold payment (`Cost: GoldCostRule.Variable(atResolution: true)`): it is quoted and validated when the action is announced and chosen (`GameCommand.GoldAmount`, `SourceIds`) exactly like every other variable cost, but it is part of the action's RESOLUTION, not a cost (Mendon): `PayForAbility` and the negated-action path skip an `AtResolution` rule, so an action negated whole pays nothing for either player, and the effect pays it (through `Pay`) when it resolves, ahead of the second player's decision. "Choose another player" is the one opponent, so nothing is asked about who.
- Once the amount is paid, the played Strategy goes to the discard pile (in the open) and a `PendingOpponentPayment` (state) with `PendingChoice.Type == "opponentPayment"` hands the decision to the other player, who holds priority. `QueryActions` offers them one action, `chooseOpponentPayment`, carrying a variable `GoldQuote` (every amount from 0 to what they can produce, each with suggested sources; `Sakuma` and every other Gold source the payment path knows are counted) and a complete default command (`GoldAmount: 0`, no sources), so a bot or any legal-actions-driven client can always answer. The answer is the same command with `GoldAmount` and `SourceIds`; it goes through `Pay`, so sources are bowed and excess production is lost as in every payment. An amount above what they can produce, a negative one, short, foreign, bowed or duplicated sources, sources for a zero amount, and any other command are rejected atomically. Only the deciding player may answer.
- When the second player can produce no Gold there is nothing to choose: they pay nothing, `opponentPaymentAutoResolved` is announced and the action finishes without a decision (the Either/Or convention).
- What the two amounts do is a per-card spec (`OpponentPaymentSpecs`, `OpponentPaymentHonor`). Virtuous Charity's: whoever paid more gains Honor equal to the difference, capped at 4; equal amounts move nothing. A fixture spec (`training-opponent-payment`, each player gains Honor equal to what the other paid) proves the seam is not Virtuous Charity's own.
- The Honor gain is the last step and can be negated alone: a reaction that adds an `honor-gain` negation for the action's source card (the fixture `training-negate-honor-gain`; no printed card does this yet) leaves both payments made and gives nobody Honor (`honorGainNegated` is announced). The negations are kept with the paused action (`PendingEffectChoice.Negated`) across the second player's decision. Whole-action negation is exercised through a fixture reaction (`training-negate-untargeted-action`, a before-action reaction to an action that names no card) and the `NegateEveryAction` seam; no printed card negates an action that names no card.
- The action then finishes in the order it always would have (`CompleteAbilityResolution`, shared with the conditional effect choice), priority passing on as after any action. Seppuku is not offered and is rejected while the decision is open (as for Either/Or and copy).
- The practice opponent answers with the engine's default (paying nothing).

**Hidden information: what each viewer sees.** `MatchView.PendingOpponentPayment` (`OpponentPaymentView`) is present in both views while the decision is open.

| Field | Acting player | Second player (deciding) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| card, who is acting, who decides, `FirstAmount`, `Prompt`, `Detail` (the Honor at stake in the card's terms) | yes | yes (the second player is shown the first amount before choosing) |
| `Gold` (the variable quote with suggested sources per amount) | no | yes |
| `Outcomes` (what each amount would do, in the deciding player's words) | no | yes |
| the amount they will choose | unknown until submitted | their own choice |

Nothing else is hidden information: Gold sources in play are public cards. The acting player's hand stays hidden throughout (the Strategy is already in the discard pile). Test: `GoldRulingsTests.SecondPlayerSeesFirstAmount`.

The client work (a decision frame for the second player, reusing the payment panel, which can serve unchanged: what is missing is a way in for a player who is not acting) is issue #377. The engine ships a scripted position for it: `MatchEngine.CreateOpponentPaymentShowcase`, scenario `opponent-payment-showcase` (the Crane passes, the Lion plays Virtuous Charity, the Crane pays); `GoldRulingsTests.ShowcaseFlow` plays it through and replays it.

### Total Gold cost is the current cost (ruling 2)

Already true on `main` when this work started: `MatchEngine.CardGoldCost` (issue #336) is the printed cost plus every `GoldCost` modifier and token, never below 0, and `UnitTotalGoldCost` sums it over the Personality and each attachment (Followers, Items, Spells). The line in the 2026-09-18 doc that said "printed costs" described that change's first version; it is superseded. This work pins the ruling through the derived-cost cards: Disgraceful Conduct and A Stain Cleansed are priced term by term from current costs (a modifier, a token, both, a raise, an attachment's own reduction, a cost reduced to 0 counting as 0, a Personality reduced to 0 leaving the attachments), and the price floor is applied to the total. Entry discounts ("enters play paying 1 less Gold if you are a Crane Clan player", Katagi, Hotako, Prosperous Village) are payment discounts, not changes to a card's Gold cost, and do not enter the total (`DisgracefulZeroAndFloor`). The readers that stay on the printed cost are those whose text says so ("base Gold Cost": Yasuki Tsujiken, Reckless Rush) and cards not in play (`target-printed`).

### At Any Cost (`oracle-l5r-639`, `at-any-cost-recruit`)

`Dark Virtue Battle, :gstar:: Recruit your target performing dead Samurai.` A Battle action (so it needs a unit at the current battlefield) whose target is one of your own dead Samurai (`own-dead-samurai`). The price and every restriction come from one place: `QuotePurchase(..., fromDeadPile: true)`, the recruit path's own quote, where the only difference from a province recruit is where the card is. So Unique and Singular, Honor Requirement (with no Clan allegiance bypass, as for Shinden Shorai and Your Clan Needs You!, whose recruits are "all costs"), Loyal, and every entry discount apply exactly as for any recruit, and the quote is never Proclaim-eligible. The Personality enters play at the current battlefield, the one At Any Cost was played at (Mendon), through the same entry sequence as a province recruit (`EnterPlayFromRecruit`, `OpenRecruitEntryWindows`, refactored out of `BringProvincePersonalityIntoPlay` with no change to it), so he is in that army at once (a recruited Magistrate can perform a Battle action immediately), Sensei discards the player's other Sensei, Hotako's Honor loss applies and entry Reactions open. He returns as a new card: `ResetCardState` (also used by the Overlay transfer) clears modifiers, keywords, uses and tokens, so a Personality who died bowed returns straight. **Superseded 2026-10-13 (R-081, EE-STATE-008):** this line also said a Personality who died dishonorable returned honorable. A face-up dishonorable Personality now stays dishonorable outside play and on re-entry unless an effect rehonors him; see [the validation-report reading](2026-10-13-validation-report-reading.md) (item 13) and RULINGS R-081. The regression `GoldRulingsTests.AtAnyCostEntry` now pins that he returns dishonorable and bowed status still clears. The played Strategy is spent as it is played. The Battle action's own presence requirement (a unit of yours at the battlefield, the scan's "If you control any units at the current battlefield") is unchanged: the action is not `Absent`.

### Disgraceful Conduct (`oracle-l5r-2020`)

`Battle: Even if you control no units at the current battlefield, pay Gold equal to a target Personality or unit's total Gold cost minus 4: Bow it.` Absent Battle actions (no unit of yours needed; the target still has to be at the battlefield), either side's Personality. The player chooses the Personality or the unit (Mendon), modelled as two abilities of the card that share a usage key, so the choice is part of the command the engine offers and each mode has its own quote:

- `disgraceful-conduct-bow`: the Personality alone, `TargetCurrent(-4, floor 0)` (his own current Gold cost, a new derived basis), only he bows;
- `disgraceful-conduct-bow-unit`: the whole unit, `TargetUnitTotal(-4, floor 0)` (his current cost plus every attachment's current cost), and the Personality and every card attached to him bow. It is offered only for a Personality that has attachments (`personality-with-attachment`): for a bare Personality the two modes are the same number, so he is offered once, in the Personality mode.

Stalwart-style bow negations are judged per card.

### A Stain Cleansed (`oracle-l5r-82`, `a-stain-cleansed-destroy`)

The scan: "Battle: Choose your performing unbowed Magistrate, target an enemy card without attachments or an enemy unit, and pay Gold equal to the card or unit's total Gold Cost minus 2: Destroy it." Performer: your unbowed Magistrate at the battlefield. Target: an enemy Personality or an enemy attachment. Price `TargetUnitTotal(-2, floor 0)`: a Personality's whole unit's current total (a bare Personality: his own cost) or an attachment's own current cost, minus 2. Each legal target ships its own quote; a target you cannot afford is not offered (and with none affordable the action is disabled). Daidoji Ebizo alone costs 7. A Personality's unit is destroyed with him; an attachment goes alone (its Personality stays). The published text is the catalogue (Oracle) text, which words it the other way round ("Gold Cost equal to what you paid minus 2"); the scan governs the rule and the sign is the one number `GoldCostRule.TargetUnitTotal(-2, floor: 0)` on `a-stain-cleansed-destroy` (`StainSignIsOneConstant`).

## Behaviour changes to existing cards

None from ruling 2: `main` already summed current costs (Gold and Steel's "equal or lower Gold Cost", Bonds of Coin's "0 Gold Cost" and the unit total all read `CardGoldCost`). `GoldQuotes.TargetRecruit` now also prices a dead Personality and its basis names the discounts that applied ("Daigotsu Hotako recruit cost 9 (Daigotsu Hotako cost 11 − 2 (Lion Clan))"); the province recruit quotes are unchanged. `MatchEngine.QuotePurchase` gained one optional parameter (`fromDeadPile`); every existing caller is unchanged. Your Clan Needs You! had returned early when its recruit opened a window, skipping the tail that discards a hand-played Strategy (found while wiring At Any Cost; fixed separately in #380, which spends the card as the window opens); At Any Cost spends its card before it can.

## Confirmed readings (Mendon, on PR #378)

The four readings this work first implemented provisionally were answered and are applied; none is open.

1. **A Stain Cleansed**: the scan governs. Price = the target's current total Gold cost minus 2 (Ebizo 7), unit total for a Personality with attachments, the card's own current cost for a card without. (An intermediate message relayed +2; it was withdrawn.)
2. **At Any Cost**: the dead Samurai enters play at the current battlefield, not Home; every ordinary recruit restriction stays and he is never proclaimable. One point to confirm: Mendon's suggested test, "a battle with no units of the player", cannot arise while the action needs a unit at the battlefield (the scan's condition, kept); the test instead proves he is a unit at the battlefield the moment he enters. If At Any Cost should also be playable with no unit of yours there, it is one flag (`Absent: true` on `at-any-cost-recruit`).
3. **Disgraceful Conduct**: the player chooses the Personality or the unit and pays accordingly; the unit mode bows the Personality and every attached card.
4. **Virtuous Charity**: the payments are part of the resolution. A negated action pays nothing; a negated Honor gain leaves both payments made.
