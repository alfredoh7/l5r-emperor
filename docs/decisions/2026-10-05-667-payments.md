# Coin Pouch, Near Miss and Profits: three payment sources (#667)

Status: accepted as engineering readings built to Mendon's rulings (quoted below); the readings marked "to confirm" are Mendon's to confirm or overrule. Lane: rules (mdornbrook). Issue: #667.
Waves: `item-wave-44` (Coin Pouch, oracle-l5r-1427) and `strategy-wave-113` (Near Miss, oracle-l5r-5523; Profits, oracle-l5r-6075). Coin Pouch and Near Miss are Promotional-Celestial printings (the only printing
the catalogue has; the scans were read and print the catalogue's text); Profits is the Promotional-Emperor printing.

## Mendon's rulings (confirmed)

- **Coin Pouch**: "Technically, this can react to an opponent's gold production but don't wire that as it's unlikely to occur. Instead, flow it into the gold payment path as a payment option and don't offer it as a
  reaction. If the player uses a payment path that utilizes the coin pouch's reaction, bow it after paying for the card. It cannot produce 1 gold on its own, but it can augment a holding. [...] 'if a player bows a
  holding to produce >0 gold, they may also bow the Coin Pouch to produce 1 gold toward the same cost.'"
- **Near Miss**: "when in hand, treat it as a gold source when paying for an attachment. If the player selects a gold flow (add it to the gold flow like a holding) option that uses Near Miss to produce gold, discard it."
- **Profits**: "Recruit profits to a player's holdings area. When in play, add 'use Profits' to the gold flow when paying for cards (for each Profits in play, mouseover this button highlights the specific Profits it
  affects) (as any other action, it may only be used once per turn). Once Profits has >0 tokens on it, add it to the Gold flow payment path option as a source of gold. If the player selects a payment path option with
  Profits in it, discard profits."

## One extension point

The Gold payment path already had "payment tokens" (EmperorMechE.cs, issue #501): a source named in `GameCommand.SourceIds` as `<card>#<kind>`, listed in the quote's `Sources`, searched by `PaymentOptions`, validated by
`ValidatePayment` and spent by `Pay` (`UsePaymentTokens`). `PromoPaymentSources.cs` adds four kinds to that list (`PromoPaymentSourceTokens`, appended in `PaymentTokens`); nothing else about the contract changes.

| Token | Card | Produces | Offered when | Cost of choosing it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `<pouch>#pouch` | Coin Pouch (attached, unbowed) | +1 | the payment has a Holding that can bow for Gold, and the Pouch's turn use is unspent | the Pouch bows after the payment; only valid beside a Holding bowed in the same payment |
| `<near miss>#near-miss` | Near Miss in the payer's hand | +2 | paying for an Item, Follower or Spell (`PaymentKind.Equip`) | discarded as the payment is made |
| `<profits>#profits-reduce` ("Use Profits") | Profits in play | -1 | paying for a card (`Recruit`, `Equip` or `Play`) whose printed Gold Cost is 1 or more; once a turn for each Profits | a Wealth token is put on that Profits |
| `<profits>#profits-gold` | Profits in play with Wealth tokens | the number of Wealth tokens | any payment (with a subject) | discarded as the payment is made |

The reduce and the discard of one Profits are one card's tokens, so a payment never names both (RequirePaymentTokens: one token per card).

Payment suggestions (`PaymentOptions`, which the bots also take as `Payments[0]`) never include "Use Profits", and list every payment that needs no optional source (a Pouch bow, a Near Miss or Profits discard) before
any that does. A suggestion that names the Pouch always has its Holding.

Near Miss's first Reaction is an ordinary targeted-action Reaction played from the hand (`ReactionTriggers.TargetedByAction`, like Know No Fear): it negates the held action's destruction of the attached card
(`NegateEffects(card, "destroy")`).

## Readings built in

1. **The Wealth token is put on Profits as the payment completes**, not in a separate step after the other card has entered play. Every `Pay` is followed immediately by the card entering play in the same command, so
   the two are indistinguishable except that a payment whose command is then rejected is rolled back with the rest of the command.
2. **"A card with a base Gold Cost of 1 or greater"** is the paid-for card's printed Gold Cost (`Definition(card).GoldCost`), as "base" says. A card discounted to a 0 Gold payment names no
   sources at all (the engine's "free action" rule), so Use Profits is unavailable there; a discount that leaves the payment at 1 or more still allows it.
3. **"Paying for a card"** is a card bought (`Recruit`), equipped (`Equip`) or played from a hand (`Play`); an action of a card in play (`Action`) is not. A Strategy of Gold Cost 0 is never paid for.
4. **Profits' Gold Reaction** works for any payment that has a subject (the same reach as Seaside Bazaar: a payment with no subject, an internal one, has none). Chosen with other Profits' tokens it is one use of that
   card; Profits discarded this way discards the card from play (Fate discard pile) with its tokens.
5. **"Once per turn"** for Use Profits is one use for each Profits card in play (its `AbilityUses`), so two Profits give two uses.
6. **Coin Pouch "produces one additional Gold"** is read as a +1 source of the Pouch's own that must be beside a Holding bowed for more than 0 Gold in the same payment (any non-token Holding source of the payment; the
   Holdings' alternative production tokens are not counted as "bowing a Holding"). It is used once per turn (its Reaction use), and the Pouch bows as the payment is made; it is not offered while bowed.
7. **Coin Pouch enters play bowed** wherever an attachment is put onto a Personality by Equip and by the attach-by-ability path (AttachAction.cs); the other special attach paths (a few single-card abilities) do not
   apply it. **+2 Gold Cost** is a term of `CardGoldCost` for the Personality for each Coin Pouch attached to him (so it follows the recruit price and every comparison of Gold Cost).
8. **Near Miss's negation** is offered against another player's Battle action (`IsBattleAction`) whose target is an Item, Follower or Spell of the reactor's attached to a Personality, that could destroy it (an Attack, or
   an ability on the curated attachment-destroying list, `DestroysAttachmentTarget`). It negates the destruction only (the action's other effects stand). It is not offered against the reactor's own action. (to confirm)
9. **Cherry Blossom Festival** bars Coin Pouch, Near Miss and Profits' Gold Reactions like every other Gold-source Reaction, but not Use Profits (an optional "before you produce" term, not a Reaction).

## Client contract

No new message. `PaymentSource` already carries `Id`, `CardId`, `Kind`, `Label`, `Produced` and `ConflictingSourceIds` and the client already renders one check box per source, grouped by card. New: `Kind "reduce"` with a
negative `Produced` (Use Profits) is understood by the panel and counted in its summary; pointing at (or focusing) any source highlights its card, so each Profits in play names its own card; and a chosen
source's card shows the selected mark after a re-render (`<card>#<kind>` ids). Probe: `gold-profits[-confirm]` in `tools/check-gold-payment.sh`.

## Known limits

- The payment panel's Coin Pouch choice does not explain, before the player confirms, that it needs a Holding in the payment; the engine refuses a payment that names the Pouch without one.
- Near Miss is a source for an attachment paid for through Equip (the Limited and Battle equips, and attach-by-ability payments priced as an attachment); an attachment that enters play without being paid for
  (a free attach) has no payment to add it to.
