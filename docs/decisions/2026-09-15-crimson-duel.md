# Crimson Standoff duel presentation

Status: art direction accepted by Alfredo on 2026-09-15. Issue #205, PR #220, UI lane.

The exact approved target is `assets/ui/duel-arena/crimson/approved-crimson-standoff.png` (concept iteration 5). It supersedes the moonlit garden, architecture, lantern and central-pine direction in the previous decision. Preserve those historical files but do not load them in the new default review.

The accepted composition has a crimson sunset and layered mountains; an indigo campaign cloth with oxblood binding, gold trim and subtle aged blood stains; a muted five-ring emblem with crossed-katana duel marker; large, flat personality cards with physical attachments; focus rows, local hand and decision controls. No environmental warriors, DUEL/VS heading or foreground reeds. Actual original card scans replace generated mockup cards. This direction authorizes built-in imagegen to derive production textures, superseding the old external-only image workflow for this task.

Implementation proceeds through composition, hybrid environment, animation and native comparison. A detailed landscape plate preserves the art, actual Blender cloth geometry provides the foreground, and instanced vegetation receives coherent root-anchored wind. Cloud movement must preserve the fixed sun and mountains. Motion must be judged in recordings and measured frame times; a changed-pixel test alone does not establish AAA quality. Reduced motion freezes every ambient clock. Honest native evidence, source fidelity and human art acceptance remain distinct.

The current bounded pass is the reusable presentation and standalone native duel review. Authoritative mechanics remain with Mendon. Live match transitions, focus/reveal commands and outcomes require the later integration stage and existing engine-provided choices. No generated mockup is evidence of gameplay or original scan fidelity.
