## `RefillProvince`, the one new search disposition

"Refill this Province with him, face up" (Kitsu Miwa, Jade Pearl Inn) cannot use the existing `RefillSourceProvinceFaceUp`, which holds the card until a revealed Event leaves its Province. Both cards act after the Province has already refilled face down (Miwa has just been discarded from it, the Inn has just been recruited from it), so `RefillProvince` puts the card found into the Province face up and returns the card that had refilled it to the Dynasty deck, which every search shuffles afterwards: the same as if the refill had been replaced. A search that finds nothing leaves the ordinary face-down refill in place. The Province is remembered in `MatchState.Wave739Refills` (one entry, replaced at each use).

## Readings for Mendon to confirm or correct

1. **Ikoma Jeiku**: "the Province Jeiku entered play from" is the Province of his recruit; the Invest is offered only in the entering-play window of a Jeiku recruited from a Province. As after Agasha Ryo's refill, the card that refilled the Province face down is turned face up (a Province left empty by an empty deck is refilled face up). It is an Invest, so it is not a Reaction (R4) and costs 1 Gold.
2. **Iuchi Wattu**: "in your discard pile, honorably dead" is the dead pile (`Zone.Dead`, not Dishonorable); "other dead copies of Wattu there" are copies of Wattu in that pile. With none the Province refills face up from the Dynasty deck; with one the Province refills face down as after any discard. A Province whose slot is the second of a two-card Province refills normally.
3. **Iweko Seiken**: "will not be dishonored" is P11's `NoDishonor`; "will not gain a Clan Alignment" is P10's gain block for as long as he is in play (as for Iweko Shibatsu). "After you Recruit Seiken" is an entering-play Reaction available only when he entered play from a Province; "Recruit a non-Unique Samurai with lower Gold Cost" targets a face-up Samurai in one of his controller's Provinces whose Gold Cost is lower than Seiken's (11), who enters at home ignoring Gold and Honor Requirement; a face-down card is not a candidate. The Favor action is a Political Open with the bow as its cost.
4. **Kitsu Miwa**: the discard (a cost, paid at the announcement) and the 2 Gold are paid before the search; the search is not optional while a non-Unique Lion Clan Shugenja is in the deck, and a Unique one or one without the Lion Clan keyword is not eligible. The Shugenja found refills Miwa's Province face up (reading above).
5. **Yoritomo Mikaru**: the printed "(*)" is read as paying the recruit's Gold (the Holding's printed Gold Cost, 1 less), with "entering play bowed" as printed. Only a face-up Holding in one of his controller's own Provinces is a target. The recruit enters at no battlefield and without a Proclaim.
6. **Disciples of Master Coin**: "a Holding with equal or lower Gold Cost" is compared with this Follower's Gold Cost (4), and any player's Holding is a target. "Did not enter play this turn" is P21's `EnteredPlayTurn`.
7. **Blood of the Preserver - exp**: "the amount paid" is the Gold chosen for the variable cost; his "Gold Cost" is his current Gold Cost. If it is more than the amount paid he stays dead, but the Honor is lost and the Item destroyed (the condition belongs to the first sentence only). He enters at home. The action is listed as Battle/Open with the Maho keyword.
8. **Paneki's Mask**: "who was in play during your last turn" is P21's reading (one of the owner's Personalities in play at the end of his last turn). The Gold is the Mask's printed Gold Cost ("you must pay its cost"). The Personality enters at home ignoring costs and restrictions and gets Shadowlands and Undead permanently, whether or not the Mask can attach (a Mask that cannot attach, for instance because the Personality will not take it, stays in the hand); 3 Honor is lost either way.
9. **Poison the Cup**: "This Shugenja has Shadowlands" applies to the Shugenja it is attached to while it is attached. "Remove a target token" is a choice, by the acting player, among the target Personality's tokens (one token needs no choice), and the target must have a token. The token is removed, then the -1C Plague token is given and 1 Honor lost, all by the acting player.
10. **Unbound Essence**: the cards shown are shown publicly, from the top of the target's controller's Dynasty deck, until a Personality; the cards shown before him stay on top in their order, and the deck is not shuffled. The overlay is refused (the target stays) when P22's overlay rules refuse it (a Unique card already controlled, a Loyal card of another Clan). "Who must be non-Unique unless this Shugenja is Void" is checked as the target is chosen.
11. **Now We Are Enemies**: "in the same army" is two Personalities of the same controller at the current battlefield; "do not share a Clan alignment" means their Clan alignments have nothing in common (two Unaligned Personalities qualify). The offer lists a candidate for the second slot while some legal first choice admits it, as P25's reading 2 says.
12. **Hidden Weapons**: "Equip Weapons from under this Holding as a Battle/Limited action" is one ability for each timing, paying the Weapon's ordinary Equip cost to a Personality of yours, any number of times. The Weapon put under it face down stays private to its owner. Cards under a Holding that leaves play are discarded (P21's reading).
13. **Jade Pearl Inn**: the Interrupt is modelled as a Reaction in the Holding's entering-play window, usable while the Holding is bowed (Holdings enter play bowed), as a search that the owner may not decline while a qualifying Holding exists ("a non-Unique, Gold-producing Holding with a printed Gold Cost of 1" is `GoldProducing` with a printed Gold Cost of 1). The refill is the one reading above.
14. **The Tachikaze**: "your Follower with a Gold Cost of 1 or more" is the Follower's printed Gold Cost; "put it face-up under this card" is optional (a Reaction). The Battle and the Limited attach are the same use ("Any number of times per turn"), paying the Follower's whole cost (`AttachCost`) to a Personality of yours.
15. **Dark Audience**: the scan's "Limited:" on an Event is its resolution when revealed. "Two attachments with different titles" is Follower, Item and Spell cards; when fewer than two titles are available the most that can be taken is required (P20's reading), and "another player" is the one other player. The Event is discarded afterwards.
16. **Kokure's Lands**: "This province does not hold Dynasty cards" stops the Province from refilling (and from receiving a Dynasty card by any refill or search) while the Region is attached; a Dynasty card already in the Province stays there until it leaves. Revealed in the Events Phase, the Region attaches and the refill that follows every reveal finds the Province locked. When the Region leaves, the Province refills again.
17. **Asako Usohara**: "Put a Fudo Ring from your hand into play. This counts as playing it by its own text" is read as: the Ring enters play unconditionally (none of its own conditions is checked), is recorded as entering by its own text (it counts for the Enlightenment Victory and for Fudo's Assurances), and, because every Fudo Ring is a False Ring whose own text says "lose 2 Honor", its controller loses 2 Honor. "Remove Usohara from the game" is an effect, so his unit leaves the game after the Ring entered. Brotherhood Schism and Losing the Way still bar a second Ring in a turn.
