## Not wired (1), with a blocker note in `tools/content/pe_wave_740_blockers.json`

- **The Empire's Foe Exposed**: "Limited: Target two Unique Personalities in play, exactly one of which is yours. Your Personality challenges the other Personality. Destroy the duel's loser." Its scan prints no "After you reveal" clause, so it is an ordinary Event that resolves when it is revealed in the Events Phase. Every merged duel starts from an ability a player uses (`IssueChallenge` is reached only from the duel-issuing branch of an ability's effects, in a battle or the Action Phase). Starting a duel from inside an Event's reveal, and resuming the Events Phase's reveal loop when the duel ends, is not a merged path, and the Event has no ability to use later. It is left unpublished rather than approximated.

## Readings for Mendon to confirm or correct

1. **Akodo Kano - exp**: "Kano's Force bonuses from the rulebook Tactical Advantage action" are the bonuses of his own use of it (he is the performing Tactician); each gets +2 on top of the discarded card's Focus Value. Another Tactician's use is unchanged.
2. **Ikoma Ayumu - exp**: the scan's "is an Open action for you if you target Ayumu with it" is read as Ayumu performing Tactical Advantage: it is then a Battle or Open action (Battle as printed, and Open in the Action Phase, in either player's segment), so P19's `Battle/Open` timing. "Will not gain Fallen or Shadowlands" is a P10 block while he is in play; a printed keyword is not removed.
3. **Doji Hakuseki - exp3**: "another Personality" excludes Hakuseki himself, so his own Proclaim gains nothing from his trait; each Hakuseki the Proclaiming player controls in play gains 1 Honor. "If the duel was against The Dark Naga" is read as either duelist being titled The Dark Naga; the loser is bowed first, then destroyed.
4. **Mirumoto Kalen - exp**: "Ranged 3 Attack" is a fixed strength of 3 (the existing Ranged Attack machinery); the additional action is granted after the first ability's Attack resolves, whether or not it hit, and only his second printed ability (`w740-kalen-attack2`, which bows him as its cost) may use it. As for every additional action, taking any other action forfeits it.
5. **Seppun Ryota**: "Before an action resolves that targeted your Personality at Ryota's location: He enters any duel created by its effects instead of the target" is P18's substitute duelist: it is offered against another player's challenge-issuing action that targets one of Ryota's controller's Personalities standing where Ryota does, and Ryota (not himself the target, and legal against the challenge) is substituted.
6. **Yoritomo Harumi**: the waiver is hers, once per battle, and is spent only when she needs it (bowed, or with no enemy army at the battlefield); an Equip (Battle) she performs unbowed and opposed spends nothing. Her Battle action's "Force is now 0" is judged after the -5F; "you control Yoritomo Hiromi" is a copy in play under your control (checked by name). The destruction is the action's effect (negatable like any); "If it is still in play" is the target after the destruction step, and the additional Battle action is the ordinary unscoped grant. "Enemy" is a card another player controls; a Personality with any Follower attached is not a target, while any enemy Follower attached to anyone is.
7. **Frontier Farmer**: "You may Equip this Follower from your discard pile" is the ordinary Equip (Limited) from the owner's discard pile (P19): the cost is paid, the timing is Equip's, and it does not count as attached from a hand (so the hand-only triggers skip it).
8. **Fortune's Charm**: "Ranged and Melee Attacks targeting this Personality" is its wielder (the Item's host), as an Item's default scope. The Focus Effect's "the duel's winner may take an additional action" is the winner's controller's additional action (either player's), a tie gives none. "Before another player's card destroys this Personality during the Action Phase" is read as a destruction by an action's effects of a card another player controls while the Phase is the Action Phase (a battle's own resolution is not "during the Action Phase" here); it is negated once and the Item is destroyed, and a Personality whose Charm has gone is no longer protected.
9. **Focus on the Flame**: "This Shugenja" is the Spell's host, who must be at the battlefield as for any Battle action; Fire is judged from his keywords when the duel is issued and while it lasts, and Duelist is his for that duel only. The Spell is destroyed after the duel's outcome even when he lost or tied.
10. **A Magistrate Falls**: "unless exactly one of the targets is Fallen or The Dark Naga" is judged when the challenge is issued from the two Personalities then (Fallen as a keyword or a Clan alignment); the challenged player may refuse unless exactly one is. The 2 Gold is the text's fixed cost. The target may be any Personality other than the challenger (an own Personality included).
11. **Demonstrating Technique**: "Each Personality must focus exactly once, if possible" is P18's `ExactFocusEach: 1`; "the winner draws a card" is the controller of the winning Personality; a tie has no winner and both Personalities are dishonored (each is a loser).
12. **Practicing Kata**: the timing grant exists while the Kata is in play (the Zone.Home of a Kata) and goes when it leaves, including when another Kata is put into play; the once-per-turn use is spent when an Equip is taken with it and is not spent when the Equip's ordinary Limited timing already allowed it.
13. **Harbingers of War**: "base Gold Cost" is the printed Gold Cost (1 to 4). The grant is game-long and the controller's, covers equipping a Follower or Item from the hand (a Spell has its own Equip (Battle)), and the draw follows the first such equip each turn whatever timing it used; an equip from the discard pile (Frontier Farmer) does not draw.
14. **Hantei's Guidance**: "has Absent for you" and "may target any Personality with it" are the controller's while the Celestial is in play. The Political Interrupt is offered against any held Political action, the controller's own included, whether or not the action names a card; its cost (discard the Celestial) is paid when it is used and the held action is negated whole.
15. **The Empire's Foe Exposed** is unpublished (see above); its reading ("exactly one of which is yours" with "the other Personality" as the challenged one) is for the wave that builds the reveal-time duel.
