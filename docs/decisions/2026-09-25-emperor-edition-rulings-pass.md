# Emperor Edition rulings pass: Mendon's answers to the review of PR #493 (issue #494)

Mendon answered the open questions of `2026-09-25-emperor-edition-remaining-cards-pass.md`. This change applies every ruling, across five parallel groups (R1-R5) plus two follow-up groups of rulings, and wires the cards the rulings unblocked. Only **3 Emperor Edition cards** are now unwired (Plains of Otosan Uchi, No Hiding Place, Rend the Soul); `docs/CARD_INVENTORY.xlsx`: 2183 rows, 1252 wired. Art pack `catalogue-art-pr494-20260925-r2` (3699 images).

## Rulings applied

**Battles without a province (R1).** A battlefield may have no province (declared without one, or its province was destroyed before engagement, e.g. two fronts). It resolves normally, destroys no province and triggers no "destroys a province" effect; it has no adjacent battlefields. The declarer of the Attack Phase is the Attacker unless a card says otherwise (Retribution's player declares an extra phase at a province; Chikushudo's Trickery names the Attacker; Surprise Attack keeps the current Attacker and Defender). Built as additional battlefields numbered from 100 (`MatchEngine.FirstAdditionalBattlefield`), `MatchView.Battlefields`, a client lane for them, and a section in `docs/rules/EMPEROR_EDITION_CORE_RULES.md` quoting the ruling. Wired: Surprise Attack (strategy-wave-62), Chikushudo's Trickery (spell-wave-25); also wired and, with Mendon's scans, published in strategy-wave-64: Shinobi Assault, Two Fronts, The Perfect Moment (Aramasu's Legacy still needs more, see its blocker note). The Perfect Moment lets Paragons and Battle Maidens assign and move; every other Personality of its player may not assign and does not move during the additional Attack Phase.

**The shared delay mechanism (R2).** Effects a delaying card holds back are recorded per card (`MatchState.DelayedEffects`, application order) and applied at their trigger point: end of battle, end of phase, end of turn, the next Dynasty Phase, the End Phase. Each is re-validated when its turn comes: an effect that can no longer be applied (move home from home, move to a battlefield when not at one) is negated; destroy, dishonor and bow apply from any location; an effect on a card that left play is negated. Spirit of the Berserker, In the Heart of Battle and Deadly Orders now run through the store. Wired: Halls of the Forgotten (stronghold-wave-20), The Honor of the Lion, The Loyalty of the Scorpion, The Compassion of the Unicorn, The Courage of the Mantis, The Honesty of the Phoenix (strategy-wave-63) and The Strength of the Spider's Focus Effect. Journey's End Keep's printed "will not be negated or delayed" is now honoured.

**Durations (R3).** Effects are permanent only if the text says so; province strength effects last until end of turn, and a Region's effect on a province is passive "while attached". Only Willing Spirits, Boastful Proclamation and Debt to the Kaiu are permanent (an audit test pins them). Changed to until end of turn: Ring of Earth, Tamori Kusugi, Burn the Towers, Besieged, Yoritomo's Guidance, Improved Defenses, Expensive Achievement, Siege Volley, Genji's Students, Indomitable Home, Stone-Hewed Shrine, Scavenging Party, Yogo Adi, The Second City, Courtesy of the Crane. Togashi Hizumi's Battle half, wrongly unwired, is now wired. Yoritomo's Guidance is a trait: it fires at every battle any Mantis player attacks. The Sincerity of the Dragon follows the corrected scan (Dragon Clan player, removed from the game even if its action is negated). Kaiu Village is wired with the software-only created Kaiu Weapon (`oracle-l5r-10006`, a local id; region-wave-10, item-wave-16).

**Honor and reactions (R4).** Rhetoric and Solid Defense apply to any Honor gain or loss caused by an action's effects (`GainHonor` opens a `honorChangeReaction` window; the label guess is gone). No published card prints Honor as a cost before the colon; the audit is below. Cherry Blossom Festival bars Reactions including Proclaim, not Overconfident and not the 24 trait-modelled abilities (`TraitReactions`, audited against all 248 Reaction-timed abilities). Luxurious Silk does not reduce the Dynasty recruit or a recruit that pays all costs normally, but does reduce an action whose cost is a Personality's Gold Cost and whose effect recruits him. The Killing Grounds fires once per player. Bad Kharma and Footsteps of Madness may be played at any time, on top of other modifiers; a Focus Effect is not an action. **Ordered searches:** a card in the first named location must be taken from there (the searcher chooses among that location's qualifying cards and never sees later locations); only if the first has none may the searcher search the next; a searched deck is always reshuffled. **Deception Revealed:** only Rings bowed by an action (Limited, Open, Battle, Reaction), as a cost or an effect, qualify, not Focus Effect bows.

**Clans, units, Terrain, choices (R5).** There is no Ninja clan: Ninja is only a keyword; 16 published cards were corrected (Goju Oyoto, Ninube Onchi (all versions), Ninube Shiho and four other Goju cards to Spider; Shosuro Konishi, Tsuji, Chikata, Hawado exp2, Bayushi Hurunayi, Mitsuo, Nitoshi, Amorie and Shosuro Nobu to Scorpion). Ki-Chang's Reaction is only for another Personality entering play. He's Mine! (and Bayushi Irezu, same shape) act only in an accepted or mandatory duel. Whole-unit bow/straighten bows every card of the unit individually, honouring per-card prevention (fixed: Fortitude, Barred Passage, Hard Pressed, Block Supply Lines, The Conqueror). A Terrain can never be bowed or straightened. Sasumata's "enemy card" is any card the opponent controls at the battlefield, attachments themselves included. "May compare" clauses (Unseen Assailant, Tsuruchi House Guard, Asako Nobunori) are the player's choice (`GameCommand.Choice`); an Attack without a chosen substitute compares Force.

## Readings still to confirm (new)

**R1.** Chikushudo's Trickery: the target attacks and the Spirits defend; the target is shielded from destruction only until the resolution begins; the Defender holds the first opportunity. Surprise Attack's target is "there or at home"; its battlefield is fought later in the Attacker's battle order. Shinobi Assault: the 5 Honor is lost after the battle. The Perfect Moment: "will not move" covers every movement of its player's Personalities that phase but not the automatic return home. Two Fronts needs the targeted player to have two or more provinces.

**R2.** "The effects of losing this duel" are the duel outcome's effects on the delaying player's own losing Personality and his unit; a winner is never asked and other Focus Effects resolve as usual. The Honesty of the Phoenix follows the corrected scan Mendon supplied (Phoenix Clan player condition; removed from the game even if the action is negated). "Another unit" on Compassion means a Personality other than the performer; The Loyalty of the Scorpion's performer may be the target himself. "Until the end of the turn" is applied at the top of `EndTurn`. A delayed Block Supply Lines records only the move home: the bow belongs to the move ("as he moves"), so a negated move bows nothing.

**R3.** Kaiu Village's "your performing Personality" is the Personality that entered; Overlay recruits count as entering from a province (no test of their own).

**R4.** Solid Defense and Rhetoric are offered on gains as well as losses (per "any honor gain or loss"). Any Honor change during a running action's resolution counts as from that action, including a trait triggered inside it. Unimpeachable Name is also playable at the Honor-loss moment. A Ring bowed as the cost of a negated action is not recorded (a negated action opens no after-action windows). A replayed command a window's Reactions made illegal is cancelled with an `actionCancelled` event. Enslaved Djinn, Celestial Sword of the Dragon - exp and Asako Chukage: blocker notes refreshed (a Ring put-into-play search effect and the hand as a searchable place are missing).

**R5.** He's Mine!/Irezu do not re-check the original action's target restriction for the substitute. Block Supply Lines bows the unit only if the move home happened (confirmed by Mendon). The reference library (`library.json`, catalogue JSONs) still lists "Ninja" among the source `clans` of the corrected cards; it is reference data and was left alone.
