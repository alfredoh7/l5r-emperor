# Battle Phase rulings, round 2: the Declaration Segment, one ordered before-resolution set, Reactions are actions, Rebuilding Kyuden Hida, the deferred Attack's source card

Status: accepted for implementation; each ruling is recorded below as confirmed by the rules lead (Mendon), with how it is implemented, what it changed, and what is still open.
Lane: rules (shared: new legal-action shapes, one new phase, new public view members the client must render). Issue: #354. Builds on `2026-09-18-battle-timing-primitives.md` (#310/#324) and `2026-09-18-recon-action-tag-and-daidoji-ebizo.md` (#321). UI counterpart: #311 (battle sidebar; commented).

## 1. Declaration: "Declining to attack ends the attack phase. An attack is either declared or not (and then, if declared, there is a battlefield at every province regardless of whether or not there are any assigned attackers/defenders)."

**Confirmed and implemented.** Declaring or declining is ONE explicit decision by the Attacker, and the phase machine has a step for it.

- **Phases.** `Phase.AttackDeclaration` is now the Declaration Segment (it used to be the Attacker's Infantry assignment prompt, whose empty assignment was the only way to decline). A new `Phase.InfantryDeclaration` is the Attacker's Infantry Maneuvers step, named for its Cavalry twin (`CavalryDeclaration`). `DefenseAssignment`, `CavalryDeclaration`, `CavalryDefense`, `BattleSelection`, `Battle` are unchanged. `PrintedPhases` (the six printed phases) counts the new phase inside the Attack Phase.
- **Commands.** In the Declaration Segment the engine offers `declareAttack` ("Declare an attack") and `pass` ("Decline to attack"); nothing else, and no assignment. `declareAttack` is disabled with a reason while Utaku Chikako's "may not declare an attack this turn" holds (the check moved from the assignment to the declaration itself; the assignment still refuses, defensively). Only the Attacker declares, only in that phase.
- **Decline** (`pass`): `DeclineAttackPhase` -- the Attack Phase ends at once. No Maneuvers, no defender prompt, no battlefield; `SetPhase(Dynasty)` as before, so Wave Man's "after an Attack Phase ends" still fires there. Event `attackDeclined`.
- **Declare**: `DeclareAttackPhase` creates a battlefield (`UnresolvedBattlefields`) at EVERY one of the Defender's provinces still standing, before anyone is assigned (a destroyed province is gone -- core rules: the row closes up). Event `attackDeclared` (once per attack; it used to be emitted per Maneuvers step, so the client's battle journal now resets once, at the declaration). Then Infantry Maneuvers (Attacker, `attack` with any number of units, possibly none; Defender `assignDefense`), Cavalry Maneuvers, and always Battle Selection: assignments place units at battlefields that already exist and no longer decide whether one does. Consequently **the Defender may assign defenders to any battlefield, attacked or not**, and **every battlefield is fought exactly once, including one with no unit on either side** (core rules, Fight Battles: "even a battlefield with zero units on both sides still has its one battle"): the two passes resolve it as a battle with no outcome (0 v 0), nothing changes. A battlefield with only defenders is a battle the Attacker cannot act in (Rule of Presence), which the Defender wins with nothing destroyed.
- **BattleStepView.** New constants `BattleSteps.Declaration` (segment) and `BattleSteps.DeclareOrDecline` (step): `Declaration/DeclareOrDecline`, decision player = the Attacker; then `InfantryManeuvers/AttackerAssigns` (which `AttackDeclaration` used to report); after a decline the view reads `None` again. Public, identical for both viewers, a plain constant so an older client that does not know it shows nothing wrong.
- **AI.** `TrainingOpponent` scores `declareAttack` with the old per-maneuver test applied to its whole unbowed home army against the weakest province (declare when the army beats that province plus the defence, otherwise pass); a bot with nothing to attack with declines. Its Maneuvers scoring is unchanged. AI-vs-AI matches still end in military victories.
- **Client.** Compiles and follows the new phase (`InfantryDeclaration` in the battle-active lists, the phase track, the tracker, the journal, the phase guide/help text, and `declareAttack` rendered with the other phase buttons). No visual design was done: the Declaration step is a plain button pair ("Declare an attack" / "Decline to attack") for Alfredo to design (#311).

*Changed existing behaviour.* A declined attack takes one decision (was up to four empty passes). A declared attack against a board where the attacker committed to one province now offers all standing provinces to the defender and fights all of them (up to four battles per attack; the empty ones are two passes each) -- a much longer flow for the same attack. The practice client's auto-pass helper and Alfredo's sidebar may want to fast-forward vacuous battles; the rules lead ruled that they are NOT skipped (ruling 6 below), so the engine offers every battlefield and pass as it always did.

*Existing tests changed, and why* (the old flow no longer exists): core `Priority` (declare first; four battlefields now stand instead of "straight to Dynasty"), `CavalryManeuversSegment`, `DaigotsuKendoConquerorAndIkomaNatsuCavalry`, `BrashForceRedirectAndBowReactions` (each: a `declareAttack` step before the Attacker's assignment), `UtakuChikako` (the restriction now refuses `declareAttack`; declining ends the phase), `FollowerWave11Primitives` (Wave Man's declined-attack path is the Declaration pass), `CompleteMatch` (the scripted match declares before it assigns); published `CavalryAfterEmptyInfantry` and `Replay` (the scripted full game declares; it still reaches a winner and replays deterministically, so the transcript replay needed no regeneration -- there is no stored transcript, it is generated and compared with itself), `DaigotsuBofana/SixPrintedPhases` and `RealTurnAcrossPhases`, `attach-from-discard/CavalryGrantIsLive`, `either-or/HesitationScope`, `battle-timing/StepViewSegments`. AI tests: one added (declines with nothing to attack with) and every full match now asserts a military victory has a declared attack. Transport tests: `BattleStepView` round trip extended with the Declaration constants and the new member.

## 2. Before-resolution effects: "resolve in the order decided by the active player ... after both players have passed consecutively but before army forces are tallied and compared."

**Verified, then generalized.** `PendingBeforeBattleResolve` already was ONE set ordered by the active player (`AdvanceBeforeBattleResolve`: the turn player chooses among the remaining items via `beforeBattleOrder`, the last one resolves directly, each reads the battlefield live, all before `ContinueResolveBattle` reads Force). There is no alternating reaction round and none was added. What was missing was two kinds of member; the set now holds all of these (`BeforeResolution.cs`):

| Kind | Item id | Who is asked | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| a card's trait (mandatory or "you may") | the card id | its controller for an optional one | unchanged (Encircled Terrain and the fixtures) |
| a **delayed effect** registered by a Battle action ("Before this battle resolves, bow him") | `delayed:N` | nobody (mandatory) | new primitive |
| a **printed "Reaction: Before a battle resolves"** | `reaction:CARD` | its OWN controller, who uses it or passes | new primitive |

- **Delayed effects.** `RegisterDelayedBattleEffect(player, source, kind, target[, stat, amount])` from any ability's execution; kinds `bow`, `straighten`, `destroy`, `modifier`. Stored in `MatchState.DelayedBattleEffects` (plain data, `DelayedBattleEffect`), announced (`delayedEffectRegistered`), gathered into the set when both players have passed, applied at their turn (skipped when the target left play; Stalwart still negates a bow), and **expiring with their battle**: `FinishBattleResolution` clears the list, applied or not (an effect registered too late to join the set never carries into the next battle -- tested by planting one mid-resolution). Fixtures: a Personality whose Battle actions register a delayed bow / +2F.
- **Reactions.** A Reaction of Trigger `before-battle-resolves` joins the set when its owner could use it right now (asked with the window open, exactly as they will be: a Strategy in hand, or a card in play), so the window opens only when a Reaction is worth asking about and an unusable one never prompts. When its turn comes it is asked again (an earlier effect may have removed its performer) and, if still usable, opens `beforeBattleReaction` for its owner: the engine offers the plays (`useAbility` with the performer choices, validated like any action -- a Reaction is an action, ruling 3) and `declineReaction`; using or declining spends the item. A Reaction that itself opens a window (a before-action window on its target, say) suspends this one and is finished when it returns (`FinishSpentBeforeBattleItem`, run from `Apply`).
- **Steady Resolve and Akodo Dairuko.** The two printed abilities are built and proven through training cards that carry them: `steady-resolve-boost` (a new generic performer category, "your performing opposed [keyword] Personality at the current battlefield") and `dairuko-rally` (Tireless: usable while bowed; a per-battle flag `ResolutionWillNotBowPlayerIds` that `FinishBattleResolution` reads, so the promised army's resolution does not bow it, only for that battle and that player). **Not published**: the real cards need a strategy wave and a personality wave -- packaging with scan verification and shipped art, which needs the printings downloaded (an action that needs the rules lead's leave). `BLOCKERS` says exactly that for both. Dairuko's Melee 8 Attack is also still to add.
- **View.** `BattleStepView.BeforeResolutionSourceIds` now holds item ids (a card id, `delayed:N` or `reaction:CARD`) and a new optional trailing member `BeforeResolutionEffects` (`Id`, `Kind` Trait/Delayed/Reaction, `PlayerId`, `CardId`, `Label`) lets a client name each without knowing the id scheme. `beforeBattleOrder` options carry the label; a delayed item has no `cardId`.
- **Tests** (`battle-rulings-2`): ONE set holds a trait, a delayed effect and a Reaction; the active player's order changes who wins (ordered delayed boost, Reaction, trait: Attacker 6 v 5 wins; trait first: the Attacker's army is sent home before the boosts and the Reaction has no performer, the Defender wins); every effect precedes the Force line in the event stream; only the active player orders; the Reaction's owner (not the active player) answers, both players' Reactions in one set; an unusable Reaction opens nothing; delayed effect lifecycle and expiry; Dairuko's flag, its Tireless use, its scope (own army, one battle).

## 3. Reactions are actions, engine-wide: "Reactions count as actions (they follow the same flow in the rulebook). Thus, a Recon Reaction that resolves in the Engage window contributes a force bonus ... Yes [Ebizo's +1F lasts until end of turn and stacks]."

**Confirmed.** One predicate, `IsAction(AbilityDefinition)` / `IsAction(GameCommand)` in `ReactionsAreActions.cs`: an ability is an action when its Timing is Limited, Open, Battle, Battle/Open or Reaction (a trait, a Focus Effect and a before-resolution trait are not). Every place that asked "is this a Reaction, so not an action?" asks it.

**What the rules reference says** (`docs/rules/EMPEROR_EDITION_CORE_RULES.md`): "When can an action be taken?" lists Limited, Open, Battle **and Reaction actions**; "If multiple Reactions are eligible on the same trigger, resolve them in an action round of their own"; the Coils of Madness supplement says Engage plays exactly like a Reaction and Interrupt plays exactly like a Reaction to "Before an action's effects resolve" ("Both the action itself and its printed form are called 'actions'"). Nothing there makes a Reaction consume, or count as, a Battle-action opportunity or a pass; a Reaction is played on its trigger in a window that keeps its own alternation. **So window and priority behaviour is unchanged**: a Reaction played in a window hands the opportunity to the other player and resets that window's pass count, two passes close it, and it neither is nor consumes a pass of the Combat Segment.

**The audit.** Every `Timing == "Reaction"` guard in the engine was read. Findings:

| Place | Treated a Reaction as not an action? | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Before-action windows (`BeforeActionReactionApplies`: Kakihara, Rokai, Tamoko, Champion of Thunder, Brothers of Goemon, Yoritomo Sakuma, fixtures) | **Yes** (the Akodo Kakihara work: "a Reaction is not an action", copied onward) | a Reaction that names another player's card opens the window like any action |
| Daigotsu Bofana's once-per-phase "another player's action targets him" trait (`OpenForcedDiscardForTargetedTrait`) | **Yes** | a Reaction that targets him spends the trigger and asks for the discard |
| "After this action" windows (`OpenBowReactionWindow`: Togashi Hizumi, Tamori Ruya; `OpenMoveReactionWindow`: Legion of the Bat; `OpenNikutaiReactionWindow`) | **Yes** | opened after a Reaction resolves too (Stormless Fury's is a "Battle action" window: a Reaction is no Battle action, so it never applies) |
| The Engage window (first-action claims, Naval Invasion, Yuki) | **Yes** ("PendingChoice has no stack": the announce / before-action / Unimpeachable Name windows were skipped) | a claiming Reaction is an action other players may react to and negate; the claim is recorded only when it resolves |
| The targeted-by-action reactions of #341 (Ezuno's redirect, Blade of Champions, Yurishi, Jinn-Kuen, Mochitoko: `SubjectForAnnouncedAction`, `SubjectForAttack`, `TargetedActionReactionApplies`; merged while this branch was open) | **Yes** (each carried its own "a Reaction is not an action (no nested windows)" guard) | a Reaction that names their card is held, redirected, negated or re-checked like any action; tested with a Merchant guard in the Engage window |
| The "after the resolution of your [keyword] action" funnel (Ebizo, Master Scouts, Apprentice Shinobi) | No (docs said so; confirmed) | unchanged; confirmed for the Engage-window Recon Reaction |
| Negation (`NegateDeferredAction`, Sakuma, Champion's "all"), once-per-turn/phase accounting (`AbilityUses`, `TrySpendPhaseUse`), War Weariness, Additional Action bookkeeping | No | unchanged |
| Unfortunate Hesitation's forced pass | printed scope: "a Battle, Limited, or Open action" -- a Reaction is none of those three designators | unchanged, by the card's text |
| "May not perform actions" (action-lock, PR #348, not on main) | n/a | that PR should call `IsAction` and include Reactions ("can't use his own abilities or his attachments' abilities") |
| Additional Action grants (`ConsumeAdditionalActionGrant`) | grants are for "Battle" actions by definition | a Reaction no longer forfeits the player's pre-existing grants and never keeps priority for a burst: each action of a burst is atomic and independent (ruling 7 below; this row first read "forfeits, as an ordinary action", which the rules lead overruled) |
| Encircled Terrain, Reserve recruitment, `PlayedFromDiscard` | not actions / not about action-ness | unchanged |

**The mechanism a Reaction being an action needed.** A Reaction is only ever played inside a window that is waiting, and `PendingChoice` holds one window. A window that opens because of the Reaction (before-action, forced discard, Unimpeachable Name, "after this action") therefore **suspends** the one it was played in (`MatchState.SuspendedWindows`, plain serialized data: the window and the transient state that belongs to it) and gives it back, exactly as it was, afterwards: a Reaction that was set aside behind its own window carries on inside the window it was played in (so it queues what it queues for that window's close, as a Reaction played directly would); one that already resolved gets the window reopened with the opportunity handed to the other player. Tests prove the restore, the snapshot round trip and the queueing.

**One consequence to know:** an "after this action" window now opens only for a watcher whose once-per-turn use is unspent (the before-action and Engage windows already worked that way). Without it a Reaction that had just spent Ruya's use would reopen her window over itself. This also applies after non-Reaction actions (a spent watcher used to be offered a window with nothing to do but pass); no existing test depended on it.

**Behaviours that changed** (the report list): (a) a Reaction that targets Kakihara / Champion / Brothers / Rokai / Tamoko / Sakuma-protected cards opens their before-action window; (b) a Reaction that targets Bofana spends his trigger and asks for the discard; (c) Hizumi / Ruya / Legion / Nikutai react to a Reaction's bow / move; (d) the Engage window's Reactions are negatable and no longer skip the announce / before-action / Unimpeachable windows; (e) a spent watcher opens no after-action window; (f) `SuspendedWindows` exists. Nothing else.

**Existing tests changed:** `AkodoKakiharaReactsToNonAttackActions` (its "Ruya's Reaction names Kakihara" block asserted the old rule; it now asserts the window, the suspension, Kakihara's bonus, and the restore) and `daigotsu-bofana/OnlyAnotherPlayersActions` (its "a Reaction that names him" block likewise). Everything else passed unchanged.

**Engage-window Recon Reaction (the ruling's example):** confirmed with tests: Yuki's Recon Reaction resolving in the Engage window gives Ebizo and Master Scouts +1F, only their controller's, stacking with a later Recon action (+2F), until the end of the turn (a modifier cleared with the turn -- the rules lead's answer to the earlier open question 1 of the Recon document: end of turn, stackable).

## 4. Rebuilding Kyuden Hida (`oracle-l5r-6200`, the Event)

**The earlier relayed ruling, "Kyuden Hida does not have a forced pass", was about the Stronghold, not this Event. Corrected by the rules lead:** for the **Event** -- "the Attacker may not announce a Battle action before the Defender's first normal opportunity to act or pass in a battle" -- an Attacker who gains the first action while it is in effect **must pass**: the language bars "announcing", so the only other option is to stay silent. The `MustPass` limit built in #324 is therefore **correct and stays**; that pass IS a pass and counts toward the two consecutive passes, so a Defender who then passes ends the Combat Segment; the Defender's first normal opportunity follows; without a claim nothing changes. (My first reading of the relayed ruling removed it; that change was reverted before it landed.) `2026-09-18-battle-timing-primitives.md` decision 6 and open question 6 are updated to say so.

**Audit: Event vs Stronghold.** The catalogue has exactly two Kyuden Hida cards: the Event `oracle-l5r-6200` "Rebuilding Kyuden Hida" (wired in `event-wave-2`, effect `rebuilding-kyuden-hida`, Unique) and the Stronghold `oracle-l5r-4638` "Kyuden Hida - exp" ("Recon Reaction: After engaging: You have Reconnaissance at the current battlefield. Create and attach a 0F Scout Follower to each of one to three of your Personalities in your current army..." -- **not wired**). Every reference in the engine, tests, decision documents, `BLOCKERS` and the packaging script names the Event when it means the Event (`BattleTiming.cs`, `MatchEngine.cs` event resolution, `event-wave-2`, the two decision documents); the Stronghold appears only in the list of unwired Stronghold Recon Reactions. Nothing was mislabelled; nothing needed fixing. A test now pins the separation (the effect id belongs to the one Event card; no Stronghold carries it; the Stronghold is not in the catalogue).

**Pinned by tests:** for each way to hold the first opportunity -- a Sneak Attack shape claim, an attacker's Yuki, Naval Invasion -- and for either player controlling the Event: the Attacker holds the opportunity, may only pass (no Battle action, `playStrategy` or Reserve recruit is offered; `FirstActionLimit` is `MustPass`), the pass counts (`ConsecutivePasses` 1), the Defender's first normal opportunity follows, and the Defender's pass ends the battle actions; with no claim the flow is untouched.

**Naval, confirmed by the rules lead (nothing to change).** Naval Invasion is restricted to the first action being performed by a Naval Personality in the attacking army (Spells and Items count through their wielder; Strategies where the Naval Personality is named as the performer); passing stays legal; combined with Sneak Attack it is still one opportunity and passing is allowed. That is what #324 built.

## 5. Deferred Attacks keep the ability's source card

**Confirmed and implemented.** (The original text of the limit: the pending Attack kept the performer, not the ability's own card.) The inherited-keyword check for "was that a [keyword] action?" reads the card the ability is printed on. A deferred Ranged/Melee Attack (one that opened an attack reaction window) kept only the performing Personality, so an Item, Spell or Follower's keyword was not seen (and the performer's was, wrongly: an ability inherits the keywords of the card it is on, not of whoever performs it). The pending Attack's declaring command (`PendingAttack.Command`, added by #341 while this branch was open; its `CardId` is the source card) is what `ResolveAttack` now reads, and `PendingDuel.SourceCardId` (the same defect for a duel-issuing ability, set in `ExecuteAbilityEffects`, the one funnel every deferring action passes through) is what `ResolveDuel` reads. Tests: a Recon-keyword Strategy's duel is a Recon action and a plain Strategy's is not whatever its performer has (`DeferredDuelSource`); an Item, a Spell and a Follower carrying the Recon keyword on the same Attack ability deferred behind an attack reaction window each give Ebizo +1F when the Attack RESOLVES (not when announced) though the performer is not Recon; a Personality's own Recon keyword still counts; a Recon-keyword Personality wielding a plain weapon does NOT make a Recon action (the old code did); the synchronous path agrees.

## 6. Vacuous battles are not skipped; actions that move Personalities to a battle are Absent Battle actions (rules lead, 2026-09-19)

**Ruling.** "Allow players to pass": every battlefield stays fightable and passable, including one at which nobody has a unit (nothing is skipped, in the engine or the practice client). And: **any action that moves Personalities to a battle (the current battlefield) may be played as an Absent Battle action by EITHER player, without presence, in any battle including one with no units**; derive it from the ability's effect kind rather than another hand-kept id list; keep the `Absent` flag for explicitly Absent cards; an ability that says it may be performed without presence is treated the same.

**Implemented.**
- `AbilityDefinition.MovesToBattle` (default false), declared on the ability whose effect case moves a Personality to a battlefield. `ValidateAbility`'s presence block reads `ability.Absent || ability.MovesToBattle`; the old id list (`michi-move`, ...) is deleted. There is no declarative effect model in the engine (effects are cases of one switch), so the flag is the "effect kind"; **`BattleAbsentMovesTests.FlagMatchesEffectCases` scans the effect cases of `RunAbilityEffects`** (`MoveToBattlefield(`, `TryMoveToBattlefield(`, `BringProvincePersonalityIntoPlay(... CurrentBattleProvince ...)`) and fails when a case that moves a Personality to a battlefield is on no marked ability, or when a marked ability has no such case. A new move-to-battle ability therefore cannot ship without the flag.
- Everything else about such an action is unchanged: it is a Battle action (Battle phase only), keeps its own printed conditions (opposed / enemy units present / the Defender), its own target and performer rules, its Focus and once-per-turn use, and the target's own location rules (Return to Action's target is at home).
- Pass is not touched: it is offered to both players in every battle and two passes resolve it (`EmptyBattleIsStillFoughtAndPassable`).

**Every wired ability that moves Personalities to a battle** (the enumeration, from the effect cases):

| Ability (card) | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `michi-move` (Mirumoto Michi), `rikyou-move` (Togashi Rikyou), `takasho-move` (Takasho), `intervening-spirit-move`, `houken-exp-straighten` (Mirumoto Houken - exp), `sanctuary-of-the-waves-move`, `mai-move-force-penalty` (Mai), `your-clan-needs-you` (Your Clan Needs You!), `return-to-action-move` (Return to Action, printed Absent) | playable without presence (the old id list, or the `Absent` flag) | unchanged, now by the flag |
| `trusting-instinct-move` (Trusting Instinct) | needed a unit at the battlefield | playable without presence (still needs enemy units where he would be opposed) |
| `charge-into-danger-move` (Charge Into Danger) | needed presence (printed plain Battle) | playable without presence (still "assigned to, or would be opposed at") |
| `hawado-move-battlefield` (Shosuro Hawado) | needed presence | playable without presence (still a battlefield with enemy units) |
| `earth-flows-move` (The Earth Flows) | required its wielder at the battlefield, and had no Defender check in code | the wielder need not be there; **Defender only** is now enforced ("If you are the Defender": an Attacker is shown it disabled and rejected) |
| `munefusa-move` (Asahina Munefusa) | on the old list, though it moves an attacking Personality HOME, not to a battle | not a move to a battle; keeps its no-presence clause as an explicit `Absent: true` (see `2026-09-19-discipline-and-absent-flags.md`) |

Not on the flag: every move-HOME ability without an explicit no-presence clause (Overgrown Grove, Effortless Counterattack's move, Fubatsu Plate, Ring of Water's move-home, Incapacitated, Block Supply, ...) -- moving home is not moving to a battle, and each keeps the presence rule it had (`HomeMoveNeedsPresence`). `recruitReserve` is a command that was already an absent battle action. "The Way of the World" and the move-destination spec of PR #348 are not on main; when they land they need `MovesToBattle` (the scan test will say so).

**Tests** (`battle-absent-moves`): flag == scanned effect cases and the marked inventory pinned; an empty battle is fought and both players may pass; with no unit anywhere EITHER player (as Attacker or Defender) is offered, and plays, a move-to-battle action from hand through `QueryActions`, then the other player may too, and pass stays offered; a move-home fixture with no flag is still disabled and rejected without a unit and legal with one; every real marked ability is offered enabled to a player with no unit at the battlefield; The Earth Flows for Defender and Attacker. Fixtures: `training-move-to-battle` (marked) and `training-move-home` (unmarked) with Strategy cards.

## 7. Additional Action bursts and Reactions (rules lead, 2026-09-19)

**Ruling.** A Reaction played during a burst ends it ONLY if it negates the action that created the additional actions; otherwise it interrupts the burst, and the burst continues, the next additional action offered when the interruption is over ("each action ... should be treated as an atomic, independent action"). Unspent additional actions are lost only if the creating action is negated.

**How the engine models it.** A burst is the player's outstanding grants (`MatchState.AdditionalActions`). What used to end it: any action of that player that did not itself consume a grant (a forfeit of their pre-existing grants), an explicit pass, and a negated action (the negation tail forfeited all of the actor's grants). A Reaction counted as such an action (ruling 3 made it one), so a Reaction played in a window opened during the burst -- the holder's own Ruya, say -- ended it. Now (`ReactionsAreActions.ForfeitsAdditionalActions`, false for a Reaction):
- A Reaction never forfeits the player's grants: not when it resolves (`CompleteAbilityResolution`), not when it is negated (`ResolveNegatedAction`), not when its cost is blocked (`AbortActionForBlockedCost`), and Yoritomo Sakuma's reaction no longer forfeits before it asks its question.
- A Reaction never "keeps priority" for a burst: it is played inside a window that alternates on its own (`NextAction` stays false for it); the burst waits behind the window.
- Every window the holder's action opens (Hizumi / Ruya bow windows, Legion, Nikutai, Stormless Fury, the Overconfident / forced-discard queue) now closes back to the HOLDER while a burst is outstanding (it closed to the opponent, who then acted between two additional actions). The holder keeps priority when the last window closes, so the next additional action is offered and the opponent does not act in between.
- The creating action's negation needs no forfeiture: a negated action does not resolve, so the additional actions it would have granted are never created ("lost only if the creating action is negated"). This is why "a Reaction negating the creating action ends the burst" and "a non-negating one does not" fall out of one rule. An additional action negated by Effortless Counterattack loses only itself and what it would have created; the other outstanding grants stay, and priority goes back to the holder (it went to the Active player, wrong when the holder is the Defender).
- **`SuspendedWindows`**: a Reaction that opens a window of its own over the one it was played in (Ruya's bow opening Hizumi's) suspends and restores it exactly as before; the burst's grants are plain state outside the windows and survive the suspend/restore, and the snapshot round trip mid-burst continues identically (`SnapshotMidBurst`).
- Unchanged: an ordinary action outside the grant's scope (or not a Battle action) still ends the burst, and so does the holder's explicit pass in the Combat Segment; Again! (a new grant from an unscoped action) still keeps priority, Two Hands' restriction still blocks NEW grants only.
- **Limit, recorded.** An Attack (Ranged/Melee) that creates additional actions does not exist in the catalogue today; when one does, its deferred resolution (`ResolveAttack`) is the place that must create them after the Reaction window closes, as any creating action does.

**Fixtures.** `training-grant-bow` (Battle: bow a target enemy Personality at the battlefield, take one additional Battle action; card `training-grant-bower`).

**Tests** (`additional-action-bursts`): the baseline (grant created, holder keeps priority, next additional action offered); the holder's own non-negating Reaction leaves the grant, the window closes to the holder, and the next additional action is offered and taken; the opponent's non-negating Reaction likewise, control returning to the holder; a nested window (Ruya opening Hizumi's) through the whole chain; a Reaction negating the creating action (no grant, priority passes) against declining it (grant, holder keeps priority); a negated additional action loses only itself, with the holder as Defender to prove the burst hands priority back; an ordinary action outside a scoped grant and an explicit pass still end it; a negated Reaction does not forfeit; a snapshot mid-burst continues identically.
