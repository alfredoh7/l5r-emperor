# Duel introduction and focus cues

Accepted direction from Alfredo, 2026-09-16, issue #205 / PR #220. This supersedes the persistent DUEL masthead and extra on-mat stat numerals from earlier presentation iterations.

- Keep the large DUEL wordmark during the introductions, then fade it while returning to the playable mat. Skip and reduced motion dismiss it immediately.
- Position each introduced name above its own projected champion stack. Leave room for the full cards and separate the name from the central wordmark. Shade the opposing side with a soft gradient/vignette, blending sides as the camera changes subject.
- Use Satoshi Bold for YOUR MOVE and PLACE FOCUS. Keep the shared black lacquer menu and inspection typography unchanged.
- Make the legal focus destination visibly stronger on hover. A dragged card over the destination shows RELEASE TO FOCUS, a bright outline and stronger contrast without requiring an animated pulse. Reduced motion keeps the same feedback.
- Remove the extra stat numerals from the mat, including their former result placement. Original printed card faces remain intact. The winner stays visible and Duel details retains the authoritative final totals, stat contributions and effects.

Scope: presentation only. Legality, command submission, hidden-card access, duel calculations and results remain in the existing engine.
