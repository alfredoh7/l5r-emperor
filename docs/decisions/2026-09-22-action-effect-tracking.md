# Action-effect tracking (issue #437)

Requested by Mendon on 2026-09-22, as a follow-up to the grouped-blocker review of issue #431: evaluate the difficulty of a temporary game log tracking every action, its performer, target, costs and effects, for triggers to read and for players to review, then build it if viable.

**Result: extended the existing GameEvent/command-journal machinery rather than building a new logging subsystem.** `MatchState.Events` (a permanent, serialized, replayed `List<GameEvent>`) and the full command journal (`MatchEngine.ExportReplay()`) already cover "a log of everything that happened" and "export for player review" -- 259 `Emit(...)` call sites and a proven deterministic replay/journal-recovery story existed before this issue. What was missing was narrower: a *structured, queryable* record of what one resolved action affected, for a Reaction whose own trigger asks about it. Two pieces, both in `ActionEffectTracking.cs`:

- **`MatchState.LastResolvedAction`** (`ResolvedActionFacts`: `AbilityId`, `PlayerId`, `SourceId`, `Affected: List<AffectedCard>`) -- what the last fully-resolved action did. Recorded by `RecordActionEffect` at the handful of existing choke points that already narrate a destroy or move (`DestroyUnits`, `DestroyAttachment`, `MoveHome`, `MoveByRule`'s to-battlefield path -- see the file's own header comment for why each is gated or unconditional), snapshotted and cleared by `FinishActionEffectTracking` at the three points an action -- including its own reaction windows -- is truly done: `CompleteAbilityResolution`, `ResolveAttack`, `Dueling.cs`'s `FinishDuelResolution` (the same three `FireAfterOwnActionTraits` already uses). Never populated by the battle-resolution mass casualty sweep, which carries no acting player id -- that is "the battle's own resolution," not "a Battle action," the same distinction the printed cards below draw themselves.
- **A new Reaction trigger**, `AfterActionReactionTriggers.AfterActionResolved`, offered through the same alternating-window shape `AfterResolution.cs`'s own "after a battle's resolution ends" window already established (`ValidateAbility`'s trigger switch, `AbilityActions`' window-gating, `Apply`'s command routing, `QueryActions`' listing -- four small, exactly-mirrored additions, not new machinery). Each Reaction's own condition is a per-ability-id case in `AfterActionResolvedApplies`, the same shape `AfterResolutionApplies` already uses.

**What is NOT tracked: a bow.** Unlike destroy and move, nothing bows a card through one shared choke point today -- upward of a hundred call sites set `CardInstance.Bowed` directly, none narrated individually. Reading every one of them (or building a shared `Bow()` helper and migrating all of them) is its own, much larger task, out of scope for what was asked here ("a small structured field," "a contained change"). Hida Defenders (oracle-l5r-3083, "after your action bowed, destroyed, or moved an opposing Personality") stays blocked on exactly that piece, now that the destroyed/moved half of its own condition is otherwise expressible.

## Wired

- **Measure of Devotion (oracle-l5r-5013)**: "After the resolution of a Battle action that destroyed one of your Deathseekers: Create a 2F/2C/0PH Lion Clan Samurai Deathseeker Personality at the current battlefield. Gain 1 Honor." The created Personality is the Oathsworn Deathseeker (oracle-l5r-9710, a Proxy scan matching these exact stats/keywords was already fetched for this card and simply hadn't been checked against the merged catalogue -- the earlier note's "has a local scan" was correct and this pass confirms it), published alongside as a `CreatedOnly` Personality (personality-wave-48) the same way Farmlands Conscripts/Bird of Prey were.
- **Revenge (oracle-l5r-6302)**: "Destroy it if another player's action has destroyed one of your Personalities since the end of your last Battle action this battle." This needs more than the single `LastResolvedAction` slot -- a *history* bounded by two reset points, not just the most recent action -- so it reads a small derived flag instead, `MatchState.EnemyDestroyedOwnPersonalitySinceLastBattleAction` (per player): set true in `FinishActionEffectTracking` whenever another player's action destroys one of a player's own Personalities, cleared when that player's own Battle action next finishes resolving (the same point) and when a new battle begins (`SelectBattle`, "this battle" bounds the window).

## Not wired: the narrower remaining gap

- **Hida Defenders (oracle-l5r-3083)**: needs bow-tracking (see above).
- **Akodo Kobi - exp (oracle-l5r-232)**: his Reaction's own trigger -- "another player's Battle action destroyed one of your Lion Clan Personalities at the current battlefield" -- is now expressible (`LastActionDestroyedOwnKeyword` already generalizes to any keyword). Still needed: a "before the current turn ends" window (the Reaction is offered once, immediately, not held open until end of turn) and a Recruit of the dead Personality "ignoring costs and Honor Requirements" by its own original controller (`BringDeadPersonalityIntoPlay` exists for a different shape -- a dead Personality taken under the ACTING player's control, Goju Plot -- not verified as the same as a Recruit by the ORIGINAL controller).
