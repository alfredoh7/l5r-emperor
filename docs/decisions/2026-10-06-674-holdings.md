## Readings built in

1. **Bamboo Harvesters - exp2 ("Bow this card: Produce 0 Gold. Give this Holding a +1 Gold Production Wealth token.")** is a Gold source, so it is used inside a payment. Its printed production is 0, so the ordinary
   producers never list it; it is a payment source of its own (`<card>#bamboo`, the `#alt` family of Jade Mine) that produces the Gold its tokens add (the token is `Wealth`, Stat `GoldProduction` +1, so
   `ShadowsEmbraceBatch6Production` reads it), bows the card when the payment is made, and gives it the next token. A source that adds no Gold is never suggested (a suggestion never bows it for nothing), but the
   payer may name it, beside other sources, to feed the Holding. The printed "enters play bowed. (It straightens normally on your first turn.)" needs no code: the engine bows every Holding it puts into play and
   the experienced Bamboo's "will not straighten before your third turn" hold is not on this card. The setup slot (the second player's bowed Bamboo) is the existing deck-workshop slot; its wiring is unchanged.
   *To confirm:* that producing 0 Gold with no payment to make is not a stand-alone action (a Gold production is only used to pay).
2. **Border Keep - exp2: "Once or twice during your first turn (only)"** is `UsesPerTurn` 2 plus the first-turn rule of the inexperienced Border Keep (`player.TurnsTaken == 1`); the refresh itself is the existing
   province cycle (`CycleProvinces`): the named cards go to the bottom of the Dynasty deck and the provinces refill face-up.
3. **"If paying for a Holding"** (Border Keep - exp2, Damaged Port) is the existing `PayingForHolding` condition (a Holding recruited from a province; Yasuki Dokansuto's reading), not an action of a Holding in play.
   The scan has no "which may only pay for that card": Gold never carries over between payments, so it makes no difference.
4. **Exotic Market: "Bow this card: When paying for an Item, it enters play for 3 less Gold."** is read like Colonial Temple's attachment discount (holding-wave-50): a conditional production of 3 when the payment is
   an Equip of an Item (new condition `PayingForItem`, one enum line and one switch line in `ConditionalProduction.cs`), against its 2 for any other payment. It does not stack with the card's own 2 (one bow).
5. **Merchant Outpost: "bow this card if the target is not yours"** is two abilities that share a use (Moto Hailung's pattern): turn a face-down card in one of your own provinces face-up, or bow this card and do
   it to a face-down card in another player's province. Only a **face-down** card is a legal target (a face-up one has nothing to turn; the bow would buy nothing). Three out-of-play target categories were added
   (`wl-own-terrain-in-discard`, `wl-own-face-down-province-card`, `wl-enemy-face-down-province-card`); each needs one line in `ValidateAbility` and one in the candidate listing. As for Shosuro Hawado's province
   target, the listed ids are opaque; they name no card. *To confirm:* a bowed card takes no action, so a bowed Outpost cannot turn up even its own card.
6. **Green Lake Dojo: "Bow this card, destroy it if it is your turn, and target a Personality: Give him Cavalry."** is two abilities that share a use: on your own turn the cost is the bow and the destruction (so it
   is a cost, paid even if the action is negated), on another player's turn the bow only. Cavalry lasts until the end of the turn (R-001), as Fudoist Temple's.
7. **Fudoist Advisor: "Destroy a Madness token."** names no card, so the token is chosen by choosing the card that holds it: the target category lists the cards in play with at least one Madness token, and one
   token is destroyed. This means a card-targeted Reaction can see the choice as a target. *To confirm:* whether choosing the holder should count as a target.
8. **Fudoist Advisor and Productive Mine's parenthetical** ("Limited, 2 Gold: Discard a Kharmic card from your Province and refill it face-up") is the Kharmic keyword's own repeatable action; the keyword is on
   the card and nothing else was added. (Productive Mine prints "Repeatable Limited"; Fudoist Advisor "Limited"; both are only reminders of the one keyword action.)
9. **Hero's Memorial: "After another player's action resolves which destroyed one of your Personalities"** is the after-action-resolved window (`LastActionDestroyedOwnPersonality`, the shape of Akodo Kobi - exp
   without its Battle or Clan narrowing): the action must be another player's, and a destruction by a battle's own resolution is not an action. It needs one line in `AfterActionResolvedApplies`.
10. **Questionable Merchant: "Move a target attacking Personality home. Destroy this Holding."** both sentences are effects (there is no colon before them, so the Holding is not a cost). The target is any
    attacking Personality at the battle (Shibata's category, which the controller of the Holding will choose from the other player's army); the Holding is destroyed as the action resolves.
11. **Imperial Explorer's Dojo** puts a Terrain from your **Fate discard pile** into your hand and destroys the Holding as part of its effect; the bow is its cost.
12. **Recruitment Station: "Battle/Open, [Gold], bow: Equip a Follower, paying 2 less Gold."** The Gold box is the Equip's own cost (Chiboshi's and Kurou's `:gstar:`): the Follower comes from your hand to your
    performing Personality (at the battle in a Battle; any of yours as an Open), at its cost less 2 (never below 0). There is no printed limit but the bow, so the use limit is unlimited.
13. **Temple of Tengen: "Draw a card. Discard a card."** draws, then the player owes a forced discard (the generic "discard a card" window); a player with an empty hand owes nothing.
14. **The Forgotten Bay Dojo: "a Follower with a Gold Cost of 1 or more"** reads the Follower's current Gold Cost (the scan says "a Gold Cost", the scraped catalogue text says "printed"). *To confirm.* "Raise its
    Force to 4" is Visage of the Orochi's delta-to-a-value shape: a Follower already at 4 or more is unchanged, never lowered.
15. **Glassworks: "Your Items have +1F."** an Item's Force conveys to its wielder whether or not it is bowed; the term adds 1 for each Item attached to a Personality, for each Glassworks its controller has in
    play (Singular keeps a second copy out, so it is normally one). The Item itself shows no change on the card face; the wielder's Force does.
16. **Silk Works** names the dishonorable Personality of either player; "his controller" is its owner (the control-transfer rules of #CONTROL are not built). Hero's Memorial and Glassworks are Singular, which the
    engine already treats like Unique when buying.
