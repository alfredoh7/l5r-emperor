## Readings built in (for Mendon to confirm or correct)

1. **Infantry** (Yoshie): "Infantry" is "not Cavalry", the convention Mirumoto Hatsuto already uses, so Yoshie reuses `hatsuto-weaken` and "Infantry Personality or Follower" means an enemy Personality or Follower without the Cavalry keyword.
2. **Rukia's "Bow ... Destroy it if it is an attachment"** is Hiroto's "bow it (destroy it instead if it is an attachment)": an attachment is destroyed (its bow would be moot); anything else is bowed. A Stalwart-style negation of the bow therefore cannot save an attachment from the destruction.
3. **Chokichi's "if Chokichi has a Follower"** is a condition of the *second* target alternative, read when the action is chosen. A "Spider Clan Personality" may be of either side (the text says "a target", not "enemy") and may have attachments; it is recognised by Spider Clan alignment or the Spider Clan keyword.
4. **Kodanshi**: the two targets may be the same Personality (the card says "your target ... Ninja" and "a target Personality", with no "another"); each bow is its own effect (a negation of one leaves the other), and the "will not straighten this turn" sentence applies when the second target is Fudo or Fallen whether or not his bow happened (the whole turn: `StraightenLockedTurn`, as Basic Lesson).
5. **Nobukai's "If it is your turn or you control a Courtier"** is a condition of use: with neither true the Open action is not offered or accepted. "Your turn" is the active player's turn; a Courtier is any Courtier Personality you control in play (including one at Home).
6. **Hiromi** may pay 0 Gold (a strength-0 Attack, destroying nothing), as Celestial Sword of the Mantis may; the strength is exactly the Gold paid, with the usual player-scoped Attack bonuses added once.
7. **Invest** (Nigawa, Sayaka) is optional and made once as the card enters play; the tokens are Invest tokens (+1F each), as for Clay Kshatriya.
8. **Katagi's** Equip is a Battle action and needs Katagi at the current battlefield; the Weapon's printed Equip cost, with every applicable discount, is paid through the Gold flow.

## Not covered by a dedicated test

The Expendable draw on destruction and the Destined draw on entry are the engine's existing keyword effects (pinned by their own suites); here only the published keywords (and, for Destined, the entry draw of Yoshie and Takashi) are asserted.
