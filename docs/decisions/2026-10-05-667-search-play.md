# Blocked Promotional-Emperor cards, cluster "search-play" (#667)

Status: accepted as engineering readings built to Mendon's confirmed rulings; the points under "Readings built in" are for Mendon to confirm or overrule. Lane: rules (engine); no client change.

Six of the cards blocked by #653 are wired: **Gozu** (`oracle-l5r-2887`), **Hyobuko** (`oracle-l5r-3534`) and **Masaya** (`oracle-l5r-4853`) in `personality-wave-125`; **Heart of Fudo - exp2** (`oracle-l5r-10523`), **The Sacred Rosary of the Constrictors** (`oracle-l5r-9982`) and **The Topaz Armor - exp** (`oracle-l5r-9980`) in `item-wave-43`. Each was read against its own scan (Gozu, the Rosary and the Armor: Promotional-Emperor; Hyobuko and Masaya: Promotional-Celestial; Heart of Fudo - exp2: Coils of Madness). Engine: `src/L5R.Core/SearchPlay*.cs`; tests: `SearchPlayTests.cs`, `SearchPlayCardsTests.cs`.

## Mendon's rulings, as built

- **Gozu.** "Once the optional action of discarding a card is selected, the terrain play is mandatory and the player may not pass; if there is at least one terrain, they must select a terrain and then it is played." The discard is the second way of taking the action (Ikoma Shika - exp's two-abilities-one-use shape, the discarded card riding on the command). The search must take a Terrain when the Fate deck holds one (a pass is legal only when none qualifies); it is shown, goes to the hand, the deck is shuffled, and the engine then asks its own action list (`QueryActions`) whether the Terrain could be played now. If it could, a **mandatory** additional Battle action scoped to it is granted and the play is **owed** (`MatchState.SearchPlayOwes`): the pass, "Pass all battles", the decline of the additional action and every other action are refused and listed disabled with the reason until the card is played. If it could not (a Terrain already at the battlefield, no unit there, no Gold, a lock on additional actions), the card is discarded, as printed.
- **Masaya.** "A Reserve-like ability that allows the player to recruit him during the Action Phase as an Open action." Rise of the Dark Naga's province recruit (target: the face-up province card; shared entry sequence, entry windows, province refill) taken by the card itself, at its ordinary recruit price less 2 Gold (never below 0). An Open action is an Action Phase action (R-030), so it is not offered in the Battle Phase.
- **The Sacred Rosary.** "A search effect that puts a Celestial into play with no province, plus remember which of the two places the card came from to remove the Rosary." `SearchEffect` `sp-celestial-into-play` enters the Celestial through the one shared entry (`EnterCelestialPlay`, extracted from `ResolveCelestial`, which still refills the province); the place searched is the search's own `Deck`, and a Dynasty discard-pile source removes the Rosary from the game.
- **The Topaz Armor.** "A list of keywords applied like the 'Spirit of...' cards; when it leaves play (discarded, returned to hand or deck) it loses the selected keywords." The keywords are granted with `GrantKeyword(..., GrantDuration.Attachment)`, which every leave-play path already removes; they also follow a Transfer. The choice is a trait (mandatory), and a re-entry chooses a fresh pair.

## Readings built in (not confirmed rulings)

- **Heart of Fudo - exp2:** "If this Personality is unbowed" conditions the search only; the bow and the destruction are printed as separate sentences, so a bowed wielder may still use the Item (it then only destroys it). The wielder's bowed state is read as the action resolves. The three steps are applied bow, destroy, then search (the order does not change what any of them does).
- **Hyobuko, attach:** "Attaches Heart of Fudo ignoring Gold cost" is the attach price of a Heart of Fudo (either version, compared by base title) that goes to Hyobuko himself (Quelsa's "attaches Spells paying 2 less Gold" shape); it does not let him attach it to another Personality for free. **Hyobuko, search:** an ordered search (R-011) of the Fate discard pile, then the Fate deck, matching the printed title "Heart of Fudo" (both published versions have that name). "Discard Hyobuko" is a cost (a Personality discarded from play: his unit leaves play with him, his attachments go to their owners' Fate discard piles, and he goes to the Dynasty discard pile, not the dead pile, so no destruction Reaction or dishonorable-death Honor loss). "Singular" is only a keyword; no engine effect is invented for it.
- **Masaya, the return:** "Before the turn ends, put him on top of your Dynasty deck" is paid at the top of EndTurn (beside the other turn-end checks). If Masaya has left play by then (destroyed, discarded, sent to a deck) nothing is put on top and the debt is dropped (most conservative reading). His unit leaves with him as Ninube Shiho's does: attachments go on top of their owners' Fate decks.
- **The Sacred Rosary:** the discard of "all your Celestials in play" is the action's first step and happens even if no Celestial is then found; they are in the Dynasty discard pile the search may read. A search that finds nothing ("searched your discard pile for it" with no card taken) removes nothing: the Rosary leaves the game only when a Celestial was taken from the discard pile. A Personality that had only Chi thanks to the Rosary is destroyed when it leaves (as for any attachment that leaves play).
- **The Topaz Armor:** the pair is chosen one keyword at a time (the first of eleven, then the second of the ten that remain) through the shared Event decision (`eventChoice`), which every client and the bot already answer; a keyword the wielder already has is not granted twice, so nothing printed is lost with the Armor. "Ignoring costs" is a zero Gold price on a Topaz Champion.
- **Gozu, "legal":** a Terrain is "legal" to play when the engine's own action list shows it enabled at the moment the search ends (an enabled `playStrategy`, or `useAbility` for a Terrain played through its own action). While the play is owed and the opponent's reply makes it impossible, the first command the player sends releases him and the card is discarded, so no player is ever left without a legal action.

## New primitives and hooks

`SearchPlayFinishSearch` (`FinishSearch`), `SearchPlayKeepsPriority` (`FinishDeferredAction`), `EnforceSearchPlay` / `ApplySearchPlayToOptions` (`ApplyCore`, `QueryActions`), `SearchPlayApplyEffect` (the default branch of a search's effect), `SearchPlaySourceZoneAllows` / `SearchPlayHasProvinceAction` (a source in a face-up province), `EnterCelestialPlay`, `SearchPlayDiscardPersonalityFromPlay` (the `DiscardFromPlay` cost), `SearchPlayAttachesFree` (`EquipGoldCost`), `ResolveSearchPlayTurnEnd` (`EndTurn`), `SearchPlayEventChoice` / `SearchPlayEventChoiceActions` and `ApplySearchPlayEntryEffects` (the Armor's queued entry decision), `SearchPlayTransferGrants` (`TransferAttachment`), and the three-line "carries a cost card" hooks Ikoma Shika - exp already uses.

## Client contract

No new state is sent to the client. Every decision reuses an existing generic presentation: `searchSelect` / `searchDeck` (Gozu, Fudo, Hyobuko, the Rosary), `eventChoice` actions (the Armor's keywords), the per-card discard options of an ability with a chosen cost (Gozu's second way), and ordinary disabled options with a reason (the owed play). Masaya's action is a `useAbility` option whose `CardId` is a face-up province card; it needs a native look to confirm the action list shows it (the Reserve recruit already lists province cards).
