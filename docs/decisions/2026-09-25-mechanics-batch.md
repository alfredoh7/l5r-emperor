## Primitives

- **Additional actions (2-5):** grants usable in the Action Phase; optional, mandatory, keyword- and target-scoped grants; a pass uses up one grant; `declineAdditionalAction` (a decline is not a pass); a phase or battle ends only when both players pass consecutively; a per-battle record of each player's Battle actions; a Battle/Open action in a battle uses up a grant (before, it ended the burst).
- **Aramasu's Legacy (1):** a whole Attack Phase inside a paused Action Phase (no start/end phase effects fire), resolution destroys no defending army or province and does not bow the controller's Mantis Clan Magistrates or his attachments, a per-phase win record and a delayed optional bow with a straighten hold.
- **Control transfer (6-10):** `TransferUnitControl` takes, gives and returns a whole unit (home or battlefield side), no recruit, no entering play, no Proclaim; Loyal and Unique/Singular checks; control returns on turn end, on leaving play, or on the next announcement of the same ability; the Infiltrator straighten trait.
- **Copies (32):** every copied card exposes `IsCopy`, `CopyOfCardId`, `CopyOfDefinitionId`, `CopyConditions`, `RealOwnerId` in the view; the frame and tooltip are Alfredo's (issue #502).
- **Costs (11-16, 20, 21, 27):** the Marshalling Fields waiver of the one-Terrain rule; optional costs that feed the effect; additional costs of entering play (`entryCostDecision`); the common `TargetedByAction` trigger (Sakonoko); phase-dependent bow costs; two performer slots with cross-slot conditions and the live `UnitsOnSide` count; `CostVerbs.DiscardFavor` (mandatory or one of an alternative cost; 14 earlier hard-coded Favor cards migrated).
- **Payment (17-19, 22-26):** payment tokens in the source lists for Reaction Gold on a card that also bows (Silk, Library of Rebirth, Seaside Bazaar), Zlkyt's destroy-a-Mine-or-Farm options (listed last, highest cost first) and Rani's discount (`PaymentSubject.Wielder`); the Gold-minus-N floor and pay-or-else-straighten; a Focus Effect that sets the duel winner's Gold Cost to 0.
- **Records (28-31):** the per-player Recon counter (reset each turn end), the attach log, cards put into hand per phase, timed removal ("when the turn ends", "when its player's next turn begins"), Equip (Limited) now accepts Spells.

## Mendon's rulings applied during the pass

Aramasu's Legacy follows its scan (bow until after the next Straighten Phase ends; Mantis Clan Magistrates and attachments not bowed). A partially negated action counts towards a record, a wholly negated one does not. Equip (Limited) attaches Spells, Followers and Items. Using a Reaction is always optional and a player may always pass. Beyond the Line may be played by either player but only its player takes the granted action (Hidden Falls Dojo is the one Recon Limited/Open ability that targets a province). Inokuchi, One Action Two Strikes, Yajinden's return and Amazing Feat confirmed. "Ninja Personality" means Ninja as a keyword.

## Readings for Mendon to confirm or correct

1. Shika: the scan prints "+2 strength if you discarded a card" (Ranged 6, else Ranged 4). The Terrain draw happens as the action resolves and is lost if the whole action is negated.
2. Kazuko destroys only a Follower attached to her; if that destruction is prevented the whole action is negated. En'you: the cost is paid on every recruit (province, dead pile, deck search) but not by a created copy; one way to pay is automatic; Sake tokens are those on any card you control; the removed card cannot be En'you.
3. Marshalling Fields: the waiver is automatic (no separate "may" prompt); Terrains that replace the existing one still destroy it; a Terrain created by a non-Terrain action does not count for its draw.
4. Sakonoko is offered only when the action could destroy the target (an Attack or a curated destroy list); a bowed Sakonoko cannot react even by bowing a Spell. Six older Reactions (Mirumoto Rokai, Champion of Thunder, Brothers of Goemon, Akodo Kakihara, Yoritomo Sakuma, Kitsuki Yataku) still use the before-action trigger instead of the shared one and so do not react to Attacks: migrate?
5. Ascending the Ranks in a battle has no "unbowed" requirement; Fury of a Mob's Nonhumans may be Personalities or Followers, unbowed, at the battlefield; Temple of the Seven Fortunes is Open (Action Phase) and gives +2 to each undestroyed province until end of turn.
6. Aramasu's Legacy: no Declaration Segment; a tie destroys only the attacking army; the target may be either player's Personality or Holding; the hold applies even if the bow is negated; a Retribution after it gets no exemptions. Momoko follows the scan ("non-destruction costs"; waived: bow, discard, remove from game, Honor, Gold; still paid: destroy, sacrifice, destroy-token, region, province) and her watch is used up by the first action on her Spell even if it has no cost.
7. Control: Asuka's give is a trait, and a control change is not a move (she can end up in a defending army); the Infiltrator trait is mandatory and automatic with one candidate; Only a Shadow's trait stays even when the control change is refused for Loyal or Unique; Shadow Dragon's return triggers on the next announcement of the same ability even if negated; Dark Naga's Reaction needs an unbowed Forest (bowed as its cost) and "Human" is "without Nonhuman"; Sachina may target her own Personality (nothing happens); Black Marketeering fails a Follower's Honor Requirement when his current Personal Honor is below the printed requirement and the dishonor and destruction happen even if the take is refused.

9. Payment: the Reaction and the bow of Silk, Library of Rebirth and Seaside Bazaar may both be used in one payment (a change for Silk and the Library); Reactions of Bazaar, Zlkyt, Rani, Singh and Tanimura need their card unbowed (only Silk and Library say "even if bowed"); Zlkyt works for any Gold cost and reads printed Gold Cost; Rani lowers only that Spell's price for that payment; Kae's trait fires on any player's Battle action, Attacks included; Greasing the Wheels' Battle half: declining straightens the whole unit even if the payment would have been 0; The Heir's Wrath pays exactly the unit's total cost and is not "unable to be delayed"; The Host's Advantage: the Courtier may be bowed if the Favor is discarded instead.
