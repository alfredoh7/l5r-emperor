# #674 pers-gates-1 (personality-wave-133): fourteen Gates of Chaos Personalities

Status: engineering readings built from the printed scans; the points under "Readings built in" are Mendon's to confirm. Lane: rules. Issue: https://github.com/alfredoh7/l5r-emperor/issues/674. Engine: `src/L5R.Core/WlPersGates1.cs`; packager `tools/content/package_personality_wave_133.py`; tests `WlPersGates1Tests`.

All fourteen cards of the cluster are wired from their Gates of Chaos scans (each scan read: stats, keywords and text match the catalogue). None is blocked. No new primitive and no new state: existing mechanisms only (ordered search, Invest as an own-enters-play trait, EntryHonorLoss, forced discard, AttackDefense fields, Hayate's three-way keyword action).

## Readings (rulings by Mendon, 2026-10-06, issue #679)

Each reading below is marked **confirmed** or **corrected** on 2026-10-06 and built as such.

- **Asahina Kitiaru - exp: corrected.** The card discarded after the search MAY be the Ring just found (`SearchSpec.DiscardMayBeFound`; other ToHandThenDiscard searches keep issue #368's exclusion). Nothing is discarded when no Ring is found.
- **Invest: corrected.** Invest is an enter-play effect, not a Reaction: it behaves like a Reaction (an optional entry-window action) but cannot be targeted by effects that target or react to Reactions. It is a `TraitReactions` member, which every Reaction ban and Reaction-targeting rule asks. Audit of every Invest card on main (Clay Kshatriya, Togashi Noritada, Yoritomo Toganin, Susumu Takada, The Broken Man, Doji Razan, Hida Gojiro, Isawa Hajime, Kitsune Yuko, Shosuro Nigawa, Utaku Sayaka): one gap, Nigawa's and Sayaka's `wl2-invest-two-tokens` (personality-wave-134), now a trait; a test sweeps the catalogue so a future Invest cannot miss it.
- **Enter-play effects occur top to bottom in the printed order: corrected.** Gojiro and Hajime print Invest above "After X enters play, lose 1 Honor", so the loss now happens after the Invest window closes (`wl1-invest-then-loss-N` Effect, applied as priority returns, `MatchState.WlPersGates1DeferredLosses`). Tobei (Destined, then the loss) and Legulus (the loss is first) keep it at once. No other card on main prints both an Invest and an entry Honor loss, so no other card changed behaviour.
- **Boyoh Mercenary: confirmed.** The entry discard is a forced discard of the controller's choice (an empty hand owes nothing); every Boyoh in an army that destroys a province draws a card.
- **Daidoji Tametaka - exp: corrected.** If Tametaka would WIN a duel against Yoritomo Hiromi (either version) he loses INSTEAD and the result is the same as a tie (both duelists lose). Hiromi does not win. A tie and a Hiromi win are unchanged.
- **Hoshoku-sha: confirmed.** He may destroy himself as part of his action. The practice bot never does (a destroy aimed at its own card scores below passing; a bot-policy test pins this for Hoshoku-sha).
- **Asako Karachu - exp: confirmed.** One use a turn; when an effect grants extra uses each keyword is gained at most once a turn (an already-gained keyword is not offered again).
- **Doji Razan: corrected.** "A Fudo card" is any card in play whose Fudo keyword is read from the card (`wl1-fudo-card`); Strongholds are not cards and are never targets, Regions are cards and qualify (no Fudo Region exists in the catalogue, so none is tested). The target's destruction and Razan's are independent: a negation of one does not stop the other.
- **Legulus, Ide Hinobu: confirmed.** "Your deck" is the Fate deck; -3 strength via the AttackDefense data; Hinobu has a printed production of 3.
- Karachu's keyword lasts until the turn ends (R-001); Hoshoku-sha's +2F too.
