# #674 pers-gates-1 (personality-wave-133): fourteen Gates of Chaos Personalities

Status: engineering readings built from the printed scans; the points under "Readings built in" are Mendon's to confirm. Lane: rules. Issue: https://github.com/alfredoh7/l5r-emperor/issues/674. Engine: `src/L5R.Core/WlPersGates1.cs`; packager `tools/content/package_personality_wave_133.py`; tests `WlPersGates1Tests`.

All fourteen cards of the cluster are wired from their Gates of Chaos scans (each scan read: stats, keywords and text match the catalogue). None is blocked. No new primitive and no new state: existing mechanisms only (ordered search, Invest as an own-enters-play trait, EntryHonorLoss, forced discard, AttackDefense fields, Hayate's three-way keyword action).

## Readings built in

- **Asahina Kitiaru - exp**: "Discard a card" follows the found Ring like Ikoma Shizuka's Terrain search (R-011/R-012 ordered search, then ToHandThenDiscard): the Ring just found cannot be the card discarded; when no Ring is found nothing is discarded; when no other card is in hand the discard is skipped.
- **Invest (Razan, Gojiro, Hajime, Yuko)**: the existing shape (Togashi Noritada): an optional own-enters-play trait paid in the entry window, not a Reaction (TraitReactions). "May also pay ... once" is not extended beyond the entry window.
- **Entering Honor loss (Tobei, Gojiro, Hajime, Legulus)** is the mandatory EntryHonorLoss, applied before the Invest window.
- **Boyoh Mercenary**: the entry discard is a forced discard of the controller's own choice (empty hand owes nothing). The draw is read where a battle's resolution destroys a province: every Boyoh in the attacking army at the battlefield draws.
- **Daidoji Tametaka - exp**: "loses any duel against Yoritomo Hiromi" is applied to the result after the totals: if Tametaka would win against a Personality titled Yoritomo Hiromi (either version), Hiromi wins and Tametaka loses; a tie (both lose) and a Hiromi win are unchanged. This is one new line in `ResolveDuel` (Rend the Soul's neighbour); it is not the general P18 auto-lose primitive.
- **Hoshoku-sha**: both sentences happen; the target may be himself (then he gets no +2F). +2F lasts until end of turn (R-001).
- **Karachu**: the keyword lasts until end of turn; one use per turn across the three choices.
- **Doji Razan**: "a Fudo card" is any non-Stronghold, non-Region card in play (Hitaka's Jade shape); Razan is destroyed even when the target's destruction is negated.
- **Legulus**: "your deck" is the Fate deck (Followers are Fate cards); the -3 uses the Shieldman AttackDefense data (Both, Unit).
- **Ide Hinobu**: a printed production of 3 (Daidoji Soken's shape).
