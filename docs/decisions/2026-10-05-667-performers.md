# Promotional pass #667, the "performers" cluster (item-wave-46, strategy-wave-114)

Status: accepted as engineering readings built from Mendon's confirmed rulings; the points under "Readings built in" are his to confirm. Lane: rules (shared files touched in small, local ways, listed at the end). Issue: https://github.com/alfredoh7/l5r-emperor/issues/667.

Five cards that the Promotional-Emperor pass left blocked are wired (plus the created Ambusher scan). Three are Promotional-Emperor scans (The Blade of the Balash, The Isha's Yumi - exp, Winter Sled); Wyrmbone Katana and Lying in Ambush are Promotional-Celestial scans, so their printing is asserted as such by the packagers.

## Mendon's rulings, as built

- **The Blade of the Balash / The Isha's Yumi - exp.** A Dragon (Mantis) Clan player may attach the card as an Item or discard it from his hand. Discarded from the hand, it is treated as a Strategy and the card is the one producing the Melee 5 (Ranged 6) Attack; attached, it is an Item and its wielder performs the Attack. Built as two catalogue entries sharing one use: the bow half (wielder at the battle, the card in play) and the hand half (discard as the cost, only a player whose Stronghold Clan is Dragon / Mantis, the Rule of Presence of any Battle action: a unit of yours at the current battlefield). The hand half's Attack has no wielder: its source is the card itself.
- **Wyrmbone Katana.** "Assigned there" means he was assigned to the battlefield earlier this phase and has since moved away. Built on the record Back to the Front keeps (`IvoryMovementState`, the battlefields a Personality was assigned to this phase); the move is refused while he is at the battlefield or when the record does not name it.
- **Winter Sled's static** (see the ambiguity below).
- **Lying in Ambush.** Any created Personality counts as long as it does not have the Shadowlands keyword; it counts whether or not the created Personality leaves play later; and it counts after an action that creates several Personalities as long as at least one is not Shadowlands. Built from a record the one creation site writes into the running action's record (kind `created`, its location, and whether it had Shadowlands as it was created), read by the existing "after an action resolves" Reaction window.

## Winter Sled

Built as printed and **confirmed by Mendon**: "While you control a Winter card, other players' cards' effects will not negate this Personality's movement." Only OTHER players' cards are barred from negating his movement; his own controller's cards still can (if he has the means to). `MovementNegationBarred(mover, negatingPlayerId)` is the one place that knows whose card negates; it is true when the mover carries a Sled whose controller controls a Winter card and the negating card belongs to another player. Every negation source routes through it (NegatesEffect, Request Authorization, movement locks, Too Close to Home, Chikushudo's Trickery, Ambush, Kyuden Kitsune, Ugulu no Oni, the delay store); the own-controller shields (Blessings, Show of Restraint, Twin Forks, Hida Kaiji, The Snow Has Teeth, Shieldman, Star-Crossed Lovers, Goju Asuka, Hotei, Fudo, Suikihime) still apply to his own movement.

"A Winter card": any card with the Winter keyword that the Sled's controller controls in play (a Holding or Stronghold, a Terrain, a face-up Event in play).

## Readings built in (not confirmed rulings)

- The hand half of the Blade and the Yumi remains an Item that may be played like a Strategy (Mendon, confirmed): it is played from the hand with its discard as the cost, as Tsubute's hand half is. The engine has no Strategy discard-to-play pathway for an Item, and no Strategy play-count watcher exists to feed, so it stays a one-off entry (low risk) that a future "after you play a Strategy" watcher would have to include.
- **Winter Sled's Reaction** needs enemy units at the current battlefield and the wearer not already there ("move this Personality there" with nothing to move is not offered).
- **Wyrmbone Katana's destroy**: "an enemy card with ... no attachments" is a Follower or a Personality without attachments (Headbutt's reading), compared with the wielder's current Force (attachments included), strictly lower.
- **Lying in Ambush's location**: "at the same location" is two catalogue entries sharing one use (home or the current battlefield), offered by where the first non-Shadowlands Personality of the action was created; several created at different locations use the first. The Samurai is Ambusher (`user-scan-ambusher`, personality-wave-127), the scan Mendon supplied (assets/requested-scans/ambusher.jpg): a CreatedOnly 2F/2C/2PH Samurai (the fan's 2 is its Personal Honor; the dash in the pink box is its Honor Requirement, none), given its creator's Clan alignment at creation by the creator's own text. No scan is outstanding. The action must be the Reacting player's own and a Battle, Limited, Open (or Battle/Open) ability; a Reaction's own creation does not count.
- Shadowlands is read as the keyword or a Shadowlands Clan alignment at the moment the Personality is created.

## Not covered

- Only Personalities count, as printed: created Followers, Items and Holdings are not recorded. Every Personality an action creates (including copies) goes through `CreatePersonality`, so every one is recorded.
- Art: the five scans are shipped under `Content/Published` but are not in the catalogue art pack; a publisher step (`build_catalogue_art.py` and a release) is pending, as for every new wave.
