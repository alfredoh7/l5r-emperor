# Unified catalogue controls — accepted direction

Alfredo approved concept B's compact layout with concept A's checkbox clarity and keyword search, plus a draggable card-size slider on 2026-10-01. This is design acceptance; the implementation and playable preview still require review. Issue: https://github.com/alfredoh7/l5r-emperor/issues/549.

Use one shared browser/filter component for Deck Builder and Card Library. Keep the persistent multi-select clan medallion row restored by Mendon in #563. Open searchable, spaced checklists beside their triggers, with select-all/deselect-all and immediate updates; closing a menu is not confirmation. Keep removable selection summaries, scoped keyword counts and A–Z/most-common ordering. Explain when a family/filter change clears incompatible selections.

Keep Mendon's existing additive OR semantics within groups and AND across groups, family-derived type options, chronological set order and earliest-printing reprint sorting. Clearing all selections removes a group restriction. Preserve reference/playable distinctions, pinned scans and workshop eligibility; no Core/server behavior changes are authorized here.

The browser presents complete card faces with a draggable size slider and reflows without resetting filters or losing the browsing anchor. Deck Builder retains deck totals, quantities, setup, comparison, persistence and validation in the adjacent workspace. Card Library retains full inspection and printing provenance. Ordinary set filtering remains single-select; multi-set selection and owned-copy tracking are not part of this task.

Concept originals and prompts: artifacts/unified-filters-concepts-2026-10-01 in Alfredo's primary project. Generated card text/counts/sigils are illustrative and must not replace the actual game resources. Reuse the established cloth, clan medallions, WeatheredQuill/Caudex fonts and lacquer assets; native captures are the implementation evidence.
