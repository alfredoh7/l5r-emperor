# #699, the "rings" cluster: how a Ring enters play (spell-wave-57, celestial-wave-29)

Status: accepted as engineering readings; every point under "Readings built in" is Mendon's to confirm or correct. Lane: rules (a few one-line edits to shared files, listed below). Issue: https://github.com/alfredoh7/l5r-emperor/issues/699.

Mendon's brief: "How Rings enter play matters. There are several actions that put rings into play that disqualify them from counting towards an enlightenment victory. Find those cards and evaluate a mechanism for tracking how Rings enter play such that cards like Fudo's Assurances and an enlightenment victory can be adjudicated, then build that engine primitive. Also, create a reaction trigger for Rings entering play so that Witness the Untold (and other Ring reactions) can be wired."

Engine code: `src/L5R.Core/WlRings.cs`; tests: `tests/L5R.Core.Tests/WlRingsTests.cs` (suite `wl-rings`); packagers `tools/content/package_spell_wave_57.py` and `package_celestial_wave_29.py`.

## Research: what the rules and the cards say

**The rule.** Core rules, Winning the Game (p. 88, `docs/rules/EMPEROR_EDITION_CORE_RULES.md`): a player wins if they start their turn controlling all five Rings (Air, Earth, Fire, Water, Void, one each), "each of which *most recently* entered play via its own printed text (not, e.g., overlaid or granted by another card's effect)". So the test is on the Ring's LAST entry into play, not on how it got anywhere earlier. The engine already checked Enlightenment at the start of each turn (`MatchEngine.StartTurn`, and `PhaseOrder.cs` for the "another player has five Rings" test), with a per-Ring mark (`AbilityUses["ee-e:no-enlightenment"]`) that some entry paths set and the Ring's own recruit cleared. The mark was a one-bit stand-in: it could not say who put the Ring there, and nothing but Enlightenment read it.

## The rule built in: which entries disqualify

A Ring qualifies for the Enlightenment Victory (and for "last entered play by its own text") only while its LAST entry into play was **by its own printed text**. The entry paths are:

| Path | Counts as |
|---|---|
| The Ring's own recruit: Ring of Air/Earth/Fire/Water/Void's play from the hand, each False Ring's "put this Ring into play" (Air after a won battle, Earth after a duel, Fire's Open, Water's Battle, the Void after a Province destroyed) | own text |
| Asako Usohara's second Limited ("This counts as playing it by its own text") | own text (not yet published) |
| Togashi's Guidance - exp: put a Ring in your hand into play | another card's effect |
| Starting on the Path's granted Limited | another card's effect |
| Asako Chukage's entry search, "put it into play" | another card's effect |
| Celestial Sword of the Dragon - exp's entry search, "put it into play" | another card's effect |
| A Ring taken into the HAND by any of these | not an entry; no record (and no trigger) |

The Fudo keyword still excludes every False Ring from the Enlightenment count regardless of how it entered, as the False Rings print. For "five Fudo Rings that last entered play by their own text" the False Rings qualify through their own recruits, and a False Ring that Togashi's Guidance or Starting on the Path put into play does not.

## The primitive

* **Record.** `MatchState.WlRings` (`JsonPropertyOrder` 21400): `Entries`, a map from Ring card id to `RingEntryRecord(Mode, SourceCardId, AbilityId, Turn)`, with `Mode` `"own-text"` or `"other-card"`. The record is overwritten at every entry, so it always answers "how did this Ring LAST enter play". A Ring with no record counts as having entered by its own text (a Ring placed directly in play, as fixtures and old saves do). The hand-over `SearchEnteredRingId` lets an entry search tell `FinishSearch` that a Ring entered play outside every action.
* **One entry point.** `MatchEngine.RingEntersPlay(player, ring, byOwnText, source, abilityId)` (`WlRings.cs`): writes the record, keeps the older per-Ring mark in step (existing tests and any reader of the mark are unchanged), sets `RingPutIntoPlayTurn` (Brotherhood Schism / Losing the Way), and records the entry on the resolving action so the trigger hears it. Every path above calls it; the five scattered hand-written mark/clear/turn lines are gone.
* **Readers.** `RingLastEnteredByOwnText(ring)`, used by `CountsForEnlightenment` (the start-of-turn victory test and the "another player has five Rings" test of `PhaseOrder.cs`), by Unsettling Doubts' target ("a Ring which did not last enter play by its own text") and by `FudoRingsByOwnText` (Fudo's Assurances).
* **View.** Nothing new is sent to the client: the record is engine-private bookkeeping. The Enlightenment test and the card effects read it on the server.

## The trigger

`ReactionTriggers.RingEntersPlay` ("ring-enters-play"): "After your Ring enters play". A Reaction carrying it is offered in the existing **after-action-resolved window** (the window Yung, Introspection and Kikugoro already used) to the Ring's controller only, for every path above:

* A Ring that enters inside an action (its own recruit, Togashi's Guidance, Starting on the Path, the False Rings) records a `ring-entered` effect on that action; the window opens when the action finishes, as for any after-action Reaction.
* A Ring that enters outside every action (the entry searches of Asako Chukage and Celestial Sword of the Dragon) opens the same window from `FinishSearch`, through the catalogued pseudo-action `wl-ring-entry-search` (never on a card, so it is never offered), and priority goes back where the entry left it.

The window is optional and passable, opens only for a player holding an applicable Reaction (no window for nothing), and the AI's existing pass handling for that window applies. Reactions are the controller's: an opponent's Ring entering play does not open it for my Witness the Untold or Yung. Yung and Introspection were moved onto the new trigger (their old recognizer is the same `YungRingEnteredPlay`, now generalised to every recorded entry); Kikugoro is left on his own wiring (his Ring half also needs his own source in play and shares one use with his Henshin halves).

## Readings built in (not confirmed rulings)

1. **Own text.** A Ring's "own printed text" is its own recruit/put-into-play ability. Another card that puts a Ring into play (Togashi's Guidance, Starting on the Path, the two entry searches) does not, even when it says "put it into play". Asako Usohara's "This counts as playing it by its own text" is an explicit exception on the card (own text), recorded with Usohara as source.
2. **Last entry only.** A Ring that another card put into play, then returned to the hand and recruited by its own text, counts again (the core rule says "most recently"); the reverse (own text, then Togashi's Guidance) disqualifies it. A Ring that leaves play keeps its stale record until it enters again; nothing reads a record for a Ring not in play.
3. **Fudo's Assurances.** "five Fudo Rings" is at least five Fudo Rings the acting player controls in play, each last entered by its own text, re-checked as the effect resolves. The Battle/Open "Straighten a Ring" targets any Ring in play (either player's), like Mukudori's. "destroy a Province" names no owner and no "target": the acting player names any Province not already destroyed (either player's), through the same "any province" choice as Ring of Earth. The action is only offered with five qualifying Fudo Rings in play, so no bot reaches it in an ordinary deck. The Open action is not taken in a battle (it is Open, not Battle/Open). Destroying the last Province of a player is a Military victory, as everywhere.
4. **Witness the Untold.** "your Ring" is a Ring its controller owns; "Dynasty or Fate deck" is a choice among the player's own two decks (Control Your Destiny's look), nothing but the reorder; the Spell is bowed as the cost and must be in play. Offered after the action finishes, not mid-effect.
5. **Entry searches and the restriction.** An entry search may not put a Ring into play when Brotherhood Schism or Losing the Way forbids it (the choice is refused and the Ring may still be taken into the hand); this closed an existing gap (see below).
6. **Chukage's / the Sword's search of the hand.** A Ring found in the hand that the search then puts into play "enters play", so it gets a record and opens the window; one taken into the hand never entered play.

## Gap closed

Entry searches (Asako Chukage, Celestial Sword of the Dragon - exp) never asked Brotherhood Schism / Losing the Way before putting a Ring into play, unlike every other path, and never opened "after your Ring enters play" for Yung, Introspection or Kikugoro. Both now do (`EmperorFollowupsSearch.cs`, `DeckSearch.FinishSearch`).

## Blocked (2), `tools/content/pe_wl_699_rings_blockers.json`

* **Asako Usohara** (oracle-l5r-10493), SEARCH: "Limited, bow: Search your Dynasty discard pile, then deck, for a Fudo Celestial. Put it into play." needs a search whose destination is a Celestial in play; the second line (a Fudo Ring from the hand, counted as its own text) is buildable on the new record but a card with two printed lines is wired whole or blocked.
* **The False Path** (oracle-l5r-10561), GRANT: "You may not win by Enlightenment" is now a one-line read of the Enlightenment check, but the Stronghold also grants Monk/Fudo keywords to Personalities in and out of play, waives Fudo Honor Requirements and Honor loss, and has a bow search; none of that is built, and no stronghold scan is packaged here.

The blockers formerly recorded for Fudo's Assurances (`pe_wl_items_misc_blockers.json`, PROVENANCE) and Witness the Untold (`pe_wl_spells_rings_blockers.json`, TRIG-AFTER) are removed; both are wired.
