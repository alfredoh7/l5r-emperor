# Honor and Treachery pass: every unwired card re-checked against the engine (issue #458)

Requested by Mendon: repeat the Seeds of Decay pass (#453, `docs/decisions/2026-09-23-seeds-of-decay-pass.md`) against the smaller Honor and Treachery reference set -- evaluate every unwired card against `docs/rules/EMPEROR_EDITION_CORE_RULES.md` and the engine as it now stands, wire everything that can be wired, and record a specific blocker for the rest. Lane: rules. Issue: [#458](https://github.com/alfredoh7/l5r-emperor/issues/458).

Of the set's 60 reference cards, 43 are reprints of Personalities/cards already published and wired in earlier waves (Akodo Regulars, Bamboo Harvesters, Bayushi Manami, Daidoji Kenshi, and so on) -- nothing to re-wire; their existing `AbilityCatalog` entries and stats stand. **17 cards carry a new printing unique to this set** (`oracle-l5r-10240` through `oracle-l5r-10256`, plus The Towers of the Yogo, `oracle-l5r-8392`, a Stronghold reprint whose own granted-ability text was still unwired). **16 of the 17 are now wired**; **1 stays blocked**, with an updated, specific blocker note in `tools/content/build_card_inventory.py`.

## Wired (16 cards, `personality-wave-61`, `holding-wave-8`, `spell-wave-19`, `strategy-wave-51`, `stronghold-wave-13`, `follower-wave-27`)

Akodo Shihiro, Bayushi Irezu, Bayushi Kasumi, Bayushi Utoro, Border Keep - inexperienced, Communion with Earth, Devoted Yojimbo, Elite Training, Isawa Taiken, Shiba Mitsushen, Shiba Tsukimi - experienced 3.5, Shosuro Chikata, The Sacred Temple of the Phoenix - inexperienced, The Towers of the Yogo, Tsubo - experienced, Yogo Haruto.

## New primitives (`HonorAndTreacheryPass.cs`, plus small extensions to existing engine files)

- **A controller-wide static ABILITY grant** (`StaticGrantedAbilityIds`, The Towers of the Yogo: "Each of your Scorpion Clan Personalities has the ability, 'Battle, bow: Bow a target enemy Follower or Personality without Followers.'"): the same shape `StaticKeywordRules.cs` already gives static KEYWORD grants, but for a whole granted ability, read live wherever a card's current abilities are joined. `AbilityIdsOf` (the one join point of printed + granted + now static-grant abilities) moved from a `static` to an instance method to read engine state; seven small `private static` wrapper methods that called it lost their own `static` as a cascading, behavior-neutral fix.
- **A combined payment-kind-or-Shugenja `ConditionalProduction` condition** (`PayingForSpellOrShugenja`, The Sacred Temple of the Phoenix: "produce 5 Gold which may only pay for equipping a Spell or recruiting a Shugenja"): the existing primitive covered a single payment kind or a single card-type condition; this is the first condition combining a `PaymentKind` check with a `CardType`-or-keyword check on the paid-for card.
- **Bayushi Irezu's home/battlefield swap and duel-substitution Reaction**: `IrezuMove` reuses `MoveHome`/`TryMoveToBattlefield` for the printed "move home, move Irezu to the battlefield" swap (`MovesToBattle`, the target's own category carrying no location restriction since "move home" is a no-op for a target already there); `irezu-duel-enter` reuses `YojimboRedirectApplies`/`RedirectAction` (Ezuno's own shape) narrowed to `acting.IssuesDuel`, the first duel-specific use of that Reaction family.
- **Shiba Tsukimi's "may use its abilities a second time" refresh, generalized past one card** (`RefreshBattleAbilities`): the Kensai's Weapons "again" boost (issue #331) generalized from a Kensai's own Weapons to any card's every Battle-timing ability, by clearing each one's per-turn-use stamp.
- **A shared army-membership Target/Performer category** (`own-follower-or-personality-in-army`, Devoted Yojimbo: "bow one of your Followers or Personalities in this army to straighten a target Follower or Personality in this army"): the first category letting the PERFORMER be a Follower attached in-army, not only a Personality; needed in *two* separate switches (the validation switch already covered every existing category, but the QueryActions listing switch that enumerates legal performer candidates for an offered command did not -- an ability whose Performer category is missing from that second switch is offered to nobody at all, since it falls back to a null-performer default that no such ability's validation ever accepts).

## Readings and assumptions (Mendon to confirm or correct)

1. **Shosuro Chikata's printed Clan field is "Ninja"**, not "Scorpion" -- his Scorpion Clan allegiance is carried as a printed keyword ("Scorpion Clan"), matching how the catalogue already records every Ninja-type Scorpion agent. His toggle (`chikata-bow`/`chikata-straighten`) shares one `UsageKey`, per the printed "Bow *or* straighten" wording.
2. **Devoted Yojimbo's own bow is its cost, not its target's effect**: "bow one of your Followers or Personalities... to straighten a target..." reads as `ActionCost.BowPerformer` on the chosen performer (which may be Yojimbo himself), distinct from the straightened target.
3. **Border Keep - inexperienced's "on your first turn" reads as `Player.TurnsTaken == 1`**, the same per-player turn count every other "on your first turn" card already reads, not `MatchState.TurnNumber` (match-wide).
4. **Communion with Earth's granted Stalwart is a one-time entry effect** (`AttachGrantsKeyword`), applied at the moment of attaching/equipping/transferring -- `TransferAttachment` already re-applies it at the new wielder and removes it from the old one, so the Battle transfer needed no new code of its own.

## Not wired (1 card) -- exact blocker note in `tools/content/build_card_inventory.py`

- **Bayushi Nitoshi - inexperienced** (`oracle-l5r-10248`): "Battle/Limited: Look at the top card of your Fate deck. If it is an attachment, you may attach it to one of your Personalities at Nitoshi's location, paying all costs. If it is a Strategy, you may take an additional action to play it (if legal)." `LookSpec` only covers the existing look-then-discard/keep/reorder shapes; this needs a new "peek the top card, branch on its own type, and either attach it in place (paying costs) or grant an additional action to play it, else leave it on top" primitive, all three of which are new.

The pass also corrected a stale blocker note: `oracle-l5r-1992` (Devoted Yojimbo) carried an old entry from before the Follower Performer category existed at all ("own-unbowed-follower-in-source-unit... but this card's performer is different") -- removed now that `own-follower-or-personality-in-army` wires it.
