# Accepted battle presentation selections

Alfredo selected 1A Frayed ink, 2B Daisho seal, 3A Crimson breathing ink, 4A Broad ink arrow, both 5A Named defeat and 5B Aftermath, and 6B Battle exchanges. Issue #75 / PR #76. These refine the existing shared-court, assignment and province-fall passes; they do not authorize permanent extra space between provinces or a different Home layout.

- A destroyed province retains an empty frayed perimeter and extinguished, fractured live-number seal at its original location. It is never an assignment target.
- Only the first **confirmed attacking root** at a province starts its blood presentation. Local drafts, hovering, defender assignment and battle selection cannot start it. Once engaged it remains blood-marked during that attack, even if a unit leaves. A new turn resets engagement.
- First activation uses uneven blood absorption into canvas fibers, not the regular gold ink apparition. It then becomes a slow ominous pulse. Panning, selection, resizing and rerendering do not restart absorption. Reduced motion shows the settled state immediately, without pulse.
- Crimson pigment coverage is increased 25% over the extracted reference. Blood stays at the perimeter, leaving cards readable.
- Assignment pointers use broad source-derived ink arrows and larger stones. The tip follows the final curve tangent.
- Named public destruction notices precede card transfers. Aftermath records real Dead/discard/Home destinations. Bowing and returning home are not described as destruction. Reduced motion retains the textual outcome without delaying decisions.
- Battle exchanges occupy a collapsible, reserved left column, distinct from the right action inspector. It presents public events, including explicit accepted pass events from the engine. The client does not infer passes from priority changes or compute battle outcomes.
