# P16: Tireless and Home as ability data (issue #672)

Status: **implemented on `claude/672-p16`, awaiting integration**. Lane: rules. Requested by Mendon on 2026-10-06 under issue #672, from section P16 of [the primitive prompts](2026-10-05-remaining-unwired-primitives-prompts.md). Engine: `src/L5R.Core/TirelessHome.cs`. Tests: `tests/L5R.Core.Tests/TirelessHomeTests.cs` (`tireless-home`).

## What was built

- **`AbilityDefinition.Tireless`** (Coils of Madness rule 9: "the action may be taken even while the card is bowed"). Read by `ValidateAbility`'s bowed-source rule as one more term, `|| ability.Tireless`, beside the per-id list. The list was **left in place**: no older "even if bowed" ability was converted, so no existing card changes. The flag waives that one rule only. A Tireless action that prints a "bow this card" cost is refused while its card is bowed, because a bowed card cannot be bowed to pay.
- **`AbilityDefinition.Home`** ("the action may be taken even while the card is at home rather than at the current battlefield"). Read by the two source-at-the-battlefield checks of `ValidateAbility` through `MatchEngine.HomeWaivesSourceLocation`, beside `SourceMayBeAway`. It waives them only while the source's unit is at home. The acting player still needs a unit at the current battlefield unless the action is Absent or moves a Personality to the battle (`MovesToBattle`).
- **Granted Home.** "Give Home to one or more Spells in this unit" is an ordinary keyword grant of `Home` on the Spell (`GrantKeyword`, duration `Turn`). An action inherits its card's keywords, so `HomeWaivesSourceLocation` reads the card's `Home` keyword as well as the flag. It needs no state of its own: the grant is delayed, negated, shown on the card and removed at the end of the turn exactly as every granted keyword is. No `MatchState` property was added (the reserved orders 9700-9709 are unused).

## What was not built

- **`TimingOverrides`** (The Sorrow: "While you control a Courtier, The Sorrow's printed ability is Battle/Limited"). Not built, and The Sorrow is not wired. `ValidateAbility` is not the only reader of an ability's timing: `QueryActions` filters by `Timing` before it validates (the `phaseOk` and `actingAsBattle` lines), and about fifteen other readers treat `Timing == "Battle"` as "this is a Battle action" whatever the phase (`IsBattleAction` and the after-action facts in `ActionEffectTracking.cs`, `TargetedActionReactions.cs`, `TerrainStatics.cs`, `EmperorP5.cs`, `EmperorPersonalityBatch3.cs` and others). A Battle action taken as a Limited action in the Action phase would be listed by none of the first and miscounted by the second. That is a shared-interface change across files outside this primitive's allowed edits, so it is left for its own task.
- **`PresenceGrants`** (Doji Tatsuzo). Not built: its demonstration card was not in this task's list.
- **Tireless Reactions through the flag.** Five reaction-window openers decide whether a bowed card may react from the card alone (`BowedBarsAbilities`), before `ValidateAbility` is asked. They were not changed, so a future Tireless *Reaction* still needs its id added there, as Red Crane Dojo's is. The six cards wired here have no Tireless Reaction.

## Readings for Mendon to confirm or correct

1. **Home is "at home", not "anywhere".** A Home action may be taken with its card's unit at home or at the current battlefield. From *another* battlefield it is refused (Shinjo Kinto - exp's move home is the test). This is narrower than the older "even if this card is not at the current battlefield" wording (`SourceUnitMayBeAway`), which stays as it was.
2. **Home does not waive presence.** A Home action still needs a unit of the acting player's at the current battlefield, unless the action is also Absent or moves a Personality to the battle. Kinto's "move home" half and a Spell given Home by the Satchel are refused with no unit there.
3. **A Home action that moves a Personality to the battle keeps the 2026-09-19 ruling as the engine already applies it.** Such an action needs no presence, and the engine also waives its source's location entirely. So Kinto's "to the current battlefield" half and My Ancestor's Steed can be taken with the card at another battlefield as well as at home, exactly as Camel Mounts and Lao-she already can. If Home should hold these to "at home or at the current battlefield", that is a change to all the marked moves, not to these two.
4. **Kinto's one printed action is offered as two abilities sharing one use per turn** (Shosuro Hawado's shape): "Move Kinto home" and "If Kinto would be opposed, move him to the current battlefield". "Would be opposed" is read as Lao-she's is: an enemy unit is at the current battlefield.
5. **A move that cannot happen is still a legal action.** Kinto may take "move home" while already at home (with presence), and "to the current battlefield" while already there; nothing moves, no movement is reported, and the use is spent. The same holds for My Ancestor's Steed naming a Spirit already at the battlefield.
6. **Tireless does not pay a bow cost.** For an ability carrying the new flag, "bow this card" cannot be paid by a bowed card, so the action is refused. The older per-id abilities keep their own wiring; in particular Red Crane Dojo's "Ninja Tireless Reaction, bow this card" may still be taken while the Dojo is bowed, as it could before. Should that card follow the same rule?
7. **The Satchel's Home lasts until the turn ends** (R-001: no duration is printed).
8. **"Koan cards in this unit"** counts every card of the unit with the Koan keyword: the Personality and everything attached to him, the Satchel included. So the Satchel alone gives Home to one Spell.
9. **"Give Home to one or more Spells" does not say "target"**, but the engine carries the chosen Spells in a target slot, so they are treated as targets of the action (a targeted-by-action Reaction would be offered for them). The slot's printed maximum is a fixed 20; the real limit, the number of Koan cards, is enforced when the command is validated.
10. **A Spell given Home acts from home by the same rule a printed Home action does**: its own costs, targets and performers keep their own rules. A granted Home that some other effect removes (a keyword removal) stops working at once.
11. **My Ancestor's Steed: "your target Spirit Personality"** is a Personality the acting player owns with the Spirit keyword, at any location (home or any battlefield). The bow icon is the Spell's own bow ("bow this card"), not the Shugenja's.
12. **Daigotsu Kanpeki - exp2: "Take the Imperial Favor"** takes it from whoever holds it, or from nobody, with no condition, once per turn.
13. **Goju Obayashi: "Ninja Tireless Battle"** is tagged as a Ninja action (`ActionKeywords`), as the printed keyword before the designator.

## Client contract

- No new command, option field or view field.
- A Spell given Home shows `Home` among its granted keywords (duration `Turn`).
- `koan-satchel-home` is a slot action (`spells`, minimum 1). Its offered maximum is 20; a selection above the number of Koan cards in the unit is rejected with "Choose at most N Spell(s): the number of Koan cards in this unit."
- Kinto - exp shows two actions; using either spends both for the turn.
