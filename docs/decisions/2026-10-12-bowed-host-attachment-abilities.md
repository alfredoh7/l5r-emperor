# A bowed Personality may use the abilities of his unbowed attachments (issue #895)

Status: **accepted** (Mendon, 2026-10-12). Lane: rules.

## Ruling

An ability on an Item or Spell is blocked only when **that attachment** is bowed. A bowed host Personality does not stop his unbowed attachments' abilities. The host is the performer ("an action from an ability on an Item or Spell in the Personality's unit"), and the performer's bowed state is a gate only when the text says "unbowed" (R-035). A printed "Even if this card is bowed" lets a bowed attachment use the ability.

## Evidence

Comprehensive Rules for Emperor Edition (`docs/rules/COMPREHENSIVE_RULES.md`):

- Physically marked card states, "Bowed and unbowed" [CLARIFIED Jun 05 2013]: "Abilities on a bowed card can not be used (note that this does not prevent the use of abilities on unbowed cards attached to a bowed Personality)."
- Glossary, Perform: a Personality or Follower performs an action that is "from an ability on an Item or Spell in the Personality's unit."
- Card features, Conditions: "Even if this card is bowed" is a permission overriding the bowed-card rule.

Found when a bowed Bayushi Utoro bowed his unbowed Nightingale Blade to bow an enemy Personality. The engine allowed it, and that is correct. My (the agent's) first reading, that a bowed host could not perform attachment actions, was withdrawn on seeing the clarification.

## Cards that print the permission for the attachment

Celestial Sword of the Crab - exp, Fubatsu Plate, Mantle of the Jade Champion - exp2 and Strength of the Tsunami say "Even if this card is bowed". No Item or Spell in the catalogue says "even if this Personality (Shugenja, unit) is bowed".
