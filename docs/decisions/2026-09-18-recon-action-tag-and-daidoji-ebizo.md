# The Recon-action classification and the "after the resolution of your [keyword] action" trait: Daidoji Ebizo (and Master Scouts)

Status: accepted for implementation; the +1F duration is flagged prominently below. Lane: rules. Issue: #321 (PR B of #309); stacked on PR A (`docs/decisions/2026-09-18-battle-timing-primitives.md`, #324), whose `AbilityDefinition.ActionKeywords` field and Daidoji Yuki this builds on.

## The ruling (rules lead, verbatim)

> "In play trait that monitors for a Recon action played and resolved by the player controlling Ebizo grants him a +1F, because this is a trait, it is repeatable and occurs every time his controller resolves a recon action, this occurs regardless of Ebizo's location."

Daidoji Ebizo (`oracle-l5r-1629`; Crane; Bounty Hunter / Iron Crane / Samurai / Scout; Cost 9, Force 4, Chi 4, Honor Requirement 4, PH 2): "After the resolution of your Recon action: Give Ebizo +1F. / Battle: Ranged Attack with strength equal to Ebizo's Force." Verified against the scan (the Promotional-Emperor printing 2 the packager selects; the original Second City printing read too: identical).

## Decisions

**1. The classification: `ActionCarriesKeyword(ability, source, keyword)`.** An action carries a keyword when the ability *prints* it before its designator (`AbilityDefinition.ActionKeywords`, added in PR A: "Recon Reaction:", "Ninja Battle:", "Political Limited:") **or** the card it is on has that keyword -- the core rule that "an ability inherits all the keywords of the card it's on". Both halves are real in the catalogue: seven Strategies carry the Recon *keyword* (Burn the Towers, Forest Cover, Scouting the Pass, Seeking the Truth, Surrounded!, Torch's Flame Flickers, Watchers in the Dark; their actions are Recon actions by inheritance), while Daidoji Yuki, Jade's Roar, Qalyar, the Stronghold Recon Reactions print "Recon Reaction:" on the ability. How the printed prefixes were treated before, so the design fits them: *Ninja* -- Apprentice Shinobi reads the resolving ability's SOURCE card's Ninja keyword (`ExecuteAbilityEffects`); *Iaijutsu* -- a hardcoded ability-id list (`IsIaijutsuAction`, Mirumoto Yozo Experienced 2); *Political / Kiho / Fire / Fear / Maho / Recon* -- plain classification words recorded in comments only, with no engine representation. Ninja, Iaijutsu and the rest can move onto the same tag+inheritance check whenever someone wants (deliberately not migrated here: no behavior change without a request).

**Already-wired abilities carrying a printed Recon prefix: none.** Every published card `Text` and the whole 2,146-card catalogue were searched: no published card prints "Recon Battle/Open/Limited/Reaction" other than Daidoji Yuki (tagged in PR A). Hiruma Sniper, Keppo and Shinjo Kinto mention *Reconnaissance* (the state), not Recon actions. So the tag's first real user is Yuki; the training fixtures (`training-recon-open`, `training-recon-attack`, `training-recon-duel`, and two Strategies sharing one untagged ability, one with the Recon keyword) exercise the rest.

**A Recon Reaction counts as a Recon action -- yes.** The repo's rules docs say nothing to exclude a Reaction (Coils supplement: Engage/Interrupt "play like" Reactions and are actions), and Yuki's own ability is a Recon Reaction: the Ebizo test (`YukiTriggersEbizo`) proves Yuki's Reaction, attacking or defending, triggers his controller's Ebizo and only theirs.

**2. The trait, once.** `AfterOwnActionTraits` (in `CardAbilities.cs`) registers a card's `Effect` as "after the resolution of your `<keyword>` action: give this card `+n<stat>`"; `FireAfterOwnActionTraits` applies it to every in-play card of the acting player whose Effect is registered, in id order. Ebizo's Effect `force-after-own-recon-action` -> (`Recon`, Force, +1); **Master Scouts (`oracle-l5r-4867`) is literally the same string**, so it is wired here too (`follower-wave-15`, scan-verified: Force 2, Chi 0, Honor Req 1, cost 3, Focus 3, Elite / Gaijin / Scout) with no new engine code. Per the printed distinction and the ruling it is a **trait**, so: mandatory (no window, no choice), repeatable (no once-per-turn limit; two Recon actions in one turn are +2), usable while bowed, and in force **wherever the card is in play** (Home, a battlefield, attached) -- not while it sits in a province, a discard or dead pile, or a hand. It reacts only to the *controller's own* actions: an opponent's Recon action does nothing to it, and each controller's own copy fires only for their own actions.

*Where "the resolution of an action" is.* The funnel is the tail of `ExecuteAbilityEffects` (every synchronously resolving ability, Reactions and Strategy-sourced actions included, before any Reaction window to the same action opens -- traits resolve first). Two actions resolve later, and reach the same helper from their own completion points: a **deferred Ranged/Melee Attack** (an attack reaction window opened) fires from `ResolveAttack`, and a **duel-issuing** ability from the end of `ResolveDuel`; both tested, both fire exactly once, neither at announcement. Known limits: the inherited-keyword half of a *deferred Attack* is read off the performing Personality (the pending Attack keeps the performer, not the ability's own card); actions that are not abilities (`playStrategy`, `equip`, `recruitReserve` -- the controller acting generically) are never "a Recon action".

**3. The +1F: until the end of the turn (`ApplyModifier`) -- the convention this changes Ebizo the most on, so it is called out.** "Give Ebizo +1F" states no duration. The repo's rule (`EMPEROR_EDITION_CORE_RULES.md`, Duration): *ongoing effects (stat changes, ...) last until the end of the current turn unless a different duration is stated*, and every precedent for a duration-less "Give X +nF" follows it: Apprentice Shinobi's own "After the resolution of a Ninja action: Give this card +1F" (the direct precedent: an `ApplyModifier`, cleared by `FinishTurn`'s `Modifiers.Clear()`, stacking with itself), Mirumoto Yozo Experienced 2's "+2F" after an Iaijutsu action, Prepared for Death, Crushing Strength, Defensive Stance, Tactical Advantage and the rest. *Permanent* stat changes exist only where the card says so (a printed token, e.g. "+1F Fire token", or a `GrantedKeyword` with Duration `Permanent` -- the "permanent grants" the client renders): I found no precedent that reads a bare "Give X +nF" as permanent, so the precedent is not contradictory. The materially different alternative (each Recon action permanently raising his Force) would make him grow without bound over a game; if the rules lead intends that reading, it is a one-line change (a Force token instead of a modifier).

**4. Ebizo's Battle ability.** `ebizo-attack`: Ranged Attack, strength `CardForce` of the performing Ebizo read when the Attack is declared -- live, so a Recon action's +1F this turn raises it (tested: a Force-6 target survives strength 4 and is destroyed at strength 6 after two Recon actions), and, as for every Attack, a declared target above the strength is refused while the action needs no target up front. It is not itself a Recon action (no self-trigger).

## Other cards sharing the trigger (reported; only Master Scouts is wired)

- **Master Scouts** -- wired (above).
- **Daidoji Akeha** (`1621`): "Reaction: After your Recon action from a Strategy resolves: Draw a card" -- a *Reaction* (optional) restricted to Strategy-sourced Recon actions, plus a deck-search ability; not trivial.
- **Daidoji Narizane** (`1663`), **Advanced Intelligence** (`10135`): need a per-turn / per-battle *count* of Recon actions taken (a counter this trait does not keep); Advanced Intelligence also reacts to a Recon *Strategy's* action.
- **Beyond the Line** (`967`, use a Limited/Open Recon ability as a Battle action), **Hidden Falls Dojo** (`3257`, an extra Maneuvers segment): need effects of their own; the Recon classification is available to them.

## Open questions for the rules lead

1. The duration of "Give Ebizo +1F" (decision 3): until end of turn (implemented, per the core rules and Apprentice Shinobi) versus permanent.
2. Should a Recon *Reaction played in the Engage window* be able to trigger Ebizo *before* the first-action decision? (Implemented: yes, immediately on resolution; the window's own order is unaffected.)
3. The inherited-keyword check for a deferred Attack uses the performer's keywords (documented limit above).

## Verification

`dotnet run --project tests/L5R.Core.Tests` (new `ReconActionTests`: 11 tests -- each controller's own action fires each Ebizo/Master Scouts (two in one turn = +2, live Force), Home / battlefield / bowed, never out of play, never for the opponent or a non-Recon action, ability tag vs inherited keyword, deferred Attack and duel resolve-time firing, Yuki's Reaction, the Ranged Attack strength, the until-end-of-turn duration, Master Scouts). Mutation checks (each made the intended test fail): trait firing for either player, only on a battlefield, ignoring the inherited keyword, no fire after a deferred Attack, no fire after a duel, a constant attack strength, the bonus amount.
