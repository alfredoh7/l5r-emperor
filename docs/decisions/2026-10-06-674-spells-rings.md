## What the scans changed

* **Keywords.** The scans of Among the Clouds, Banished, Blistering Rain, Cast Aside, Flame Lash, Mystical Augmentation, Searing Siege, Soul of Earth, Study the Wheel, Tempest of Air and The Tao of Fudo print no keyword line: the element the catalogue lists ("Air", "Fire", ...) is the keyword of the card's own action ("Air Open", "Fire Battle"), carried as the ability's `ActionKeywords`, as spell-wave-50 already does. Blood Wave prints Maho, Hidden Power Unique, The Thunderer's Protection Thunder and Armor, the Rings their element, Fudo and Unique.
* **Blood Wave's text.** The catalogue's "Maho Remote Battle" is, on the scan, "Battle: Even if this unit is not at the current battlefield, bow this Shugenja and destroy your target Personality or Follower: ...": `SourceUnitMayBeAway`. No new "Remote" keyword is needed.
* **Costs.** "Bow" in a printed cost is the bow icon, "bow this card" (Coils of Madness rule 5), so the Spell bows, not the Shugenja; where the effects say "bow this Shugenja" (Among the Clouds, Tempest of Air, Hidden Power) it is an effect.

## Readings built in (not confirmed rulings)

1. **"If this Shugenja is unbowed" (Among the Clouds, Tempest of Air, Hidden Power)** is a condition of the whole action: it may not be taken while he is bowed (an action that could do nothing is never offered), and the effects re-check it as they resolve. The Shugenja's bow is an effect, so a negated action does not bow him.
2. **Cast Aside Appearances.** Coils of Madness rule 6 (every other sentence is a plain effect) makes "If this Shugenja is Human" govern only the first sentence: a Shugenja who is not Human still has the Spell destroyed and loses 1 Honor, and gains nothing. "Nonhuman" is given as a keyword; he keeps whatever Human keyword he printed. The permanent +2F and the granted Battle action ("Bow a target enemy card with lower Force": a Personality or Follower whose Force is lower than the holder's) are permanent grants.
3. **Hidden Power.** "Put a Spell in your discard pile into your hand" is a choice among your own Fate-discard Spells (never another player's), not a target in the Reaction sense; then the Shugenja bows and the Spell is removed from the game.
4. **Blood Wave.**
   * The destroyed card is the cost card ("destroy your target Personality or Follower"), chosen with the action: one of your own at the battlefield, never the wielder himself (he would be destroyed performing the Attacks) and never the Spell.
   * Its Force counts after its destruction (the Torn Asunder ruling: it excludes the Force of his Items), halved, rounded down.
   * "Two consecutive Ranged Attacks" are two Attacks of the same strength: the first Attack's target is the action's, the second is chosen once the first has resolved (Thunder's Wrath's sequenced target). A reaction that negates the action negates both, as Engulfing Flames' ruling already reads it.
5. **Study the Wheel** may name a card in either player's province, turns it face-up if it was not (then it is public), and gives Kharmic until the end of the turn (R-001); the reminder text in parentheses is the universal Kharmic action, not an ability of the card.
6. **Banished from the Realm** gives Expendable to any card of yours in play with the Spirit keyword, until the end of the turn; destroying it that turn draws a card. **Among the Clouds'** Cavalry is also until the end of the turn (target and each Follower attached to him).
7. **The Tao of Fudo** is two catalogue entries sharing one use ("straighten it" - a Ring of yours in play; "put it in your hand" - a Ring of yours in play or in your Fate discard pile), each destroying the Spell.
8. **The Thunderer's Protection.** Offered only when the action has a performer in play to bow (no window opens for nothing); the performers are the action's performer (or the source's wielder) and every performing slot; they bow after the action has resolved, through the "bow" path that Stalwart can negate. Either player's action, targeting any card in the unit (not only a Personality).
9. **False Ring of Fire.** The Interrupt is the Honor-change window of Mendon's R4 ruling (Shiro Chugo's): played before any Honor gain or loss from an action's effects (any player's), it reduces each by 2 (to at most 0), once, with the Ring bowed as its cost. Its recruit loses the Honor while the Ring is still in the hand, so the new Ring cannot offer to interrupt its own entry.
10. **False Ring of the Void.** "After a battle resolves where you destroyed a Province" is granted exactly like Air's (the battle-recruit grant, only for the Attacker, only on a Province destroyed by that resolution). Its Engage "will not move this turn" locks Home and To-battlefield movement (the lock the existing "will not move" effects use), not assignment.
11. **Searing Siege.** The discarded card's Focus Value is its Focus stat; the Attack is the performer's Ranged Attack and the action is repeatable (the Battle actions still alternate).

The engine names a face-down card only by its province (its id is hidden from the view), and the command carries one. Question for Mendon: two separate entries sharing one use, or a two-province selection?
* **Witness the Untold** (oracle-l5r-10357), TRIG-AFTER: "After your Ring enters play" has no Reaction trigger (every Ring enters through its own recruit case); the look and reorder already exist.
