Source: primitive P13 of `2026-10-05-remaining-unwired-primitives-prompts.md` (issue #668), with Mendon's override that the primitive ships alone, proven by test-only fixtures, as P12 did. ## What it is

A small store of rules a card's action or Event sets for a scope, `MatchState.RuleFlags`, and eight kinds of rule, each read **once**, at the choke point it changes. A flag is `(Id, Kind, Scope, PlayerId, Battlefield, Battle, Turn, Until, WhileInPlay, Amount, Arg, Source)`. The store, its id sequence and the mid-command marker are omitted from the saved game until used (`RuleFlags` is a nullable list; `RuleFlagSequence` and `ActionPhaseEndedAtVersion` are omitted at their defaults; property orders 22600-22602), so a match that never set a flag saves, replays and plays exactly as before.

## The API the wave calls

```csharp
// From a card's RunEffects (feature file; ids and priorities >= 10000 as ever):
engine.SetRuleFlag(RuleFlagKinds.NoEngageForAttacker, RuleFlagScope.Battlefield, ownerId, "Lantern Oil", battlefield: province, whileInPlay: card.Id);
engine.SetRuleFlag(RuleFlagKinds.NegateBattleActionWithoutUnits, RuleFlagScope.Battlefield, ownerId, "Temple Grounds", battlefield: province, whileInPlay: card.Id);
engine.SetRuleFlag(RuleFlagKinds.NoDestructionHonorForOthers, RuleFlagScope.Battle, ownerId, "Shosuro Takazaki");
engine.SetRuleFlag(RuleFlagKinds.DefendersMayNotPlayTerrains, RuleFlagScope.Turn, ownerId, "Journey's End Siege");
engine.SetRuleFlag(RuleFlagKinds.LimitedAsOpenOncePerTurn, RuleFlagScope.Player, ownerId, "The Madness Spreads", until: engine.UntilOwnersSecondTurnBegins(ownerId));
engine.SetRuleFlag(RuleFlagKinds.NoEnlightenmentVictory, RuleFlagScope.Game, ownerId, "The False Path", whileInPlay: stronghold.Id);
engine.SetRuleFlag(RuleFlagKinds.MadnessLoss, RuleFlagScope.Game, null, "An Empire of Madness", amount: 20);   // null owner: each player; Amount: the threshold
engine.SetRuleFlag(RuleFlagKinds.EndActionPhase, source: "Chagatai's Wrath");                                       // applied at once, stored nowhere
engine.RemoveRuleFlag(id);
```

`SetRuleFlag(kind, scope = Turn, playerId = null, source = "", battlefield = null, amount = 0, arg = null, until = null, whileInPlay = null)` returns the stored `RuleFlag` (null for the two kinds that are not stored here). An illegal request (a Battle-scope flag with no battle open, a Player-scope flag with no player, a madness-loss flag with no threshold) throws the engine's `RuleViolation` and changes nothing; an unknown kind is an `ArgumentException`.

## Kinds and where each is read

| Kind | Read at | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `end-action-phase` | `EndActionPhase`, called from `SetRuleFlag` | The next printed phase of the turn's order begins now through `BeginPhaseAfterAction` (so it honours `PhaseOrder`), the additional-action burst is forfeited, the phase change is logged. Outside the Action Phase it does nothing and returns false. `NextAction` skips handing priority on for the action that ended the phase (marker `ActionPhaseEndedAtVersion == Version`, the same command only). |
| `no-engage-for-attacker` | `EngageActionBarred`: the list of abilities (hidden) and `ValidateAbility` (refused) | While the Engage window is open at a covered battlefield, the Attacker's Engage actions (`after-engaging` / `after-engaging-at-own-battlefield` Reactions, which is how the engine models "Engage:") are not offered or accepted. The Defender's still are. An Engage window nobody can use does not open (existing rule). |
| `negate-battle-action-without-units` | `RuleFlagNegatesBattleAction`, in the announce chain of `ContinueUseAbility` beside the P7 watch | A Battle action by a player with no unit at the battlefield is taken, its costs paid, its effects negated (`ResolveNegatedAction`), unless the flag's owner controls a Cavalry unit there. Protection still beats the negation. |
| `no-destruction-honor-for-others` | `ResolutionHonor`, at the four battle-resolution Honor lines | The winner gains no Honor for the units he destroys that belong to the flag's owner (a win and a tie), logged `ruleFlagHonorWithheld`. The owner's own gains and the other seat's are unchanged. |
| `defenders-may-not-play-terrains` | `RuleFlagTerrainReason`: `PlayStrategy`, the Terrain listing, and a Terrain played through its own ability (a `ValidateLate` hook) | The player whose turn it is not may not play a Terrain; it is refused with the reason and listed as unavailable. |
| `limited-as-open-once-per-turn` | P4's `LimitedAsOpen` standing rule (`TurnBoundaryRules.cs`) | Recorded as `AddTurnScopedRule(LimitedAsOpen, ..., amount: 1, arg: "Strategy")`, not in this store. P4 already gives exactly this primitive (once per turn, narrowed to a Strategy source, Plans Within Plans asked first) with its own choke point and tests, so P13 adds no second mechanism and no edit to `LimitedInsteadOfOpen`. |
| `no-enlightenment-victory` | `EnlightenmentBarred`, in `StartTurn`'s Enlightenment test | The flag's owner (null: each player) does not win by Enlightenment. |
| `madness-loss` (Amount) | `MadnessLossAtTurnEnd`, first line of `FinishTurn` | The player whose turn is ending loses (the other seat wins) when Amount or more Madness tokens are on the Personalities he owns in play. |

## Scopes

`Turn` lasts until the turn ends; `Battle` until the battle that was current when it was set ends; `Game` until the game ends. `Player` and `Battlefield` bind the flag to a player or a battlefield and last until their `Until` (a P4 `TurnUntil`: "until your next turn begins"), or as long as the card named by `WhileInPlay` stays in play, or, with neither, until the turn ends. `Until` and `WhileInPlay` narrow any scope. A flag whose battlefield is null covers every battlefield. Expired flags are ignored at every read; they are dropped as the next flag is set and as each turn begins (a `TurnBegins` callback), and the store returns to null when empty.

## Readings for Mendon to confirm or correct

- **"Engage actions" are the Engage window's Reactions.** The engine has no separate Engage timing: an "Engage:" action and an "After engaging" Reaction are both `Reaction` abilities with an `after-engaging` trigger. Lantern Oil's bar covers both kinds, hides them from the Attacker's list and refuses them. If a printed "After engaging" Reaction should stay allowed, the bar needs a flag on the ability to tell the two apart.
- **"Units here" is Personalities at the battlefield.** `Army(player, battlefield)` is the engine's notion of a unit (a Personality and what is attached). A Cavalry unit is a Personality with the Cavalry keyword (printed or granted) or one with a Cavalry Follower attached.
- **Temple Grounds negates Battle actions only**, i.e. abilities with the Battle or BattleOrOpen timing in the Battle phase. A Terrain played by `playStrategy` (a Battle action in rules terms, allowed with no unit only by Flooded Pass) and a rulebook action are not covered; the negation chain is the announce chain, so an action consumed as an additional action (which skips that chain, as it skips the other negators) is not negated either.
- **Shosuro Takazaki** is read as "the winner gains nothing for the units of the flag's owner that the resolution destroys", in a win and in a tie. Other Honor tied to destruction (The Earth Flows, Halls of Memory, a card's own "gain 1 Honor if destroyed") is a card effect, not "Honor for destroying" in the resolution, and is untouched.
- **"Defenders" is the player whose turn it is not.** A flag with an owner binds the owner's opponents only, so the card's controller is never barred from his own Terrain on his turn or on the other's.
- **The flag's owner is the controller by `OwnerId`**, as the rest of this engine reads control (a control swap mid-turn is not followed). Madness tokens are counted on the Personalities the checked player owns in play.
- **`madness-loss` is checked as the turn finishes** (after the End Phase draw and hand-size discard), the point at which "ends his turn" is complete; the other player wins. A loss on the same turn by Honor, Dishonor or Enlightenment is decided before it by the existing checks.
- **A negated or ended-early action still pays.** `end-action-phase` pays the Limited action's costs like any action and ends the phase even if the active player held more actions; a reaction window the same action opens (a bow window) is still offered in the new phase, and priority stays with the active player as that phase began.
