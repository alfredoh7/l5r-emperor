# Battle Phase timing: the Engage window, first-action claims (with Naval Invasion), the general before-resolution window, the public battle-step view -- and Daidoji Yuki, Experienced

Status: accepted for implementation, with the assumptions and open questions below flagged for the rules lead.
Lane: rules (shared: adds public view fields and new legal-action shapes the client must render). Issues: #309 (Yuki wave), #310 (Battle timing, the primary issue; supersedes #15). UI counterpart: #311 (battle sidebar).

## The spec this implements (verbatim, rules lead)

> The battle Phase has several formal segments:
> 1. Declaration Segment (a player chooses to declare an attack or not)
> 2. Infantry Maneuvers (If a player declared an attack they choose to assign infantry first followed by the defender who assigns infantry second)(an attacker is not compelled to assign any units to attack)
> 3. Cavalry Maneuvers (if a player declared an attack, they choose to assign cavalry first followed by the defender who assigns cavalry second) (an attacker is not compelled to assign any units to attack)
> 4. Fight Battles
>    4.1 The attacker chooses which battle to resolve first.
>    4.2 Players are allowed to play "Engage" actions and Reactions that say "after engaging" these resolve immediately. If there are multiple engage reactions, the player whose turn it is decides the order that they resolve in.
>    4.3 If the attacker has played an engage reaction (or has the most naval cards) and their ability to take the first action has not been negated by the defender negating the action or using an action that grants the defender the first battle action, the attacker may play the first action
>    4.4 Otherwise, the defender plays the first action
>    4.5 Each player then may play Battle Actions in alternating order until both players pass subsequently (a player may pass and then, later, choose to take an action, as long as both players haven't passed in immediate sequence (one after the other) the battle will continue until both do.
> 5. Resolution - cards that trigger "before resolution" take effect once both players have passed. If there are multiple of these, the player whose turn it is chooses the order these effects are applied. Then, the armies forces are totaled, compared, and the outcome is determined.

Rulings on Daidoji Yuki and first-action effects (same source, verbatim from the brief):

> "After engaging at Yuki's battlefield" means: After Cavalry maneuvers, when the battle that Yuki is at (attacking or defending) is selected by the attacking player to be resolved (that's the engagement timing for a battlefield). The possessive use of "Yuki's battlefield" actually means "the battlefield to which Yuki is assigned". Importantly, if Daidoji Yuki is defending and the attacker uses an effect to take the first action in the battle (e.g., Sneak Attack, Naval, or even their own copy of Daidoji Yuki), using Daidoji Yuki's ability cancels the use of another "take the first action in battle" ability and the defender plays the first battle action. This may require an additional primitive, which is that "take the first action in battle" abilities do not stack, granting multiple first battle abilities. If a player gains the first action via Naval and plays Sneak attack, and uses Daidoji Yuki's ability, they still gain only one first battle action AND if the defender plays any reaction that allows them to take the first action, no matter what, the defender is granted the first opportunity to act.

Reaction windows in general (rules lead, relayed): "if an action opens a reaction window and multiple reactions could be applied, they may all be taken. If there is a set of reactions >1, then the player whose turn it is has the first opportunity to play a reaction followed by the other player, alternating until both pass."

## Decisions

**1. The Engage window (spec 4.2).** `SelectBattle` is the engagement moment. It seeds the default (Defender holds the first opportunity, spec 4.4) and then `MatchEngine.OpenEngageWindow` opens the window (`PendingChoice` type `engageReaction`, state `MatchState.PendingEngage`) when either player has at least one *legal* "After engaging" Reaction or "Engage:" action -- the same "only open a window worth opening" convention every other Reaction window here uses, so a battle nobody can react to is exactly the pre-#310 flow, event stream included. The window is the engine's ordinary Reaction round on the existing `PassReaction` machinery: the turn player (the Attacker) has the first opportunity, each reaction resolves the moment it is played (no queue) and hands the opportunity to the other player, a play resets the pass count, and two passes in succession close it. Eligibility is re-evaluated at every opportunity (the offered list is `AbilityActions`, which validates each candidate). This is how "the player whose turn it is decides the order": at each of their opportunities they choose which reaction to play, or pass. Only `useAbility` / `declineReaction` / `pass` are accepted inside it, and only Engage/"after engaging" Reactions: a Battle action is refused until the window closes (`ValidateAbility`).

*Information note:* as with Singh Remnants' and Effortless Counterattack's windows, a window that opens only when a candidate exists tells the opponent that a candidate exists (a hand Strategy included); which card it is stays hidden until it is played. Always opening the window would avoid that at the cost of two forced passes in every battle.

*Sequencing question for the rules lead:* "Engage:" actions and "After engaging" Reactions are treated identically (the Coils of Madness supplement: "Engage plays exactly like a Reaction to the trigger 'After engaging'"), so both are Reactions to trigger `after-engaging` / `after-engaging-at-own-battlefield`. If Engage actions should sequence differently from Reactions, that is open.

*Known limit (shared with every Reaction window here):* `PendingChoice` has no stack, so a Reaction played inside the window is never deferred behind Rokai/Tamoko/Champion/Announce/Unimpeachable-Name windows (`UseAbility` skips them while `engageReaction` is open); opening one would overwrite the window and strand its claims.

**2. The first-action claim (spec 4.3/4.4) -- one primitive, decided once.** Effects call `ClaimFirstOpportunity(beneficiary, kind, source)`; nothing is decided until the window closes, when `ResolveFirstOpportunity` returns one result: the Defender's claim wins whatever order the claims were made in; otherwise the Attacker's, and any number of claims by one player are still one opportunity (they do not stack); no claim keeps the default. Claims are recorded when the granting effect *resolves*, so a granting action that is negated never records one (there is no general action-negation reaction for these yet -- see open questions). The reason is public: `Default`, `Naval`, `EngageGrant`, or `DefenderOverride` (a defender claim that overrode an attacker claim; a defender claim with nothing to override is `Default` made explicit; the claims are listed while the window is open). The beneficiary is what the card names (Sneak Attack's "The Attacker" is the Attacker whoever plays it).

**3. Daidoji Yuki, Experienced (`oracle-l5r-1696`).** `yuki-engage-recon`, Timing Reaction, Trigger `after-engaging-at-own-battlefield`, tagged `ActionKeywords: ["Recon"]`. "Yuki's battlefield" is the battlefield he is assigned to (`AtBattle`), attacking or defending. "If all your Personalities there are Scouts" sits before the effects colon, so it is a requirement to take the action (`ValidateAbility`), not a condition on part of the effect. It records a first-opportunity claim and grants Reconnaissance -- see decision 5. Once per turn, unbowed, like any ability.

**4. Naval Invasion (rules reference, Glossary -- Naval), implemented as a documented behavior change to existing published play.** The repo's rules reference states it: `Reaction: After engaging, once per battle, if you are the Attacker and your current army has more Naval cards than the side opposing it, choose your performing Naval Personality: You have the first opportunity to take a Battle action, which he must perform.` It is the universal player ability `naval-invasion` (like Tactical Advantage), a claim of kind `Naval` through the same primitive. Assumptions to confirm (also in the issue's open questions): (a) *counting* -- Naval-keyword cards in each side's army at the fought battlefield: Personalities and every attachment on them, printed or granted (`Keyword()`: Suitengu's Path, Scourge of the Sea, Isawa Kojiro's live Naval count); a Naval Follower is a Naval card of its own; STRICTLY more than the opposing side, a tie claims nothing; (b) the performer is a Naval *Personality* in the Attacker's army there (bowed is not excluded -- the text says only "Naval"); (c) "which he must perform" binds the Attacker's FIRST Battle action, and follows the core rules' Choose Performers: an action on a Personality or Follower is performed by that card itself, an Item's/Spell's by its wielder, a named performer by that card -- so a Follower's own action, a performer-less Strategy and Reserve recruitment cannot be the first action; passing remains legal and ends the limit, as does any accepted action (`FirstActionRestriction`); (d) once per BATTLE, not per turn (exempt from the once-per-turn dedupe; `PendingEngage.NavalInvasionUsed` enforces it); (e) the Defender's claim overrides it, and then the limit never applies; (f) Naval together with another attacker-side grant is still one opportunity and Naval's "which he must perform" is kept (open question -- the alternative is "unrestricted if any claim is unrestricted"). Naval is an *optional Reaction*, as printed -- not an automatic claim -- because the spec says it is negatable "exactly like an engage grant" (a Reaction is an action; an automatic effect would not be), and the AI opponent, which always passes optional reactions, therefore never uses it (a practice-bot limitation, not a rule).

*Wired cards this changes existing play for* (pinned by `PublishedShape`, computed from the catalogue, none in the pinned Lion/Crane decks): printed Naval Personalities Moshi Ikako (`10857`), Tsuruchi Yakusho (`8895`), Matsu Miura (`10853`), Yoritomo Minori (`9605`), Tsuruchi Isas (`8840`), Yoritomo Hofu (`10299`), Moshi Tomeno (`5285`), Tsuruchi Shigekazu (`8879`), Yoritomo Doho (`9539`), Yoritomo Nakoshi (`9610`), Yoritomo Tsang (`9834`), and -- added by Kahoku's personality-wave-24 while this branch was open -- Yoritomo Ebumi (`9832`); Isawa Kojiro (`3850`, Naval while he has a Spell); and the two granters Scourge of the Sea (`6501`, a Follower) and Suitengu's Path (`10133`, a Spell). A game where an Attacker's army at the fought battlefield has strictly more Naval cards than the Defender's now opens the Engage window for that Attacker in every such battle; nothing else about existing play changes, and Naval Invasion is never used automatically.

*Later printings word Naval differently.* The reminder text on Moshi Yokohime reads "Once a turn, the Attacker gets the first Battle action, if it's from a Naval Personality's unit" -- per turn, from a Naval Personality's *unit*, and no majority requirement. The Emperor Edition reference text (above, which the spec's "most naval cards" matches) governs here; recorded so the two are not conflated.

**5. Reconnaissance for a defending Yuki -- not forced.** Reconnaissance is `ProvinceState.Reconnaissance[playerId]` on the province the battle is fought at, always the Defender's. The engine's standing Reconnaissance rule (`MovementCannotBeNegated`, Shinjo Kinto's decision-note) is that a Defender can only hold it at an *enemy* province, never their own, so an Attacker's Yuki gains it (`GrantReconnaissance`, reset when the battle resolves like every grant) and a Defending Yuki's grant is a no-op, reported with a `reconnaissanceNotGained` event; his first-opportunity claim is unaffected. This was NOT forced, but it is the first published card where the assumption bites, and the data model itself (`bool[2]` on every province of both players; the Glossary's "a state a player can hold at one or more battlefields"; defending Recon cards such as Qalyar's "give its province +2 strength") suggests defenders may well hold it at their own battlefield. Flipping it is one condition in `yuki-engage-recon` plus generalizing the two readers that address the province owner as `1 - player` (`hiruma-sniper-attack`, `keppo-destroy`); left for the rules lead.

**6. Rebuilding Kyuden Hida now matters.** Its own decision note (2026-09-16-five-events) deferred its exception clause until a first-opportunity claim could exist: "the Attacker may not announce a Battle action before the Defender's first normal opportunity to act or pass" with the spec's exception ("an attacker has the most naval cards, or plays Sneak Attack, must pass instead"). Now reachable, so `CloseEngageWindow` sets a `MustPass` limit when the Attacker holds the first opportunity while the event is in play: the Attacker may only pass; the Defender then has their first normal opportunity; the default flow is untouched. Reading to confirm: the Attacker's forced pass counts as a pass, so a Defender who then passes ends the segment (the alternative -- the Attacker's first-opportunity claim simply cancelled and the Defender opening as usual -- would let the Attacker act after a Defender pass).

**7. The general before-resolution window (spec 5).** `PendingBeforeBattleResolve`/`AdvanceBeforeBattleResolve` (issue #272) already had the right ordering and placement (after both pass, before Army/Force are read; the active player chooses the order among several); only its source list was hard-coded to Encircled Terrain. It now discovers every in-play card at the fought battlefield (on it, or attached to a unit there) carrying an ability of Timing `BeforeResolution` -- or whose Effect *names* one, which is how Encircled Terrain is registered without changing its published data ("migrated": `encircled-before-battle` is now an `AbilityCatalog` entry; its three existing tests pass unchanged). A trait is mandatory (applies at its turn) or optional (`beforeBattleOptional`: its own controller declines or names the target); each source reads the battlefield live, so an earlier source's effect (someone sent home, bowed, boosted) is reflected by the next, which is why ordering can change who fights. Synthetic fixtures prove: a mandatory effect, a mandatory effect that changes who fights, an optional "you may" effect, and two simultaneous sources ordered by the turn player. Not built (the requirement was not to wire other real cards): action-registered *delayed* effects ("Before this battle resolves, bow him" from a Battle action) and *Reactions* to the trigger ("Reaction: Before a battle resolves" -- Steady Resolve, Akodo Dairuko). See the unblocked report below.

**8. Previously-unlisted windows are now offered.** `QueryActions` had no branch for `beforeBattleOrder`, `encircledOrder` or `encircledSelect` (the phase's ordinary actions were listed instead, which `Apply` rejects), so a client or the AI could not actually answer an Encircled Terrain. They now list their real options, as do the new `engageReaction` and `beforeBattleOptional` windows; the practice AI scores the four new answer types.

**9. The public battle-step view (`MatchView.BattleStep`, `BattleStepView`).** Derived from authoritative state on every `GetView`, identical for both viewers (asserted), optional so an older payload still parses, and round-tripped through both the strict server serializer and the client's lenient one (`L5R.Client.Transport.Tests`). Fields the UI should read: `Segment` (`None` / `InfantryManeuvers` / `CavalryManeuvers` / `FightBattles` / `Resolution`), `Step` (`AttackerAssigns` / `DefenderAssigns` / `ChooseBattle` / `Engage` / `BattleActions` / `BeforeResolution` / `Aftermath`), `DecisionPlayerId`, `AttackerPlayerId`, `BattlefieldProvince`, `FirstActionPlayerId` + `FirstActionReason` (`Default` / `Naval` / `EngageGrant` / `DefenderOverride`; provisional while the Engage window is open, `FirstActionResolved` false), `EngageClaims` (`PlayerId`, `Kind` `Naval`|`Engage`, `SourceId`; only while the window is open), `FirstActionLimit` (`MustPass` / `PerformerOnly`) + `FirstActionPerformerId`, `ConsecutivePasses` (0 or 1; two ends the segment), `BeforeResolutionSourceIds` + `ActiveBeforeResolutionSourceId`, `Window` (the open `PendingChoice` type). The names are the constants in `BattleSteps`.

## Open questions for the rules lead

1. **Declaration Segment.** Should declining an attack be a single explicit decision that skips Infantry/Cavalry Maneuvers (and stops prompting the defender for defenders when no attack was declared)? Today it takes up to four empty passes. Changing the phase machine ripples into AI/server/client tests, so it is listed, not changed.
2. **Reconnaissance for a defender** (decision 5): may a Defender hold Reconnaissance at their own battlefield? If yes, a defending Yuki gains it and the two `1 - player` readers need generalizing.
3. **Negating a first-action grant.** Claims are recorded on resolution, so negating the granting action prevents the claim; no general "negate this action" reaction exists for these actions yet (only the destroy/move/straighten interrupts). Nothing to build until a real card negates one.
4. **Before-resolution Reactions and delayed effects.** Should "Reaction: Before a battle resolves" (Steady Resolve, Akodo Dairuko) open an alternating reaction round at the start of the before-resolution stage, and where in the order do action-registered delayed effects ("Before this battle resolves, bow him") sit relative to Terrain traits?
5. **Naval assumptions (decision 4)**: counting, strictly-more, the Follower rule for "which he must perform", Naval combined with another grant, and Naval as printed-Reaction (this PR) versus later printings' per-turn "from a Naval Personality's unit" wording.
6. **Rebuilding Kyuden Hida's forced pass** (decision 6): does the forced pass count toward the two consecutive passes?

## Cards unblocked (or nearly) by this work -- not wired

Real cards that use the timing built here (none wired; the fixtures are `training-*`). "Unblocked" = the timing exists and only the card's own effect is missing.

- **Sneak Attack (`oracle-l5r-7311`)**: *unblocked.* "Engage: The Attacker has the first opportunity to take a Battle action or pass in this battle." is exactly `training-engage-attacker-first` (a Strategy whose ability is Reaction / `after-engaging`, claim for the Attacker); wiring is that ability declaration, a strategy-wave package and its scan.
- **Jade's Roar (`oracle-l5r-10223`)**: *nearly.* Recon Reaction at its own battlefield plus a claim (both built); the claim's "which must be a Terrain action" is a new `FirstActionRestriction` variant (a required-keyword limit, including the `playStrategy` Terrain path).
- **Reconnaissance Scouts (`oracle-l5r-10519`)**: *nearly.* "Engage: You have Reconnaissance at this battlefield. If you are the Attacker, you have the first opportunity ..." -- the claim is conditional on being the Attacker (trivial); missing: a Follower-sourced Engage action scoped to its unit's battlefield and the optional "you may target ... -3F" effect target; Reconnaissance for a defender is question 2.
- **Shosuro Toson, Experienced 2 (`oracle-l5r-7177`)**: *nearly.* "Reaction: After engaging at Toson's battlefield: Discard a card from the current battlefield's province. If this discarded a Personality, you have the first opportunity ..., which must be performed by Toson." The claim and the performer restriction are exactly Naval Invasion's shape (`FirstActionRestriction.PerformerId`); missing: "discard a card from a province" (face-down, either owner, reveal, refill) and the conditional on what it discarded.
- **Also using the same timing** (not requested): Scouting the Pass (`oracle-l5r-6513`, "first chance ... which must be performed by that Scout" -- Naval's shape again), the other Recon Strategies (Burn the Towers, Forest Cover, Seeking the Truth, Surrounded!, Torch's Flame Flickers, Watchers in the Dark: each a hand-played "Reaction: After engaging" with a card-specific effect), Kyuden Hida exp / Koshin Keep / The Golden Plains (Stronghold Recon Reactions), Qalyar, Wasteland Scout, and roughly forty more "After engaging" / "Engage:" cards whose remaining gaps are their own effects (Follower/token creation, "will not move", province-strength-until-resolution-begins, ...). Every catalogue BLOCKERS entry that named the Engage window as its blocker was rewritten to name what actually remains.
- **Before-resolution (17 unwired of 19 catalogue cards, per the issue):** the Terrain traits are now unblocked as *timing* (a Terrain's trait is a `BeforeResolution` ability): Heart of Rokugan (a mandatory sweep, nearly unblocked), Fields of Victory, Warded Premises, Heart of Darkness, Rocky Terrain and A Moving Battle (each needs its own effect, several with a "you may" choice), Extended Maneuvers (two optional choices), Hard Pressed (a unit choice then an optional bow), Lakeside Retreat (a mandatory one-target choice). Needing something else: the action-registered *delayed* effects (Holding Cells, Power of the Night, Quick Recovery, Reckless Rush, The Tale of the Disgraced, The Wave Persists, Harsh Taskmaster, Strong Defenses' straighten) need a delayed-effect queue; the *Reactions* (Steady Resolve, Akodo Dairuko) and White Shore Plain's granted Tactical Reaction need a before-resolution reaction round (question 4).

## What clients and the AI must do

- **Client (Alfredo, #311):** read `MatchView.BattleStep` (fields above) rather than inferring timing. New legal-action types the generic inspector does not render yet: `beforeBattleOptional` (Choice `use` + TargetId, or `decline`), `beforeBattleOrder` (TargetId), `encircledOrder` (Choice `attacker`/`defender`), `encircledSelect` (TargetId); the Engage window is offered as `useAbility` entries (Naval Invasion has no CardId, so it appears with the card-less abilities) plus `declineReaction`, and `StepPracticeReaction` already auto-passes solo when passing is the only option. The generic reaction-window title ("REACTION WINDOW") applies to `engageReaction`.
- **AI:** declines optional Engage reactions (it never plays Yuki/Naval Invasion); answers the before-resolution choices with modest scores.
- **Replay/determinism:** all new state (`PendingEngage`, `FirstActionPlayerId`/`Reason`/`Restriction`) is plain serializable data; a snapshot taken mid-window continues identically (tested). The published full-game transcript replay is unchanged.
