## Readings for Mendon to confirm or correct

1. **Kitsune Merihiko - exp**: "Your Nonhuman Spirits" are Personalities in play that have both keywords (printed or granted) and that the same player controls; a Spirit that enters later has the ability from then on, and one that stops being a Spirit loses it. Merihiko is not himself a Spirit. The ability is the Spirit's own: it bows itself as the cost and it is the Attacker of its own Ranged 3 Attack. It goes when Merihiko leaves play.
2. **Kitsune Yuribara**: the Reaction creates one Butterflies Personality **at home** (a created Personality is made at home unless its text says "there"; this text does not). The forced-target rule reads "at this battlefield" as the Butterflies' own location, so it applies to another player's Battle actions while a Butterflies card stands at the current battlefield, and not while it is at home. "Battle action" is a Battle ability, or a Battle/Open ability used in the Battle phase. "If legal" is judged by asking whether the same action could legally name a Butterfly at the battlefield: when it can, naming anything else is refused; with several Butterflies any one satisfies it. The Butterflies' own controller's actions are not bound. "After another player's Battle action targets this card: Destroy this card" is mandatory, after the action resolves and before any Reaction to it, for every card the action named (a single target, an Attack's target, a duel's challenged Personality).
3. **Ninube Tsukau**: "permanently copies his Force" sets Tsukau's Force to the target's current Force (P22 `CopyStat`, permanent: a modifier that stays). "One of his keywords" and "one of his base abilities" are chosen by the player when there are several (one is taken automatically). **Known difference from the scan:** the ability choice lists every ability the target has now, granted ones included, where the scan says "base"; the engine's copy step has no printed-only filter and adding one would touch the shared copy code. It matters only for a target that has been granted an ability. The once-per-game use is spent when the action is taken. Tsukau's own ability does not itself copy abilities, so no one can copy it.
4. **Shinjo Kinto - exp2**: "straighten their units if you do not control them" is judged once both have moved: each switched Personality that Kinto's controller does not control is straightened with his attached cards (`StraightenUnit`); an own Personality stays as it was. If a negation stops the switch, nothing is straightened.
5. **Tamori Tsuchiya**: "if Tsuchiya is defending" is judged when the action is announced (he is a defending unit: his controller is the Defender and he stands in the defending army). The Stalwart and the Force bonus last until the end of the turn (the text gives no other duration). The bonus is his Force after the Spell has been destroyed as the cost, so a Force bonus the Spell gave him is already gone.
6. **Watersteel Sword**: "Water Open" is a label like every elemental action keyword so far (the engine checks no element for it). The target is another non-Unique Weapon on the same Personality; the Sword copies one ability (the player chooses when there are several) until the end of the turn. An ability that copies abilities, one that cannot be copied and a trait are not offered.
7. **Befriend the Tides**: "adjacent battlefield" is read as adjacent **unresolved** battlefield (P24 reading 6); the Spell bows as a cost even when the Shugenja's move is negated.
8. **Constructing the Self**: the Illusion is created at home. The Chi and Personal Honor are the Shugenja's current values (with his Items) as the Illusion is made, written once and not following him afterwards. "This Spell may remain bowed" is the existing `choose-straighten` choice in the Straighten Phase. "After the next time this game this Spell straightens or leaves play" is the P22 watch: the Illusion is removed from the game at the end of the command in which the Spell straightens (by any effect or the phase) or leaves play.
9. **Koan's Whisper**: "Reduce a Province's strength to 0" is a penalty sufficient to reach 0 until the end of the turn (the duration every province strength effect has), against either player's Province. The optional removal is a second mode of the same action (the two abilities share one use per turn): choosing it pays "remove five Koan cards you control from the game" as a cost when the action is announced, and Koan's Whisper itself is a Koan card you control that may be one of the five. The Province is destroyed if its player still has two or more Provinces when it resolves; if he no longer does, the cost stays paid and the strength is still reduced. The first mode cannot destroy.
10. **A Doomseeker's Tomb**: one token carrying three stats, each negated or delayed on its own (P24 reading 24); "your target Personality" is one you control.
11. **Harmony**: the performing Monk need not be unbowed or at a battlefield; the trait is permanent and stays with the card for the rest of the game (P24 readings 13 to 17).
12. **Kitsuki Judgment**: the Gold Cost is the target's unit's total (the Personality and his attachments) at the moment the action is announced; the +8 is judged then too (he is dishonorable, or Kolat, Ninja or Shadowlands, by keyword). The action is Political because the card has the Political keyword and an ability inherits its card's keywords. "He commits seppuku" is `CommitSeppuku` (rehonored, then destroyed, never negated).
13. **Overwhelming Chaos**: P24 reading 11 (the actor first, then the other; the played card is not part of the hand).
14. **Shifting Waves**: the two stats are exchanged for the turn by modifiers worked out from the noted values (P22 reading on Exchange); a Personality whose Chi becomes 0 is destroyed.
15. **Sorrowful Prayer**: "Remove this card from the game" is paid as a cost (it leaves the game even if the shuffle finds nothing); P24 reading 12 for a card that left the pile.
16. **Spiritual Coalescence**: the performing Spirit's Force is read after he has been destroyed as the cost, and so without his Items (the Blood Wave ruling's count). "It permanently copies the performer's abilities which do not themselves copy abilities" copies **all** of them at once, with no choice, skipping any the target already has. The additional action is unrestricted in timing.
17. **Strength in the Earth**: "Earth" is a label (no element check); the Monk is the performer and must be unbowed and at the battle as for any Battle action; the strength is his Force when the Attack resolves. P24 readings 7 to 10 for the Defender's home.
18. **Unexpected Discovery**: "your target opposed Personality (at the current battlefield)" is one of your own Personalities at the current battlefield with an enemy army opposing it (the engine's `own-opposed`); the move is P24's adjacent unresolved battlefield.
19. **Unsettling Gathering**: "the amount paid" is the Gold chosen for X; "Gold Cost equal to or lower" compares the attachment's current Gold Cost; the attaching restrictions of the target still apply (P22 reading); control of the attachment goes to the target Personality's controller.
20. **The Tower of Vigilance**: "this battlefield" is the battlefield of the Province the Fortification guards, so the Engage action is offered only while that Province is the one engaged, which makes it the Defender's. The Attacker has the first opportunity in the Engage window and passes before the Tower's controller may act. "If you have any units at this battlefield" is judged at resolution: with none the Tower still bows and no Guard is created. The Guard stands at the battlefield and is removed from the game when that battle ends.
21. **A Moving Battle**: "one of your opposed attacking Personalities" is the Attacker's: the Terrain's controller must be the Attacker and have a Personality at the battlefield that an enemy army opposes. The destination is an adjacent unresolved battlefield (P24 reading 6). The move is offered to the Terrain's controller in the before-resolution set and may be declined (once per turn like any Reaction).
