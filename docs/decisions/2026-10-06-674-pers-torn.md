# Issue #674, cluster "pers-torn": twenty-one Torn Asunder Personalities (personality-wave-131)

All twenty-one cards were wired from their own scans (Torn Asunder printing, receipt-verified WebP working copies) with existing mechanisms; none is blocked. Code: `src/L5R.Core/WlPersTorn.cs`; tests: `tests/L5R.Core.Tests/WlPersTornTests.cs`; packager: `tools/content/package_personality_wave_131.py`. Scan differences from the catalogue: Riku's second ability prints "Iaijutsu Battle:" (catalogue: "Battle:").

## Readings built in (for Mendon to confirm)

- **Akahiko**: "Gain 1 Honor" is independent of the move (gained even if a movement is negated). The -4 strength is Destrier's data (Both, Self).
- **Nanae**: the two "You may" sentences are one engine-listed choice of four options (straighten, -4F, both, neither); "Bow him if he is Shadowlands" always follows, last (so a straightened Shadowlands target ends bowed). Usable bowed, as Battle (needs her at the battle) and Open.
- **Izuna**: Political Limited reuses `noboru-exp2-favor`; the bow is Asako Tsunefusa's two-slot action over "another player's card in a unit" (Personality, or attached Follower/Item/Spell), one use shared by the Battle and Limited halves.
- **Kyuudo / Nagai**: "defending" = a Personality at a battlefield for the non-active player, read live; Elite is granted through `Keyword()`.
- **Suzukaze**: a turn-long GoldProduction modifier; a Holding with no production gets none (modifier not applied). Any Holding, either side's, may be named.
- **Wakui**: covers any Magistrate (either controller) at his battlefield, for any other player's action, if legal; not when he is bowed, at home, or the target himself.
- **Ibara**: a MovesToBattle (Absent) move of herself to the current battlefield; "now opposes" = she stands at the battlefield opposite the target's unit; -3F, and a Mantis Clan (alignment) target is bowed. A Follower is not itself Mantis Clan.
- **Iza / Kyoshiro / Riku**: duels are not refusable ("duels him"); targets are another player's Personality with that exact title; Open duels follow R-030 (Action Phase only).
- **Fubuko**: Bofana - exp's discard-play grant, naming Fubuko as performer; a validation makes the discard-pile play legal only when Fubuko performs it. A Strategy with no performing Personality cannot be played this way. "Dragon Clan player" discount via `dragon-clan-discount`.
- **Shaocheng**: modelled as an entry-play Reaction (the engine's convention for an entry trait with a target, `TraitReactions`), so it is optional, on any card in either player's provinces; the card is turned face up; a non-Unique Personality is discarded (province refilled face down) and made honorably dead.
- **Moshi Ira**: the -3F lands when the targeted action is truly done (`FinishActionEffectTracking`); notes of an action that never finished lapse at the next turn. Offered only when the action has a performing Personality. Applies to any player's action, own included.
- **Riku**: both Tamahime printings, any controller's; another player's action only.
- **Tetsaka / Daito / Tansen**: "X or Y" printed actions are two abilities sharing one use. Tetsaka's keyword lasts the turn (R-001). Daito: bow, or exactly 4 Gold; the hold is "until the next Straighten Phase ends, whoever's turn" (no owner).
- **Sihung**: "a Personality who performed it" = the action's recorded performer; Tactical per `ActionCarriesKeyword`.
- **Makiko - exp**: Chugo's before-an-Honor-change window, gains only (offered before a gain, never a loss), with a gains-only mark cleared per action.
- **Shinoko**: Brash lasts the turn (R-001), any Personality.
- **Suying**: mandatory trait where the resolution settles; counted when her attacking army destroyed units or the province fell (a defending Suying never qualifies).
