# 2026-10-05 - Favor cluster (issue #667, item-wave-45): Mempo of the Amethyst Champion

Mechanics lane (Mendon's rulings). Card wired: Mempo of the Amethyst Champion (oracle-l5r-9971, `item-wave-45`). Engine: `src/L5R.Core/FavorMempo.cs`; tests: `tests/L5R.Core.Tests/FavorMempoTests.cs`.

## Confirmed ruling built

"The reaction window for using a favor ability should be created, anyway, because Confusion at Court relies on the use (discard) of the favor to produce an effect that Confusion at Court negates." So the window "after you discard the Imperial Favor" exists whether or not the action's effects happen: a negated Favor action still discarded the Favor (a cost), and the window follows it.

## What was built

- **One helper for every Favor discard.** `ReleaseImperialFavor(playerId, asCost)` replaces the three direct `FavorPlayerId = null` sites (`DiscardImperialFavorCost`, which every Favor-discard action, negated action and alternative cost already shared; Kyuden Otomo - exp's token action; Burning Dreams). A cost's discard is recorded on the action in progress (`MatchState.CurrentActionFavorDiscarders`, snapshotted into `ResolvedActionFacts.FavorDiscarders`).
- **The window** is the existing after-action window (`afterActionResolvedReaction`, so the client and bots already present and pass it). An action that resolved opens it from `FinishActionEffectTracking`; a negated action opens it from `CompleteNegatedAction` through a synthetic record (`__favor-negated__`) that no other Reaction can read.
- **One additional use per turn**: a new additive hook channel, `EngineHooks.ExtraUsesPerTurn`, summed into `AbilityLimits.EffectiveUsesPerTurn` (zero when unhandled). Mempo's callback gives +1 to Favor abilities (printed Favor keyword, plus the four rulebook Favor actions that print none: `shunya-honor`, `favor-cycle`, `favor-cycle-kurohito`, `favor-battle`) of the controller of an attached Mempo, whose use dictionary is the player's or one of his cards'.
- **Any unit**: `favor-battle`'s "enemy-attacking" target category also admits any Personality at the current battlefield for the controller of an attached Mempo.
- **Take the Favor**: the Reaction (`pe-mempo-take-favor`) takes the Imperial Favor through the shared `TakeImperialFavor` (so Kyuden Otomo - exp's "after you take" sees it).
- **Attaches free**: `AttachRequiresKeyword "Amethyst Champion"` and a zero Gold price (`PromoItemsAttachesFree` asks `FavorMempoAttachesFree`).

## Readings built in (for Mendon to confirm)

1. **"After you announce ... It may target any player's unit"** is built as a wider target category chosen at announcement, not a re-selection after announcement. The announced action has the same cost, window and resolution; only the legal targets differ. "Any player's unit" is any Personality at the current battlefield (the action moves him home, and every Battle target must be at the battlefield), his own units included.
2. **Where the window sits.** Mempo's Reaction is offered in the after-action window (after the Favor action resolved, or was negated), not in the middle of the action between paying the cost and the effects. Confusion at Court's "before an action resolves" window stays separate, and still precedes it.
3. **"After you discard the Imperial Favor" means the Imperial Favor.** Discarding a Kyuden Otomo - exp Favor token instead (the cost option) is not discarding the Imperial Favor and opens nothing. An Event's own discard (Burning Dreams: "Discard the Imperial Favor" is an effect, not a cost) is recorded as no cost and opens no window (Events have no after-action window).
4. **Taking back**: the Reaction may be declined (R-005). It takes the Favor whoever controls it now (like every other "Take the Imperial Favor"), but is offered only while the controller does not already hold it. Lobby's Family Honor and bowing conditions are Lobby's own, not part of "take".
5. **"One additional time per turn"** adds one use to each Favor ability separately (a Favor ability is 1 + 1 uses, not a shared pool across abilities); an ability limited to once per game, or with no limit, is not changed. Using Mempo's own Reaction is subject to its ordinary once-per-turn limit.
6. **Window gate for a negated Reaction.** A negated action taken inside another open window (a negated Favor Reaction such as Kakita Tadanobu's) does not open the take-back window; only a negated action taken with no pending choice does. (A resolved action in any window still does.)

## Mendon's confirmations (follow-up)

Readings 1 and 3 are confirmed. He added: "only sending a personality home from battle (favor-battle) or discarding a card to draw a card (favor-cycle) trigger its ability". So Mempo's take-back Reaction is offered only after the rulebook `favor-battle` and `favor-cycle` actions (Kurohito's `favor-cycle-kurohito` is the card-cycling action's variant and counts); `shunya-honor` and card-printed Favor abilities discard the Favor too but do not offer it (`FavorMempoAbilities.TakeBackTriggers`). The generic after-Favor-discard recording and window stay for every Favor discard (Confusion at Court and any later reader). For a negated action the record carries the negated action's id (SourceId of the `__favor-negated__` facts). The wider Favor Battle target already includes the controller's own Personalities and any Personality at the battlefield, attacking or not.
