# Fusako, Gaijin Sorcerer and Sleep (#667, cluster "duel-sleep")

Status: built from Mendon's confirmed rulings; the readings below are engineering choices for him to confirm.

Waves: personality-wave-124 (Fusako, Gaijin Sorcerer), spell-wave-52 (Sleep). Each is packaged from its Promotional-Emperor scan (printed text wins: Fusako prints "Iaijustu Limited", the Sorcerer's trait is "While your ritual is resolving ...").

## Confirmed rulings built in

- **Fusako**: "The challenged personality may only refuse the duel if they are bowed. If they are straightened, they may choose to bow in order to refuse the challenge." A bowed target may refuse (`declineDuel`) or accept. A straightened target's controller is offered `bowToRefuseDuel`, which bows him and leaves the challenge open (he may then refuse or still accept); with no bow he can only accept. The refusal gate and the bow are in `DuelSleepFusako.cs`; the loser is destroyed like every other Iaijutsu duel.
- **Gaijin Sorcerer**: "The doubling only counts towards the count of shugenja and does not double the Chi contribution to a ritual." `RitualShugenjaCount` weights the Sorcerer as two for his controller's Ritual; Chi totals (Capturing the Soul, Illusory Defense) still read his Chi once.
- **Sleep**: cards are discarded first and summed; the value displayed is the sum minus one; each chosen Personality subtracts its Chi; no selection may take the value below 0; "Clear selection" empties the selection, "Confirm selection" applies the bow. Built as the reusable budgeted multi-select (`PendingBudgetSelect`, `DuelSleepBudget.cs`).

## Readings built in (not confirmed)

- "Discard an equal number of cards" counts the Shugenja that actually bowed (a negated bow does not count), the Sorcerer as two.
- Any Personality in play may be chosen for the second bow, bowed or not and of either player ("target Personalities", not "unbowed"); each costs its current Chi.
- The Spell is destroyed when the selection is confirmed (an empty confirm is legal), or at once when no Personality can be afforded and so no decision opens.
- Fusako's bow by the controller is a choice, not an enemy effect, so a bow-negation (Stalwart and the like) does not apply to it.
- The Sorcerer's doubling is read only inside a Ritual action's resolution of his own controller; no other card currently counts Shugenja for a Ritual.
- With a total of 0 (empty Fate deck) the displayed value is 0 and nothing can be chosen (Chi 0 is not less than 0).

## Client contract (new)

- Command `bowToRefuseDuel` (action type of the same name) during `PendingDuel.Stage == "AwaitingAccept"`; shown with the other challenge answers on the duel screen.
- Pending choice type `budgetSelect`: actions `budgetSelectAdd` (TargetId; disabled with a reason when the Chi no longer fits), `budgetSelectClear`, `budgetSelectConfirm`. The Confirm action carries `DecisionLabel` "Remaining value: N" and `DecisionCandidateIds` (the chosen Personalities). `Main.BudgetSelect.cs` renders them; clicking a Personality on the table adds it when offered. The bot adds enemy unbowed Personalities that fit, then confirms (`TrainingOpponent`).
