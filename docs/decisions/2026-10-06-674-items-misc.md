* `MatchEngine.FollowerForce`: `+ WlItemsMiscFollowerForce(card)` (A Fever in the Blood's -1F on an attacking Follower).
* `CardAbilities.Keyword`: `if (WlItemsMiscKeywordGranted(card, keyword)) return true;` (Hachiman's Prowess's Elite).
* `SeedsOfDecayBatch1.MayAssignBowed`: `|| WlItemsMiscMayAssignBowed(card)` (Hachiman's Prowess's "May assign even if bowed").

## Readings built in (for Mendon to confirm or correct; none is a ruling)

**Engage.** An Engage action is a Reaction to "after engaging" (the Coils of Madness supplement, rule 3), so Khalimpeh-jiak, The Bestial Ones and Reconnaissance Scouts are Reactions in the Engage
window. Khalimpeh-jiak's and The Bestial Ones' targets are any enemy Personality or Follower in play (no location is printed); Reconnaissance Scouts reacts only when its Personality is at the
battlefield being engaged ("this battlefield").

**Reconnaissance Scouts.** "You have Reconnaissance at this battlefield" is Daidoji Yuki's grant for either side. "If you are the Attacker, you have the first opportunity" is a claim made only by the
Attacker (the Defender has it by default, and a Defender's own claim still overrides). "You may target and give an enemy ... -3F" is read as the pair of actions sharing one use (`wl-scouts-engage`
without the penalty, `wl-scouts-engage-penalty` with its target), the siege-volley precedent for an optional part; Reconnaissance and the claim are the same in both.

**Maddened Horde.** "Any Fate deck" is the choice between two actions sharing one use (your own deck, the other player's). The discarded card's Focus Value is the Melee Attack's strength; with an
empty deck nothing is discarded and the Attack fails (supplement rule 6: an effect that needs missing information fails with everything that depends on it). The attack is performed by the Follower's
Personality like any other Follower's Attack.

**Shinobi Vassal.** "You may Equip this Follower as a Battle action" is Fading Shadows' Equip (a performing Ninja at the battlefield is the wielder; all costs paid). The second action destroys the
target and then the Follower; the Follower's own destruction is a plain effect that happens whether or not the first half did (supplement rule 6), not a cost.

**Sycophants.** "His Political abilities that may only be used once per turn" is every action of the Personality that carries Political (its own keyword tag or the card's) and is limited to one use
a turn: each gets one more use this turn (the `ExtraUsesPerTurn` channel, as Mempo of the Amethyst Champion). The mark is per Personality and per turn; Sycophants itself is once a turn.

**The Bestial Ones.** "Lose 1 Honor" is its `EntryHonorLoss`; "discard a card" is a forced discard of the controller's own hand queued as it enters play and asked as the action that attached it hands
priority on (the same queue Hida Osote's owed discards use). With an empty hand nothing is discarded.

**Kage's Teachings.** "He chooses and bows one of his unbowed Personalities" is a decider choice (the chosen player, which may be the acting player): bowed unless negated (Stalwart). Open is used
on the other player's turn only ("if it is not your turn" is a legality requirement). The Kolat grant is `AttachGrantsKeyword`.

**Koan's Staff.** "Each other Koan card in this unit" counts the wielder and every attachment of his unit with the Koan keyword (printed or granted) except the Staff.

**Renyu's Wrath.** The destroy is Brawl's (a Region at any location, or a Terrain). "This Personality is Siege or Earth" is the wielder's keyword, not the Item's own Siege.

**Sturdy Armor.** "Give this Personality +1F" is a +1F modifier until the turn ends (R-001), three uses a turn.

**Translated Story of Shaharazad.** "+1F token" is a Force token on the Item (type "Force"); it conveys to the wielder like any Item's Force modifier.

**Parangu.** Its only text is Kharmic's parenthetical reminder; it carries no ability of its own (Rice Farmer's and Fallen's shape); the universal Kharmic action is its rule.

**Events (the supplement).** "Limited:" is ignored on every Event of Gates of Chaos, Coils of Madness and Aftermath, so each resolves on reveal.
* *A Fever in the Blood*: stays in play until its owner's next turn begins (Family Maneuvers' duration, the supplement's "Most Recent Printing" wording). "Personalities and Followers" are those of
  both players; "while attacking" is a unit assigned to or moved to a battlefield in the turn of its controller (the Attacker). Several Fevers stack.
* *Changing the Game*: "your discard pile" is the Fate discard pile (Blood of the Preserver is a Fate card); an ordered search (R-011) of hand, then Fate discard pile, then Fate deck, the card shown,
  removed from the game, 2 Honor gained only if a card was found.
* *Purge of Fudoism*: "starting with you, each player targets and destroys" is sequential: the owner chooses and destroys, then the other player chooses among what is left. A target is a
  Personality, Follower, Item, Spell or Holding of either player in play with the printed Fallen, Fudo or Shadowlands keyword (granted keywords do not count: "printed"); nothing to target is "if possible".
* *Riot in the Second City*: the printed text names no owner, so "an unbowed Samurai" is any unbowed Samurai in play (own or other player's); owner first, then the other player.
* *The Second Battle of Beiden Pass*: "this Province" is the province the Event was revealed in. It permanently holds no Dynasty cards (Illusory Defense's flag) and has +10 Province Strength added to
  its strength (not to its printed strength); the Event stays in play as the reminder of an effect that lasts until the game ends.

**Blood of Shahai.** The Chi comparison uses the target's current Chi against the Chi printed on his card. "Discard this Celestial" in the second action is the first EFFECT (it is after the colon), so a
negated action does not discard it; each Personality at the current battlefield of either player gets the Blood token (type "Blood", Chi -1), and one at 0 Chi is destroyed.

**Hachiman's Prowess.** The grant is a record read live by `Keyword` (Elite) and `MayAssignBowed` while the Celestial is in play and its Personality is in play. The Battle action discards the Celestial as a cost
(it is before the colon) and straightens the unit of any of your Personalities at the battlefield.

**The Ways of Sensei Wu.** "Battle Strategy" is a Strategy with a Battle or Battle/Open action (`IsBattleStrategy`), Unique or not; the Strategy is chosen as the action is announced; "Discard this Celestial"
is the second effect.
