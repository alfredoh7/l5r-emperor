# Issue #699: Benediction (strategy-wave-120)

Card: Benediction (oracle-l5r-10930, Aftermath, Strategy, Focus 1). Scan text: "Open: Destroy your target Holding. Give a Province +3 strength." No printed cost, no keyword line.

## Mendon's ruling (confirmed)

- The Province may be any province: either player's, face-up or face-down. It is chosen with the province selection the client already has (the hover highlight, a face-up province's card named, a face-down one marked).
- Destroying the Holding is a separate effect and happens first. If the destruction is negated (or fails), the province strength bonus is not negated.

## Readings built in

- "Your target Holding": a Holding you control (`own-holding`), in play. No Holding of yours in play means the action is listed but disabled.
- "+3 strength": the ordinary duration, until the end of the turn (R-001; the scan prints no other duration). It uses `GiveProvinceStrengthThisTurn`, the same timed province strength Ring of Earth gives.
- Both targets are chosen up front with the command. The engine destroys the Holding through `DestroyUnits` (so every destruction negation, watch, shield and "will not be destroyed" applies to it), then gives the province strength unconditionally. A Holding already gone at resolution does not stop the strength.
- Nothing hidden is exposed: a face-down province is named only by owner and index.

## New shared mechanism: province slot kind

Slots (TargetSlots.cs) gain a third kind, `TargetSlotKinds.Province` (slot category `"province"`):

- `SlotSelection` gains optional `ProvinceOwner` and `Province` (the existing three fields are unchanged and positional-compatible).
- `TargetSlotOption` gains optional `ProvinceChoices` (`ProvinceSlotChoice(Owner, Index)[]`), every undestroyed province of both players, offered for the slot.
- `ValidateSlots` requires a real, undestroyed province of either player for a province slot, and refuses cards or a player in it (and a province in a card slot).
- Other slot readers use `CardIds`/`PlayerId`, so a province slot is invisible to them (targeted-by-action windows, performer checks, created-card readers).
