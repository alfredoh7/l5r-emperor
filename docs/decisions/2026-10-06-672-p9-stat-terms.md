# P9: stat-term registry, contribution rules, army totals (#672)

Status: built as an engineering primitive with eight demonstration cards; the rules points under "Readings for Mendon to confirm or correct" are readings, not rulings.

Specification: section P9 of [the primitive prompts](2026-10-05-remaining-unwired-primitives-prompts.md), narrowed by the coordinator for this pass.

## What was built

- **`src/L5R.Core/StatTerms.cs`**: a card's always-on stat trait is a record keyed by the card's Effect, not a branch in a Force function.
  - `StatTerm(Id, Scope, Stat, Amount, AnyOf, PerCount)`. `Scope` is `Self` (the card's own Force or Chi) or `Army` (the total Force of the army the card is in). `AnyOf` is a list of conditions, any of which is enough; no list means always. `PerCount` multiplies the amount by a live count.
  - `ContributionRule(Id, Multiplier, AnyOf)`: the Personality's contribution to its army's total is multiplied while a condition holds.
  - Conditions that exist: `Attacking`, `OpposingLowerForce`, `ControlsPersonalityWithKeyword(keyword)`, `DeadPersonalityInDiscard`, `EnemyLeaderControlsKeyword(keyword)`, `EnemyLeaderHasFavor`, `OtherPlayerIsClan(clan)`.
  - Counters that exist: `AttachedWithKeyword(keyword)`, `OpposingTokens(type)`.
  - Terms are evaluated live each time the stat is read. Nothing is stored on a card or in `MatchState`, so there is no new saved state and nothing to sweep.
  - A term that is already being evaluated counts as 0 inside its own evaluation. That stops two cards whose terms read each other's Force from looping, and it is what gives `OpposingLowerForce` the card's Force without the bonus.
- **New hook channels** (declared in `StatTerms.cs`, sealed in `EngineHookStartup.Build`): `EngineHooks.FollowerForce`, `EngineHooks.PersonalityChi`, `EngineHooks.ArmyForce` (all additive) and `EngineHooks.StatTerms` (a catalogue channel, like `Looks` and `Searches`, so a later wave adds its sets from its own file). Personality Force terms use the existing `EngineHooks.PersonalityForce` channel.
- **`src/L5R.Core/StatTermCards.cs`**: the eight sets, Yoritomo Toganin's Invest and Matsu Sango's "Will not attach Items".

## Chi

A Personality at 0 Chi is destroyed, and the engine enforces that where a Chi change is applied (`ApplyModifier`, the paths that remove an attachment). A live term is never applied, so the catalogue refuses any Chi term whose amount is not positive (`StatTermCatalog.ValidateSets`): a static Chi penalty has to be built another way, and none was needed.

Toku Saiga's "+1C for each of his Weapons" is positive, but it shrinks when a Weapon leaves. Removing an attachment already re-reads the host's Chi for the sake of an Item's printed Chi, and Saiga's term is read by the same function, so he is destroyed there. A test destroys the Weapon that holds him at 1 Chi with a real action and checks that he dies.

Not covered: a Weapon that stops being a Weapon while it stays attached (its keyword removed). No wired card does that to an Item today. If one ever does, it has to re-read the host's Chi as the detach paths do.

## Left out of this pass

- Stat caps (`StatCap`): no demonstration card needed one once Asako Kaitoko - exp was dropped from the list.
- Personal Honor terms and the `CardPersonalHonor` hook: no demonstration card has one.
- The unit, controller, all and opposing scopes; the other conditions and counters the prompt lists. They are added with the first card that uses them.
- `AppliedModifier` durations (`Battle`, `WhileCondition`) and `ComputedBonus` on `AbilityDefinition`: excluded by the coordinator.
- Warded Premises' before-resolution negation and The Sky's Barrier's `OnlyWhileBowed` contribution rule.
- `PersonalityBattleContribution` and `FollowerBattleContribution` were not edited. The contribution rule is applied in `ArmyForces`, so a unit's Force as shown on the card or compared by a targeting rule is unchanged and only the army total doubles.

## Readings for Mendon to confirm or correct

1. **"During battle resolution" (Manami, Sango).** Both are applied wherever the engine totals the two armies (`ArmyForces`). That is the resolution itself, and also the two places that ask "what would the totals be": Raido no Oni's "may not perform Battle actions while your current army has higher total Force" and Fields of Victory's comparison just before resolution. So during the action segment Raido no Oni already sees Manami's +2F and Sango's doubled Force. If these should count at the resolution only, `ArmyForces` needs to be told which caller is asking.
2. **Manami bowed.** "Manami's army has +2F" is read as a trait of the army she is in, so it applies while she is at the battlefield whether or not she is bowed, attacking or defending. At home she is in no army.
3. **Sango bowed, and his Followers.** "He contributes his Force twice" doubles whatever he would contribute: nothing while he is bowed. His Followers and Items are not doubled separately; an Item's Force is part of his own Force, so it is doubled with it (he will not attach Items himself, but a token or an effect could still give him Force).
4. **Sango, "if Sango is attacking".** He is attacking while he is at a battlefield on the Attacker's side.
5. **Tochiko, "opposing ... with lower Force".** Opposing cards are the enemy Personalities at her battlefield and the Followers attached to them (Glossary: cards at the same battlefield on different sides). Their Force is compared with Tochiko's Force without this bonus, so she does not keep the bonus merely because the bonus itself put her ahead. Bowed cards on either side still count. It does not need to be the current battlefield.
6. **Tochiko against Tochiko.** Each compares against the other's Force without that other's own bonus. Two of them at 5F give neither a bonus.
7. **Toku Saiga, "his Weapons".** Attached cards with the Weapon keyword. A Weapon token (a token that counts as an attachment) is not counted, following how the engine already counts Weapons elsewhere.
8. **Hiruma Ogata, "each opposing Madness token".** Madness tokens on opposing cards: the enemy Personalities at his battlefield and everything attached to them. Tokens on his own side's cards, and on enemy cards at home or at another battlefield, do not count. At home he has no bonus.
9. **Moto Daiken.** "Ancestor Personalities you control" are Personalities in play under her controller with the Ancestor keyword, printed or granted. "Dead Personalities in your discard pile" are her controller's Personalities that are dead (not merely discarded). Either condition gives +1F; both together still give +1F.
10. **Moto Ogaru, "the enemy leader".** The Glossary defines the enemy as the opposing player in a battle, so Ogaru has the bonus only while he is at a battlefield; the enemy leader is then the other player. At home he has none, even if the opponent holds the Favor. "An Imperial card" is any card that player has in play with the Imperial keyword.
11. **Yoritomo Toganin, "if any other player is Crane Clan".** Read from the players' Stronghold Clan, as Akodo Hotaka's "if any other player is Dragon Clan" already is. His own controller being Crane does not count.
12. **Yoritomo Toganin's Invest.** Modelled like Clay Kshatriya's: offered once in the entering-play window for 5 Gold, three +1F tokens that stay. It is a trait, so Cherry Blossom Festival does not bar it (ruling R4); it answers `TraitReactions.Is` through `StatTermCards.IsTrait` and the pinned id list was not changed.
13. **Traits work in play.** A card's own terms apply only while it is in play: Toganin in a province shows his printed 2F.
