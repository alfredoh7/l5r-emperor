## Readings built in (not confirmed rulings)

- Printed "If ..." before an effect (Ichigiku: Family Honor; Ming-Gwok's Ranged 3: a dead Personality in any discard pile) is a condition on taking the action (Haruki's Deer's precedent); the action is not offered when it fails. The supplement's "failing effects do not stop the rest" would allow taking it and doing nothing.
- Takaki's "Engage" is a Reaction after engaging (supplement, major change 3), any card in play as target, no location requirement.
- Nikaru: "printed Force" is the definition's Force, used in every duel he is in, whoever issued it, ignoring modifiers.
- Hideo: two +1F tokens whenever he is the winner; a tie has no winner.
- Chukage: "it" is a Ring the acting player controls in play; the engine lists straighten/bow x (no removal | each Ring). The target is removed with its unit rules (a Personality via RemoveUnitFromGame; an attachment alone, its host destroyed if left with 0 Chi). An "all" negation on the target skips the removal.
- Tomiko / Tono / Ming-Gwok: "Moto Ming-Gwok", "Moshi Tomiko", "Shosuro Kameyoi" are matched by title (any version); Tomiko's bow is an effect (no colon), so it is not a cost. Ming-Gwok's and Tono's targets are either player's.
- Iaimiko: the bow is the cost ("to give"); three entries share one use count of 3; granted keywords last until end of turn (R-001).
- Daisuke: "then discard a card" is the search flow's discard step, and (Mendon's Terrain-search ruling, #368) the card just found cannot be the one discarded; with no other card nothing is discarded. Found cards are those shown from the look.
- Tselu: same "to hand, then discard" effect.
- Minori: uses Scavenging Party's refill (only provinces that hold a card are offered); "(not dead)" excludes the dead pile; "Discard a Fate card" is the acting player's choice from hand, after the placement (one card: no question; none: nothing).
- Kameyoi: "a target player" is either player, himself included; "not named Yasuki Tono" by title, either version.
- Dark Naga / Arakan: the Gold icon means pay the Equip cost ("all costs", Dark Naga 4 less, never below 0); Arakan's wielder is a performer, Arakan or a Zansho, who must be at the battlefield for the Battle timing; the Battle and Limited timings share one use; the keywords are permanent; Honor loss is an effect after the attach.
- Adachi: "opposing" means in a unit at the same battlefield as Adachi under the other player's control; the action's source card must be in play in that unit (a Strategy or Holding has no unit); the leader's Madness tokens are compared to his printed Personal Honor; the leader's controller owes the discard (queued as every owed discard; Pillowbook honoured).
- Crane Clan entries (Hideo, Adachi) apply their Honor loss after the Proclaim window, the existing rule.
