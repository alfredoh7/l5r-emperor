# Phase 0 report: the instrument, A/A calibration, power note, first hypotheses (issue #963)

Champion = `TrainingOpponent` on `main` at the commit this branch started from. Everything below is bot against bot; no agent played a game and no behaviour changed.
Raw games (JSON) are regenerated by the commands in `docs/bots/README.md` and are not committed; the markdown reports are in this folder.

## 1. Instrument

`tests/L5R.AI.Tests/BotTournament.cs` (opt-in `bot-tournament` case), protocol in `docs/bots/README.md`, loss analysis in `tools/bot_loss_report.py`. Pool = 10 preloaded decks + Lion and
Crane presets. **Held out (fixed by SHA-256 of the name): Allan Lion MCA, Mantis Manipulation, Unicorn Maidens**, with their own seed range (9,000,000+); development decks use 1,000,000+.
`same` pairing (one seed, only the bot differs) and `independent` pairing (fresh shuffle for the second game) are both implemented; `L5R_TOURN_PILOT` restricts a run to games where the
challenger pilots named decks; `L5R_TOURN_SEED_START` selects a fresh seed block.

## 2. A/A calibration (champion against an identical champion)

| Run | Games (decided) | "Challenger" win rate (95% CI) | Sign test p | Seat-0 win rate | Soundness findings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| dev, independent, seeds 0-5 | 864 (857) | 47.6% (44.3-51.0) | 0.17 | 50.4% | 7 stalls |
| dev, independent, seeds 6-11 | 864 (859) | 49.5% (46.1-52.8) | 0.78 | 47.5% | 5 stalls |
| holdout, independent, seeds 0-2 | 360 (360) | 51.4% (46.2-56.5) | 0.64 | 45.8% | 0 |
| dev, **same** pairing, 1 seed (smoke) | 144 (142) | 50.0% | 1.00 | 50.7% | 2 stalls |

- All A/A runs sit inside noise: no false positive in three independent runs. **`same` pairing is perfectly deterministic**: both games of every pair were identical (0 discordant pairs
  in 71), so a difference found with `same` pairing can only come from the bot.
- **Seat-0 advantage:** pooled over the three independent runs seat 0 won 1005 of 2076 decided games = **48.4%** (95% CI 46.3-50.5%), i.e. no detectable advantage; the point estimate favours
  seat 1 by 1.6 points. Treat seat as neutral but keep both seats in every pair; do not "correct" results by a seat term until a run of 3,000+ games shows one.
- Noise floor: with independent pairing and ~860 decided games the A/A scatters ±2-3 points around 50% (the sign test's 95% interval is ±3.3). A real effect smaller than about 3 points cannot
  be seen in one dev block.

## 3. Power note (independent games, exact binomial, two-sided alpha .05, 80% power)

| True win rate | Decided games | Seeds on the 9-deck dev pool (144 games per seed) | Wall clock at the measured 0.9-1.1 games/s on this 10-core Mac |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 60% | about 194 | 2 (288 games) | about 5 minutes |
| 55% | about 783 | 6 (864 games) | about 16 minutes |
| 52% | about 4,900 | 34 (4,896 games) | about 1.5 hours |

Throughput: 0.9 games/s with 9 worker threads (about 9 s per game per core, 14.9 turns per game on average). `same` pairing needs fewer games for a bot-only difference because deck, seat
and shuffle cancel, but only the independent figures are an honest sample-size guide. A deck-restricted hypothesis (`L5R_TOURN_PILOT`) halves the games per seed per deck pair, so plan
by decided games, not seeds.

## 4. What "good" means (and the current baseline)

Promotion needs: win rate beyond the pre-registered threshold **and** margin metrics not worse **and** game length not pathological **and** soundness findings at zero **and** no deck collapse.
**The champion does not pass the soundness bar today**: 12 stalls in 1,728 dev games (0.7%), **all in one pairing**, Crab Control against Dragon Destruction (12 of the 48 games of that
pairing, in either seat), always "No complete legal action was supplied for this decision" (`TrainingOpponent.Choose` found no candidate). Until that is fixed the practical bar is "no
stall outside that pairing and no more than the champion's in it"; fixing it is hypothesis 1. Refused-then-retried actions (about 10% of games, 309 and 251 refusals) are informational (an
engine-offered action the engine then refuses: rules lane).

## 5. What the champion's games show (A/A, 857 decided; see `champion-loss-report.md`)

- 88% military victories, 8% Honor victories, 3% Dishonor defeats; mean game 14.9 turns; no turn-limit games.
- Deck strength is very uneven when both seats use the champion: Crab Control 82%, Scorpion Lethargy 75% ... Scottish Lion 21%, Phoenix Talons 19%.
- Those two weak decks **declare 1.2 and 1.6 attacks per game against 3.3-5.1 for the others**. Winners declare 4.3 attacks per game, losers 2.2.
- In military games winners finish with 6.5 Personalities in play, losers 2.1; losers pass more (59 vs 49 actions), equip more (4.3 vs 3.5) and order Seppuku nine times as often (2.3 vs 0.3).

These are correlations from one A/A, not causes. Each is a hypothesis to be tested, which is what section 6 is.
