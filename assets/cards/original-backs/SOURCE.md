# Original L5R backs

Extracted without visual edits from the user-provided Sun and Moon.app/Contents/Resources/Data/resources.assets, Texture2D dynasty_back and fate_back (512 × 512 Unity textures). The original engine stored these textures square; runtime maps their full UVs onto the same portrait card proportions as the original game. These are the black and green five-ring designs in the user's screenshot, not generated replacements.
