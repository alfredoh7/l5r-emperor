# Product

## Register

product

## Users

An Emperor Edition L5R player on macOS playing private games with a friend on macOS or Windows, and a new player learning the same game. Desktop mouse and keyboard are the initial input methods.

## Product Purpose

A new standalone implementation of Emperor Edition that enforces legal play and performs all bookkeeping. The first product milestone is a fully automated Lion versus Crane match. The long-term objective is verified full Emperor content, deck building, solo opponents, and replay/spectating.

## Confirmed decisions

The user selected Emperor Edition, macOS and Windows, private online 1v1 through a small server, two complete decks first, Lion versus Crane, expansion toward the full Emperor arc, a stylized 3D war table, and suggested actions/payment plans followed by player confirmation. The user approved the phased plan and asked for implementation. The old attached brief is advisory. Sun and Moon is a reference and asset source, never the project architecture.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, composed, legible. A physical Rokugani war table: wood, ink, paper, and clan identity. The experience should feel like a coherent game rather than a collection of administrative panels.

## Anti-references

Manual virtual-tabletop bookkeeping; modern dashboard styling; mismatched mod overlays; ornament that hides card information; camera motion that interrupts decisions; rasterized UI labels.

## Design Principles

- Preserve Emperor rules and player strategic choices.
- Explain consequences before confirmation and causes after resolution.
- Keep turn, priority, cost, and legal targets apparent.
- Use animation to explain state transitions, with a reduced-motion alternative.
- Measure real gameplay readability as well as attractive screenshots.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable text and scalable UI, color-independent states, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, and audio controls. Validate 720p through 1440p and ultrawide; target 1080p at 60 fps on documented reference hardware.
